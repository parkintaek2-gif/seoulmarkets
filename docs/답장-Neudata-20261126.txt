A short overview of what we would list.

SeoulMarkets (https://seoulmarkets.com) publishes Korean financial-market data in English, built only from Korean public sources (FSS DART filings, the Korea Public Data Portal, the Korea Customs Service).

Datasets available today
- Valuation multiples (PER, PBR, ROE, debt-to-equity) for about 2,700 listed Korean companies, with the price date and fiscal year shipped as columns
- Brokerage target prices and ratings: 21 brokers, 678 companies, from June 2026 (facts only, no report text)
- Convertible bond, bond-with-warrant and exchangeable bond filings: about 5,000 records
- Substantial-shareholding (5%+) and officer ownership filings: about 21,800 filings
- Workforce filings: headcount, tenure and pay by gender for about 2,900 listed companies
- Daily levels for 168 KRX indices, with English names
- Monthly Korean exports and imports, national totals and by partner country

How it is delivered
- HTTP API (JSON): https://seoulmarkets.com/api
- Downloadable files and samples: https://seoulmarkets.com/data
- MCP server for AI assistants: https://seoulmarkets.com/mcp
- Live coverage, row counts and known gaps: https://seoulmarkets.com/v1/meta

What we want buyers to know up front
- History is short for some datasets. The broker target-price series starts in June 2026.
- Values we could not measure are returned as null with the reason in a separate column. We never fill them with zero.
- Pricing is public: https://seoulmarkets.com/pricing
