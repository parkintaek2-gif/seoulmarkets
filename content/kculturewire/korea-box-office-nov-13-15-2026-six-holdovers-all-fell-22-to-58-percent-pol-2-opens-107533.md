South Korea's cinema admissions are counted centrally by the Korean Film Council (KOFIC) in its KOBIS ticketing network, and a "weekend" there means Friday to Sunday. For **13 to 15 November 2026**, KOBIS ranks the animated ***Chiikawa the Movie: Secret of the Mermaid Island*** first with **116,176** admissions. Its Korean release date is 30 September, and it has now passed 2.9 million cumulative admissions (2,949,096). Second is the new film ***Pol 2: Deadpoint*** (Korean title romanised), released on Wednesday 11 November, with **107,533** admissions.

## What stands out

**Every holdover fell.** The six films that were already showing the weekend before all lost admissions, and the range is wide: **22.1%** for Chiikawa at the mild end, **58.5%** for Heart of Beast at the steep end. Final Interview lost 33.5%, Madoka Magica 41.3%, The Cat with a Hat 49.7% and Detective Conan 54.2%. The decline is not spread evenly. Only Chiikawa lost less than a third.

**The top three held 61.5% of the top-10 admissions.** The three leaders drew 293,215 of the 476,433 admissions in the ten rows. The top two are only 8,643 apart. The tenth film, at 14,714, is about one eighth of the leader's total.

**The weekend is not the whole week.** KOBIS's three views let each film's Friday-to-Sunday count be set against its Monday-to-Thursday count:

| Heart of Beast | 35,195 | 37,591 | 51.6% |

On Monday to Thursday, **Final Interview was first** with 46,989, ahead of Chiikawa at 44,082 and Heart of Beast at 35,195. Over the weekend it dropped to third. Heart of Beast shows the flattest pattern: just under half of its week came before Friday. Pol 2, which opened midweek, put 77.9% of its first 138,114 admissions into the weekend.

## Two older films in the top 10

Two titles in the table opened years ago. *La La Land* (KOBIS release date 7 December 2016) took 36,435 admissions on 633 screens, and *Dune* (20 October 2021) took 17,874 on only 26 screens. KOBIS lists *La La Land* with 3,861,037 cumulative admissions. The tables used here do not say why the two titles are back on screens, and this desk does not guess.

## How this was checked

The three KOBIS tables add up: the weekday and weekend counts sum to the weekly count for all eight films present in all three. The prior weekend, rebuilt from KOBIS's own change figures, matches the 9 November press report exactly for three films and within ten admissions for the other three. On Saturday 14 November Money Today reported 57,192 for Chiikawa and 48,434 for Pol 2, with 100,101 cumulative for Pol 2, which lines up with KOBIS's 140,038 cumulative at the end of Sunday.
