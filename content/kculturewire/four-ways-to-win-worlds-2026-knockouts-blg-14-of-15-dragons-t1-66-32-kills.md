Three Worlds 2026 knockout series can be put side by side on the same two counts, kills and dragons. They look alike on one and very different on the other.

## The winner's share

| Series | Result | Kills | Winner's kill share | Dragons | Winner's dragon share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BLG v Dplus KIA | 3-0 | 62-29 | 68.1% | 14-1 | 93.3% |
| Gen.G v IG | 3-0 | 72-36 | 66.7% | 10-6 | 62.5% |
| T1 v Karmine Corp | 3-1 | 66-32 | 67.3% | 12-10 | 54.5% |

The winner's kill share sits in a narrow band, from 66.7% to 68.1%. Put another way, each winner took about two of every three kills in its series.

Dragons are another matter. BLG took 14 of the 15 dragons against Dplus KIA, whose only dragon came in the third set. Gen.G took 10 of 16 against IG. T1 took 12 of 22 against Karmine Corp, only two more than its opponent, even though it made twice as many kills.

## The one set the winner lost on kills

The three series span ten sets: three, three and four. In nine of them the set winner also made more kills. The exception is Gen.G's third set against IG, where IG led the kills 18-16. Gen.G still won it, with towers 9-5 and one baron, even though IG had four dragons to Gen.G's two.

Karmine Corp's win over T1 in the third set, on kills of 8-6, is not an exception: the winner led there too.

## What this does not tell us

This is a count of three series. It does not rank the teams, and two other knockout series, Gen.G v LYON and BLG v T1, are left out because their dragon totals could not be confirmed here.
