Korean series are some of Netflix's most exported shows. This month "Take Charge of My Heart" reached the Top 10 in 64 countries and "Doctor X: Mafia in White" in 48.

Korea's own Netflix chart tells a different story. In the week ending 18 October, half of its ten most-watched series appeared in no other country's Top 10 at all.

## A year of Korea's chart

Netflix publishes a weekly Top 10 for each of 93 countries. Over the past 52 weeks Korea's TV chart had 520 places. **195 of them, 37.5%, were held by a title that charted in no other country that week.**

Among the 93 countries, only Japan keeps more of its chart to itself.

| Country | Share of TV Top 10 places that charted nowhere else |

| **South Korea** | **37.5%** |
| Thailand | 25.6% |

In most countries almost every show in the Top 10 is also in someone else's. In Korea more than a third are not.

## What only Korea watches

The title that spent the most weeks in Korea's chart and nowhere else is "I am Solo," a long-running dating show: 14 weeks. Its spin-off "I'm SOLO, Love goes on" had 8, and "Match to Marry: With Parents" 9. Scripted series appear too, among them "The Dream Life of Mr. Kim" (8 weeks) and "Taxi Driver" (7).

## What this does and does not say

The count is about what each country watched, not where a show was made. Netflix's file does not say where a title comes from, so a Korea-only entry is simply one that no other country put in its Top 10 that week.

Read that way, the pattern is clear. The Korean shows that travel are a small, visible part of what Korea itself watches. Most countries' Netflix charts are shared with the rest of the world. Japan's and Korea's are, to a large extent, their own.
