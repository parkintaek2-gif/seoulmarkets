Six recent variety episodes show how the same word, *rating*, covers several different numbers. For each, we listed every figure in the reports we used.

## The figures

| Show | Household, whole episode | 2049 or 2054 | Peak minute |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Night 2 Days Season 4 (8 Nov) | 7.7% nationwide | 1.7% (2049, Seoul area) | 11.1% |
| I Live Alone, ep. 672 (6 Nov) | 5.1% Seoul area; 4.7% nationwide (iZE) | 2.7% (2054) | 6.0% |
| Bisojin, ep. 9 (30 Oct) | 3.8% Seoul area | 1.5% (2049) | 4.9% |
| Jeonchaksi, ep. 1 (8 Nov) | 3.4% nationwide (one report) | 2.0% (2054) | 4.9% |
| Omniscient Interfering View, ep. 422 | not reported | 2.1% (2054) | 4.6% |
| Salimnam, ep. 481 (7 Nov) | not reported | not reported | 4.9% |

*All figures are Nielsen Korea as reported by the sources listed. Peak figures are for the single best minute.*

## What the table shows

All **six** episodes have a peak-minute figure. Only **four** have a whole-episode household figure, and **five** have a 2049 or 2054 figure. Salimnam has only its peak.

Four of these numbers carry labels that look alike and are not: a national household rating, a Seoul-area household rating, a 2049 rating and a 2054 rating each measure a different group. I Live Alone has two household numbers for one episode, 5.1% for the Seoul area and 4.7% for the whole country.

Only the 2054 figure is reported for three shows: I Live Alone at 2.7%, Omniscient Interfering View at 2.1% and Jeonchaksi at 2.0%. Even those three were not broadcast on the same day, so this is a comparison of reports, not of one night.

## Why we do not rank them

Placing 7.7% next to 2.1% would suggest that one show beat the other. The first is a national household figure for a whole episode, and the second is a 2054 figure. They are different measures, so we do not rank.
