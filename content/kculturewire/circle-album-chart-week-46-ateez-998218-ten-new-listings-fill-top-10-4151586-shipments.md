The Circle Album Chart for 8-14 November 2026 is a very different list from the week before. All ten of the top ten listings are new. Together they shipped 3,255,850 copies, 78.4% of the 4,151,586 shipped across the whole 100-row chart. A week earlier the top ten held 85.6% of a total of 2,775,302, so the total rose by 1,376,284, or 49.6%, while the top ten's share fell by 7.2 points.

The figures in this piece come from the chart's own table for week 46 and, for the comparison, the table for week 45. They are shipments minus returns, not sales to listeners.

## The top ten

ATEEZ's GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6 is No. 1 with 998,218 shipped. NCT DREAM's DREAM AFTER DREAM is No. 2 with 582,069. The third listing is a second version of the ATEEZ release, the POCA edition, with 348,808. LNGSHOT's S.W.I.S.H. is No. 4 with 305,062.

Ranks 5 to 10 are, in order:

- No. 5: a second DREAM AFTER DREAM version (SMC) with 206,807
- No. 6: JENNIE's Fallen Angel with 203,962
- No. 7: a third DREAM AFTER DREAM version (Nemo) with 182,707
- No. 8: Across Dimensions by the act listed as 이세계 아이돌 with 157,011
- No. 9: KEYVITUP's 2ND EP [NA-GARI], a kiwee album version, with 150,516
- No. 10: the Nemo version of IVE's Looks Can Kill with 120,690

The No. 1 listing alone is 24.0% of everything shipped that week. The gap from No. 1 to No. 2 is 416,149 copies. The gap from No. 2 to No. 3 is 233,261.

## Three acts, 65.8%

Grouping the 100 rows by act gives 49 acts. ATEEZ has four listings with 1,383,223 shipments in total, which is 33.3% of the chart. NCT DREAM has three listings and 971,583. LNGSHOT has two and 377,062. Those three acts together hold 2,731,868 shipments, 65.8% of the total, from nine of the 100 rows.

The act that placed the most listings is BTS, which the chart prints under its Korean name 방탄소년단. It has 14 listings, at ranks 42 to 95, and all 14 together shipped 22,196. That is 0.5% of the week's total from 14% of its rows.

## New listings carry the week

Of the 100 listings, 32 are marked new. Together they shipped 3,762,023, which is 90.6% of the week's total. Ten of the 32 are in the top ten, which means every other new listing sits at No. 11 or lower. The week before, 37 of 100 were new.

Stray Kids is the highest-placed returning act. THIS & THAT is at No. 12 with 81,913 shipped, up from No. 64 the week before. Stray Kids has four listings in the 100 and together they shipped 108,473. RESCENE's PULSE is at No. 15 with 56,000, and TWS's TO US, last week's No. 1, is at No. 18 with 40,996.

## How thin the lower half is

The listing at No. 50, an IVE album, shipped 2,229 copies. A week earlier No. 50 shipped 2,264, so the middle of the chart moved by 35 copies in a week in which the top moved by hundreds of thousands. The No. 100 listing shipped 784, against 898 a week earlier. The 90 listings from No. 11 to No. 100 together shipped 895,736. A week earlier the same 90 ranks shipped 398,309, so the lower ranks also rose, by 497,427. The chart does not say which listings account for that rise, and we did not work it out.

## What this does not say

The chart counts what was shipped to stores in a week, so a large first week is partly a shipping number, and the same release can look smaller or larger on another chart. Hanteo's weekly ranking, which counts sales on its own rules, puts the same ATEEZ album first with 990,321 copies. The two figures are close but they are not the same measure, and we did not try to reconcile them.

One week is one week. Which of these listings keeps shipping in week 47 will show whether the 50% rise was a release-week bump or something that lasts. We will read the next table the same way and say so in the next piece.
