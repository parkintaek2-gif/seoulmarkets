BTS won Best K-Pop at the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards on 27 September, for "SWIM." Katseye — nominated
in the same category for "PINKY UP," alongside three other nominations — lost both Best K-Pop and
Best Choreography that night. By one measure, Katseye still had the bigger day.

## The winner's readership didn't move

BTS's English Wikipedia article was read between 4,736 and 5,578 times a day, every day, from 20
through 26 September. On 27 September — award night — it was read 5,152 times: a number that falls
inside the same range as any ordinary day that week. It didn't rank among the 1,000 most-read English
Wikipedia articles that day.

## The loser's readership went to world rank 21

Katseye's article ran 15,000–20,000 reads a day over the same week — already several times BTS's
range. On 27 September it jumped to 106,190 reads, a roughly 6-times spike over its own prior-week
average, and world rank 21 of every article on English Wikipedia that day, out of roughly six million.

That is a 20-to-1 gap in same-day reads between the group that lost the category and the group that
won it.

Forbes had flagged Katseye's Best K-Pop nomination as an unusual one before the ceremony: the group
sings entirely in English and isn't a Korean-industry act the way its fellow nominees are. Whatever
readers were looking up on 27 September, it wasn't the outcome — Katseye lost.
