BLACKPINK's Lisa won Best Pop at the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards, held 27 September at the
Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, for her solo single "Dream," featuring Japanese actor Kentaro
Sakaguchi. It was her first VMA win from four nominations this year.

## Two Asian wins in seventeen years

Best Pop has been awarded every year since 2010. Across all 17 ceremonies, the winner has been a
US- or UK-based act in every year but two.

BTS won in 2020, as a seven-member group. Lisa's win, six years later, is the first by a solo
artist from an Asian act — and only the second Asian win of any kind in the category's history.

## A crowded field, one crossover

This year's Best K-Pop category — a separate, genre-specific award — carried six K-pop-affiliated
nominees: Lisa, Blackpink, BTS, Cortis, Katseye, and Le Sserafim featuring J-Hope. Lisa was the
only solo act in that group of six. Best Pop sits outside the K-Pop category entirely, competing
against the full field of pop releases regardless of origin — and the single win that crossed over
from that crowded K-pop field into the open category went to her.

Per the Naver/News1 account of the ceremony, Lisa accepted the award in English and Thai, thanking
her fan base — known as "Lilies" — by name. "Dream" was originally released in August 2025.
