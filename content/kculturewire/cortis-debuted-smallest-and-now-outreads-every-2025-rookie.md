Five Korean groups debuted in 2025, one each from BigHit, SM, JYP, Starship and F&F. If you had
ranked them by English Wikipedia reads in their first three months, you would have had the order
almost exactly backwards.

## The opening

| Group | Label | Debut | Median daily reads, first 90 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hearts2Hearts | SM | 24 Feb 2025 | 840 |
| AHOF | F&F | 1 Jul 2025 | 700 |
| KickFlip | JYP | 20 Jan 2025 | 280 |
| Cortis | BigHit | 18 Aug 2025 | 231 |
| Idid | Starship | 15 Sep 2025 | 196 |

Hearts2Hearts opened more than four times above Cortis. AHOF opened three times above it.

## A year later

| Group | Median daily reads, last 30 days | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Cortis | 2,196 | 9.51× |
| Hearts2Hearts | 912 | 1.09× |
| KickFlip | 189 | 0.68× |
| AHOF | 140 | 0.20× |
| Idid | 81 | 0.41× |

Fourth place became first. Cortis now reads 15.7 times AHOF and 27.1 times Idid — two groups that
both started above it. AHOF fell to a fifth of its opening level; Idid to two fifths.

Only one group held roughly its opening ground: Hearts2Hearts, at 1.09 times.

## The day each one peaked says the same thing

| Group | Best single day | Days after debut |
|---|---|---|
| Hearts2Hearts | 8,106 | 1 |
| Cortis | 6,788 | 104 |
| AHOF | 4,304 | 184 |
| Idid | 1,405 | 76 |
| KickFlip | 1,101 | 314 |

Hearts2Hearts had its biggest day the day after debuting and never beat it. Cortis had to wait
three and a half months; KickFlip, ten. A debut-day number is a measure of how many people were
told something was happening, not of how many came back.

## What this does not tell you

A page view is a page opened. It is not a stream, a ticket or a vote, and English Wikipedia counts
English-language readers only — a group that is read heavily in Korean may barely move here.

We also did not check what happened to any of these groups. A comeback, a drama cast list, a
televised appearance and a controversy all look identical in a pageview series, and we did not
match these curves against a news timeline. What we can say is narrow and, we think, useful: among
these five, the first ninety days did not predict the twelfth month.

*Wikimedia pageviews, en.wikipedia, all-access, user traffic only (bots excluded), daily, from each
group's debut to 4 September 2026; measured 5 September 2026. Medians, not means — one viral day
would otherwise carry a whole quarter. Two groups in the same dataset debuted before our window and
are left out rather than estimated.*
