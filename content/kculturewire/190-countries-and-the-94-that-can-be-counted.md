A Korean dating show, *I Am Solo*, is heading to Netflix — **in over 190 countries**, according to the
announcement that turned up in our morning collection.

That number is normal for this kind of news, and there is no reason to doubt it. But it is worth knowing
what can and cannot be checked about it, because the figure a reader meets next will be much smaller and
that is not a contradiction.

## Netflix publishes charts for 94 countries

We counted this from the file itself. Netflix's public Top 10 data covers **94 countries** across 269
weeks, from 4 July 2021 to 23 August 2026. For every other country on earth, there is no published chart
at all.

So "available in 190+" and "charted in N" are measurements of different things by different parties:

| | |
|---|---|
| Available in | announced by the distributor; no public record to check it against |
| Charted in | published weekly by Netflix, for 94 countries only |

Nobody outside the company can produce the first number. Everybody can count the second.

## What the second number actually looks like

Of **974 Korean titles** that ever entered any country's weekly top ten in those five years:

| | | |
|---|---:|---:|
| Charted in exactly 1 country | 447 | 45.9% |
| 2 to 4 countries | 211 | 21.7% |
| 5 to 9 | 78 | 8.0% |
| 10 to 49 | 171 | 17.6% |
| 50 to 89 | 59 | 6.1% |
| 90 or more | 8 | 0.8% |

**The median Korean title charted in 2 countries.** Almost half charted in exactly one and nowhere else.

Only eight titles in five years reached 90 or more: *All of Us Are Dead* (93), *Squid Game* (93),
*The Great Flood* (93), *Carter* (92), *Hellbound* (92), *My Name* (91), *The Glory* (91), *Desire* (90).

**93 of 94** is the widest any Korean title has ever gone in the sense anyone can count.

## Why we are printing this next to an announcement

Not to catch anyone out. The two numbers measure different things and both can be true at once: a show
can be switched on in 190 countries and reach a top ten in three of them, and that is an ordinary result
rather than a failure — it is better than the median.

The reason to say it plainly is that a reader who sees 190 in a headline and 3 in a chart will assume
one of them is wrong. Neither is. One is a distribution decision; the other is what people watched. Only
the second one leaves a public trace, and even then only for 94 countries.

[Every title we have, country by country, with the same caveat attached, is here](/netflix-which-country).

## What we cannot say

We cannot say where *I Am Solo* will chart, because it has not aired yet. We cannot say where any title
is available, because Netflix publishes no catalogue by country. And we cannot say anything at all about
the countries outside the 94 — not that a title did badly there, not that it did well.

An empty cell is an answer too, and this one is empty for about half the world.
