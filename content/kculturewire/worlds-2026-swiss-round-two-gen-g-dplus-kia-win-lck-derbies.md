Round two of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship Swiss Stage put two Korean derbies on the schedule, and on 25 October (KST) both were settled. **Gen.G beat Hanwha Life Esports. Dplus Kia beat T1.** Gen.G and Dplus Kia move to 2-0, one win from the quarter-finals. Hanwha Life and T1 drop to 1-1.

China, winless on day one, won three times.

## Gen.G 23, Hanwha Life 7

The 07:00 match produced the widest kill margin of the day. Gen.G finished with **23 kills to Hanwha Life's 7**, 11 towers to 2, and all four dragons. Hanwha Life took none.

Every Gen.G player ended with at most two deaths. ADC Ruler led on Ezreal at 7/1/8, and top laner Kiin went 5/1/11 on Rumble. On the other side, Hanwha Life's support Delight died eight times and jungler Kanavi six.

These two teams met in the LCK final in September, which Gen.G won 3-1. Hanwha Life went on to win the MSI title this year; today it got no closer.

## Dplus Kia outlast T1

The 08:00 match was the longest of the day on the live feed's clock, and it was close until the end. T1 took four dragons, mountain, infernal and two cloud, for the **dragon soul**. Dplus Kia answered with the **elder dragon** and closed out the game. The final kill score was **10-8 to Dplus Kia**, with 9 towers to T1's 6.

Dplus Kia's ADC Smash finished **4/0/3 on Kalista** without dying once, with 507 creep score, the most of any player in round two. T1's Faker played Anivia and went 1/2/4.

T1 won the last three World Championships, from 2023 to 2025. To make the quarter-finals this year it now needs two wins from its next three games.

## China gets on the board

On day one all four LPL teams lost, a first for the competition. Round two drew two of them against each other: **Top Esports beat Anyone's Legend 18-9 in kills**, so one LPL team was certain to fall to 0-2. AL did. Mid laner Creme led TES at 8/2/3 on Locke.

**Invictus Gaming** beat Team Secret Whales 21-12, with veteran top laner TheShy at 7/3/7 on K'Sante.

In the last match of the day, **Bilibili Gaming** beat Team Liquid Alienware **17-2**, taking 11 towers to 2. Mid laner Knight finished 6/0/8 on Orianna, and Korean ADC Viper did not die. Three of the four LPL teams are now 1-1; only Anyone's Legend is 0-2.

## What it means

The Swiss Stage sends a team to the quarter-finals at three wins and eliminates it at three losses. From round three, matches are best-of-three except those between 1-1 teams.
