Disney+'s ***The Remarried Empress*** (Korean title *Jaehon Hwanghu*) was first among Disney+ TV shows worldwide on **20 November 2026**, FlixPatrol's table shows, and it was first in **45 countries**. The series was released on 3 November, so it took 17 days. The ranking was reported on 21 November by Tenasia, Singlist and Sports Donga.

## How the country count grew

The count rose in steps. WikiTree reported first place in **12 countries** on the day after release. A few days later its headline put the figure at **33 countries**. On 20 November it was **45**. The countries named in the reports include Japan and Taiwan in Asia, France, Poland, Spain and Hungary in Europe, and Brazil and Mexico in Latin America.

FlixPatrol collects daily top-ten lists from each streaming service and country. A first place here means first among Disney+ TV shows in that country on that day, not among all streaming titles.

## What is known about the series

The series has ten episodes. Eight had been released by 21 November. Episodes 7 and 8 came out on 18 November, and episodes 9 and 10 follow on **25 November**. The cast named in the reports is Shin Min-a, Joo Ji-hoon, Lee Jong-suk and Lee Se-young.

## What the Korean ranking says

In Korea, Good Data Corporation's Fundex for week 47 (9 to 15 November) put the drama second in buzz with **13.96%**. The week before it was **21.16%**, so it fell **7.20 points**. In the same table Lee Jong-suk was fifth among cast members at 3.46%, Shin Min-a sixth at 3.38% and Joo Ji-hoon ninth at 2.71%.

## What comes next

The final two episodes land on 25 November. FlixPatrol's daily table will show whether the country count holds, rises or falls after the ending.
