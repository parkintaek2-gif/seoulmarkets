SBS's Friday-Saturday drama ***Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*** has now aired ten episodes. Its national household rating, as measured by Nielsen Korea, started at **11.4%**, peaked at **19.5%** in episode 6, and stood at **17.7%** in episode 10.

## All ten episodes

| Episode | National rating | Change from the episode before |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11.4% | |
| 2 | 14.9% | +3.5 |
| 3 | 15.1% | +0.2 |
| 4 | 18.5% | +3.4 |
| 5 | 17.5% | -1.0 |
| 6 | 19.5% | +2.0 |
| 7 | 16.5% | -3.0 |
| 8 | 15.6% | -0.9 |
| 9 | 15.3% | -0.3 |
| 10 | 17.7% | +2.4 |

*Changes are in percentage points. Episodes 9 and 10 are each from a single report.*

## What the row shows

Across the nine episode-to-episode comparisons, the rating went up **five** times and down **four** times. The largest rise was from episode 3 to episode 4, at 3.4 points. The largest fall was from episode 6 to episode 7, at 3.0 points.

After the 19.5% peak, the rating fell in episodes 7, 8 and 9. Episode 9 was 4.2 points below the peak. Episode 10 was 2.4 points above episode 9 and still 1.8 points below the peak.

Over the whole run the lowest figure is the 11.4% of the first episode. No later episode came near that number: the next lowest is 14.9%.

SBS said in a 30 October press release that the 19.5% of episode 6 ranked **fifth** among SBS Friday-Saturday dramas by national rating.

## One episode in detail

Episode 8 is the one for which a second set of figures is on record. It drew 15.6% nationwide and 15.1% in the Seoul area, with a minute peak of 16.7%. In the 2049 age group the average was 5.4%. These are different measures from the national household figures in the table above, so they are not mixed into it.

This is a count of published ratings. It does not say why viewers came or left, and it does not say what later episodes will draw.
