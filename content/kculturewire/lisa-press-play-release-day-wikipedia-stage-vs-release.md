LISA's EP "PRESS PLAY" is out today, 23 October. It has six tracks, follows the September single "SaWaDiKa," and is her second solo project after the 2025 album "Alter Ego." BABYMONSTER, the YG Entertainment group, also releases new music today.

Coverage tends to treat release day as the peak of an artist's attention. English Wikipedia reading for LISA says otherwise, and says it consistently.

## The last album: the release was not the biggest day of its own week

"Alter Ego" came out on 28 February 2025. That day LISA's English Wikipedia page was read 39,947 times, and the album's own page had its best day ever at 22,306.

Three days later she performed at the 97th Academy Awards. Her page was read 96,450 times that day — more than double the release day, and the highest single day in the 21 months we measured. Eleven days before the release, the premiere of The White Lotus season 3, in which she acted, had already drawn 83,043.

| Day (UTC) | What happened | Reads of LISA's page |
|---|---|---|
| 17 Feb 2025 | The White Lotus season 3 premiere | 83,043 |
| 28 Feb 2025 | "Alter Ego" released | 39,947 |
| 3 Mar 2025 | Performance at the Academy Awards | 96,450 |

## This autumn: the same shape again

The lead single "SaWaDiKa" was released on 3 September 2026. Reading went from 3,276 the day before to 14,909, then eased back over the following week.

On 27 September she performed the song live for the first time at the MTV Video Music Awards. Because Wikimedia counts in UTC, that US evening shows up as 28 September: 79,154 reads, about five times the single's release day.

| Day (UTC) | What happened | Reads of LISA's page |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Sep 2026 | Day before the single | 3,276 |
| 4 Sep 2026 | "SaWaDiKa" released | 14,909 |
| 28 Sep 2026 | VMAs performance | 79,154 |

## Where she stands going into today

Her baseline has risen. Over the 30 days to 21 October her page drew a median of 5,376 reads a day, against 3,433 over the summer, up 57%. That is now higher than the BLACKPINK group page itself, which held steady at about 3,500. She also reads ahead of bandmates Rosé (2,920) and Jisoo (1,842) over the same 30 days.

BABYMONSTER, releasing on the same day, sits at a median of 1,066 reads, down 18% from its summer level of 1,298. The two acts are not competing for the same readers on English Wikipedia: LISA's ordinary day is five times BABYMONSTER's.

## What this does and does not say

This measures one thing: how many people opened an English-language encyclopedia page. It is a proxy for casual, look-it-up curiosity, not for streams, sales or fandom size, and it cannot say how "PRESS PLAY" will perform. What it does show is a pattern visible across two release cycles. For LISA, the moments that bring new people to her page have been the ones where she appears in front of audiences who were not already waiting for her music. A release on its own has not been one of them.
