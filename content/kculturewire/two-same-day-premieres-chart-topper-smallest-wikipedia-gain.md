Two Netflix K-dramas launched on the same day, October 9: the medical noir series "Doctor X: Mafia in White," led by Kim Ji-won, and the rom-com "Take Charge of My Heart," co-led by Kim Young-kwang and Chae Soo-bin. Five days later, The Korea Times reported that Doctor X had topped Netflix's weekly non-English TV chart in South Korea, Indonesia, the Philippines and Thailand, and reached the top 10 in 36 countries.

English Wikipedia reading, measured independently of that chart report, tells a different story about the three leads.

## The same premiere week, three different reading levels

| Actor | Show | Pre-premiere baseline (median) | Premiere week (median) | Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kim Young-kwang | Take Charge of My Heart | 810 | 11,608 | 14.3x |
| Chae Soo-bin | Take Charge of My Heart | 592 | 5,806 | 9.8x |
| Kim Ji-won | Doctor X: Mafia in White | 1,062 | 3,807 | 3.6x |

Kim Ji-won leads the show that press coverage singled out as a global chart-topper. Her English Wikipedia reading rose the least of the three leads, both in relative terms (3.6x her own baseline, against 9.8x and 14.3x for the other two) and in absolute premiere-week level — her median of 3,807 daily reads trails Chae Soo-bin's 5,806 and Kim Young-kwang's 11,608, even though Kim Ji-won started from the highest baseline of the three.

## A higher starting point, a lower ending point

Before either show premiered, Kim Ji-won was already the most-read of the three actors, with a median of 1,062 daily English Wikipedia reads against Kim Young-kwang's 810 and Chae Soo-bin's 592 — consistent with her being the more internationally established name coming in. That lead didn't hold. By premiere week, both Take Charge of My Heart leads had overtaken her in absolute reading, not just in rate of gain.

## What we can't say about the show itself

Doctor X: Mafia in White's own Wikipedia page shows a premiere-week median of 9,492 daily reads and a single-day peak of 21,479 on 11 October — a lower median than Take Charge of My Heart's own page (11,572) but a higher peak (Take Charge of My Heart's page peaked at 17,260). What we can't do is state a reliable baseline multiple for Doctor X's page: it has only six days of pageview history before the 9 October premiere, too few days for a trustworthy before/after ratio. We're flagging that gap rather than filling it with a weak number.

## What this does and doesn't say

This measures one specific audience — people who looked up an English Wikipedia page, a proxy for reference-style curiosity rather than streaming consumption or chart performance. It does not contradict The Korea Times' chart report, which is about Netflix's own viewership rankings, a different measurement entirely. What it does say is specific: the lead actor of the show press named a global chart-topper did not see the largest, or even the second-largest, English Wikipedia reading gain among three leads whose shows premiered the same day.

*English Wikipedia pageviews, all-access, user traffic only (bots excluded), daily, 20 September–15 October 2026; measured 17 October 2026. Medians, not means, so single days do not carry the whole window.*
