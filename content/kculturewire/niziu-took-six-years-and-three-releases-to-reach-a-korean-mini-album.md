NiziU released "Sour Grapes," their first Korean-market mini album, on 28 September at 6 p.m. KST —
five tracks, led by a title track that reinterprets Aesop's fable "The Fox and the Grapes." It is
the nine-member group's first Korean-market activity in about a year and six months.

## From single album to mini album, one step at a time

NiziU debuted in Japan on 30 November 2020, formed through the JYP Entertainment–Sony Music Japan
audition show "Nizi Project." Their entry into the Korean market didn't happen at debut, or even in
their first few years — it came almost three years later, and in a smaller format each time before
this release.

| Date | Release | Market | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 Nov 2020 | "Step and a Step" | Japan | Single (debut) |
| 30 Oct 2023 | "Press Play" | Korea | Single album (159,599 copies) |
| 31 Mar 2025 | "Love Line" | Korea | Single album (168,636 copies) |
| 28 Sep 2026 | "Sour Grapes" | Korea | **Mini album (5 tracks) — a first** |

Each step took longer to arrive than the last gap suggests it should: nearly three years from Japan
debut to any Korean release at all, then about a year and five months to a second Korean single
album, then about a year and six months more to their first Korean release in the larger mini-album
format.

## The sales number that did move

Between NiziU's two Korean single albums, Circle Chart figures cited on Wikipedia show Korean sales
went from 159,599 copies ("Press Play") to 168,636 copies ("Love Line") — a rise of about 5.7%. That
comparison is only possible because both prior releases shared the same single-album format; "Sour
Grapes" carries more tracks, so its sales, once reported, won't sit on the same scale.

*This tracks NiziU's own Japan-to-Korea release timeline and format history, sourced to allkpop and
Wikipedia's discography record. It is not a comparison against other groups, and it says nothing about
how "Sour Grapes" itself will chart — those figures aren't out yet.*
