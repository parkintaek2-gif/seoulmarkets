Circle Chart is the Korea Music Content Association's weekly ranking, and its Digital Chart (called Digital Comprehensive in Top Star News) ranks songs by a single index value. For **week 45, 1 to 7 November 2026**, it lists 200 songs. The top spot belongs to ***NEON*** by **A2O LITE** with an index of **22,261,265**, its fourth week at the top in the Wikipedia list. The second spot, a new entry, is ***With*** by **RESCENE** at 18,941,389. That is 85.1% of NEON's total, and it is only one of ten RESCENE songs in the table.

## What stands out

**Ten of the top 50, all of them in the top 39.** No other act has more than three songs in the top 50. Hanroro and AKMU have three each, and several acts, among them ILLIT, Hearts2Hearts and KiiiKiii, have two. In the top 50 there are 33 different acts, and RESCENE accounts for 10 of the 50 places.

**Five new entries in the top 22 are all RESCENE.** Of the six songs that are new in the top 50, five are RESCENE's (ranks 2, 8, 15, 20 and 22). The sixth is by TWS at rank 49. Those six are also all the new songs in the whole 200-row table.

**Three times the next act.** Add up the index values of every song by the same act in all 200 rows. RESCENE comes to **91,304,904**. The next act is ILLIT, with five songs and 29,717,077 index points. Third is A2O LITE, whose single song is worth 22,261,265. RESCENE's ten songs are 4.1 times NEON's single entry, and they make up 12.2% of the 746,164,810 index points in all 200 rows. The 200-row total is the sum of the listed values, not a measure of the whole market.

**The same act sits on the album chart.** On the week 45 album chart, RESCENE's PULSE appears three times, at ranks 3, 8 and 14, with 336,916, 93,900 and 22,770 shipments. Our earlier piece on that chart counted the three together at 453,586.

**How the index falls away.** The index is 22,261,265 at rank 1, 8,769,669 at rank 10, 4,410,989 at rank 50, 2,762,564 at rank 100 and 1,501,708 at rank 200. The top 10 holds 18.4% of all index points in the 200 rows, the top 50 holds 50.9%, and the top 100 holds 74.5%.

## How this was checked

The Circle Chart table was read directly from the chart's own data. It shows 200 rows for the week, and the page header says Digital Chart, week 45. NEON's first place matches Top Star News and a Wikipedia list. Every other rank and index value comes from the Circle table alone; no second source for them was found. The per-act sums are this desk's additions.

This is a count of chart positions, not a judgement of any song or act.
