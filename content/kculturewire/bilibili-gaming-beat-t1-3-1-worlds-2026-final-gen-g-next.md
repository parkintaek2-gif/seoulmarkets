**Bilibili Gaming** (BLG) are into the Worlds 2026 final. On **9 November** the LPL side beat **T1** 3-1 in the second semi-final. They will play **Gen.G**, who swept Invictus Gaming 3-0 on 8 November, on **15 November at 04:00 KST**.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | T1 | 22-10 | 10-2 | 70,035-60,214 |
| 3 | BLG | 19-10 | 11-4 | 77,613-68,249 |
| 4 | BLG | 21-9 | 9-4 | 77,713-68,791 |

*Source: Riot Games official match records, end of each game. Numbers are winner first.*

## BLG's three wins

In game one mid laner **Knight** went 8/0/9 and bottom laner **Viper** 6/2/7. In game three Viper went 7/1/8 with 373 creep score. In game four top laner **Bin** went 7/1/7 and jungler **Xun** added 16 assists. BLG led gold by 7,784, 9,364 and 8,922 in those three games.

## T1's one game

T1 won game two on kills, 22 to 10, and towers, 10 to 2. **Faker** went 4/0/13, **Peyz** 5/1/8, and support **Keria** made 16 assists. T1 could not repeat it. In game three Peyz made 408 creep score, the most in the game, and T1 still lost on kills 19-10. In game four Faker made 368 creep score on a 3/1/4 line.

Across the four games Faker finished on 14 kills and 5 deaths.
