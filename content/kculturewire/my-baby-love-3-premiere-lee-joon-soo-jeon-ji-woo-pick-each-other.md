***My Baby's Romance 3***, a dating show in which parents watch their children's love lives from a studio, premiered on **11 November** on tvN STORY and E Channel. **Kim Sung-joo** hosts. The dad panel is Lee Jong-hyuk, basketball coach Jeon Hee-chul, former footballer Lee Dong-gook, comedian Im Hyuk-pil, and actors Jung Hee-tae and Choi Dae-chul. The six contestants are Lee Joon-soo, Jeon Ji-woo, Im Hye-in, Jung Min-jae, Choi Sang-moon and Lee Jae-ah.

This account rests on TenAsia's report and frames supplied by tvN STORY and E Channel. No episode 1 rating was found, so none is given.

## The first-impression vote

Lee Joon-soo and Jeon Ji-woo chose each other. Lee, 194 cm, introduced himself as someone who has never dated. Jeon was introduced as a Korean-dance major. She recognised Lee at once, while he did not yet know their connection.

Choi Sang-moon and Jung Min-jae both picked Im Hye-in. She chose Choi, making them a mutual pair. Lee Jae-ah picked Lee Joon-soo. The on-screen chart showed two votes each for Lee Joon-soo and Im Hye-in, one each for Choi Sang-moon and Jeon Ji-woo, and none for Jung Min-jae or Lee Jae-ah.

## In their words

After the first meeting, Lee Joon-soo gave his private thought:

> Lee Joon-soo: "I thought it was good that I came."

Choi Sang-moon, talking to Im Hye-in, said:

> Choi Sang-moon: "You make me nervous. That's quite something."

Both lines are TenAsia's wording, translated. The scenes they came from were not checked against the broadcast.

## Three mini dates

Lee Joon-soo and Jeon Ji-woo went shopping at a market; a caption spoke of excitement and nerves. Choi Sang-moon and Im Hye-in shared dessert at a cafe. Jung Min-jae and Lee Jae-ah cooked together and saw new sides of each other.

## The newcomer

A text announced a new woman moving in. She turned out to be Lee Jae-si, Lee Dong-gook's daughter and Lee Jae-ah's twin sister. An on-screen caption called her the first twin sister to appear on a dating show of this kind. How she changes the pairs already formed is the first open question of the season.
