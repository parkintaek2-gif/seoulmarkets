SBS's variety show ***Teumman-namyeon,*** (roughly "Whenever There Is a Gap") aired on **10 November** at 9 pm. SBS Entertainment News reports Nielsen Korea's Seoul-area figures at **3.4%** of households and a **4.9%** minute peak, with 1.3% among viewers aged 20 to 49. It was first among variety and general-programming shows in its slot for a **13th straight week**, and first among Tuesday variety shows. Topstar News says the 3 November episode was first for a 12th week, so the two counts agree.

Hosts **Yoo Jae-suk** and **Yoo Yeon-seok** were joined by Blackpink's **Jisoo** and Twice's **Nayeon**. They visited Sanbon-dong in Gunpo, Gyeonggi Province, to play short games with residents.

## Two guests, one old friendship

SBS Entertainment News says the two surprised the hosts with how close they are. Jisoo said:

> Jisoo: "We've been close since we were trainees. We eat together often."

Jisoo lived in Sanbon until she was 10, but drew a line at the local title.

> Jisoo: "I lived in Sanbon until I was 10, but the 'daughter of Sanbon' is Kim Yuna."

Yoo Yeon-seok answered, "Then Jisoo is the second daughter." Nayeon was presented as the game player.

> Nayeon: "They say money comes in if you use my photo as your wallpaper."
> Nayeon: "I like games. I often bet on them."

On coffee, Yoo Jae-suk drank his weak, and Nayeon said she likes it weak too. Jisoo, who adds extra shots, said, "At this strength, isn't it plum tea?"

## Game one: shoes against a box tower

The first game was throwing a sneaker to topple a tower of running-shoe boxes. Yoo Yeon-seok, who is weak at foot games, vowed:

> Yoo Yeon-seok: "Today I'll wipe out the disgrace of the foot."

He hit the boxes on his second try, and on the sixth the two hosts cleared stage two in one throw. Stage three required avoiding red boxes. They used a chance for two extra shoes but failed on the tenth try.

## Game two: catching the grapes

The second host was a mother and daughter who both like the group TXT. The game, "Catch the Grapes," imitates booking concert tickets. Jisoo and Nayeon explained fan jargon to the two hosts. Topstar News adds that Yoo Jae-suk said clapping alone at concerts is lonely, and that Yoo Yeon-seok asked whether "sonminsu" is a verb.

With one chance left, Yoo Jae-suk and Nayeon cleared stage two. Yoo Yeon-seok said:

> Yoo Yeon-seok: "In the end, 'the second daughter of Sanbon' and 'the goddess of luck' did it."

Nayeon asked to be called again whenever the team plays games.

The show airs Tuesdays at 9 pm. Ratings and quotes come from SBS Entertainment News; Topstar News was used to cross-check the date, cast and streak.
