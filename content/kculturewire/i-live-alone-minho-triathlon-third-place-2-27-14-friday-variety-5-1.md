Shinee's **Minho** took third place in the men's 35-39 amateur class at a triathlon in Tongyeong, South Gyeongnam, and MBC's ***I Live Alone*** showed it on **6 November** (episode 672). His finish time was **2 hours 27 minutes 14 seconds**, a personal best, over 1.5 km of swimming, 40 km of cycling and 10 km of running. Money Today reports the race as the 2026 Tongyeong World Triathlon Cup, held on 25 October.

Nielsen Korea put the episode at **5.1%** of Seoul-area households, No. 1 among Friday variety shows. Among viewers aged 20 to 54 it drew 2.7%, also No. 1 on Friday. The peak minute reached 6.0%, at the moment Minho stood one minute from the start. iZE gives the nationwide household rating as 4.7%, against 4.8% the previous Friday.

## The swim, the bike and the finish

Minho set himself a target of finishing in the 2 hour 20-minute range: swim within 30 minutes, bike in the 1 hour 10-minute range, and make up the rest on the run. He said he entered because he wanted to try an extreme sport, and that his goal was a personal best.

In the swim, another competitor's foot hit him in the face. "Fighting for space is somewhat allowed when you swim," he said. "It felt like blood." He kept going and finished the swim in 26 minutes (My Daily). On the way to the transition area, studio host **Jun Hyun-moo** noticed his eye and Minho agreed it was "slightly swollen".

The bike leg began in heavy rain, with other riders slipping on the wet road. His gear shifter stopped working on the hardest climb, and he shouted to himself, "No excuses! No whining!" Studio regular **Code Kunst** said he was riding with twice the effort. At the 17.5 km mark the chain came off, and putting it back took more than a minute. Minho said the bike leg ran 3 to 4 minutes behind his target and that he planned to make it up on the run.

He crossed the line in 2:27:14. "I couldn't imagine finishing third," he said afterwards, and he named a next goal: the 226.2 km full-distance race. "Next time I have to become a real Ironman."

## The rest of the episode

**Lee Joo-seung** appeared as a judge for a short-film festival, with 50 films to assess and criteria he gave as sincerity, originality, experimentation and mise-en-scene. He then visited an acquaintance's farm hut, harvesting sweet potatoes, cabbage and radish and making a wood-fired pizza.

In the studio, Jun said **Kim Shin-young** had received a Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism commendation at the Korean Popular Culture and Arts Awards. Kim said she does not normally react strongly to praise, but that "after getting an award, everything felt like something to be thankful for."

## Context from the same week

OSEN reported that on 1 November Minho finished the JTBC Seoul Marathon in 3:00:18, after which footage showed a pacemaker pushing him on the back, raising a question under World Athletics rules. On 3 November he apologised on social media and returned his race result to the organiser. That is a separate race from the triathlon shown on 6 November.

The next episode of *I Live Alone* airs **13 November at 11:10 pm KST** on MBC.
