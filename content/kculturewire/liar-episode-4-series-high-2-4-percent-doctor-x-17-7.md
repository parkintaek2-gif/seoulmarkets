MBC's twelve-episode thriller ***Liar*** aired its fourth episode on **7 November** at 9:50 pm KST. Nielsen Korea put it at **2.4%** nationwide, up from 2.2% in episode 3 and the highest of the series so far. It was the second rise in a row after 1.9% in each of the first two episodes. On the same night, SBS's ***Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*** drew **17.7%** for episode 10.

| Series (7 November, nationwide) | Episode | Rating | Previous episode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia (SBS) | 10 | 17.7% | 15.3% |
| Liar (MBC) | 4 | 2.4% | 2.2% |

*Source: Nielsen Korea, as reported by News1, iZE and Joynews24.*

Doctor X rose 2.4 points from 15.3% in episode 9, and Donga.com reported a peak minute of 20.2%. The gap to Liar is 15.3 points.

## What happened in episode 4

***Liar*** follows two people who describe the same night in opposite ways: **Min Jun-ho** (Yoo Yeon-seok), a cardiac surgeon, and **Kang Ji-seon** (Seo Hyun-jin), a primary-school librarian teacher. The series is directed by Jo Young-min and written by Baek Jae-young.

Episode 4 explains why Kang was marked as lying in the lie-detector test. She had been involved in a similar case before and, afraid of being seen as someone who files complaints habitually, answered that question falsely. The person she had reported earlier leaked the story to the press and sent her threatening messages. She lost her job.

Kang decided she would rather fight than "just breathe like the dead," and posted under her real name on the message board of a programme Min had appeared on. Min lost a surgery slot, and his son Min Hyun-su was hurt in a fight. Min shouted at her to stop the lies. She answered, "I can't stop. It isn't a lie."

Police detective Lee Hwa-young (Ok Ja-yeon) warned Kang that Min was dangerous, and she treated Min strictly by the book when he approached her. Ten Asia noted that Min's changed manner toward her fed viewers' suspicion.

That night, someone entered Lee's home while she slept alone. It was Min. The episode ended on his face in the dark.
