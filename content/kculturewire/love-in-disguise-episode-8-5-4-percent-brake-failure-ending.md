tvN's Monday-Tuesday drama ***Love in Disguise*** (Korean title *Na-ui Yujoe-ingan*) aired episode 8 on **10 November** at 8:50 pm. Wikitree reports Nielsen Korea's paid-platform figure, which combines cable, IPTV and satellite, at **5.4%** nationwide on average with a **6.9%** peak. The Seoul metropolitan area averaged 5.3% and peaked at 6.6%.

The report says the drama has led its slot among cable and general-programming channels in all eight episodes, and led all channels, terrestrial included, among viewers aged 20 to 49. It gives no earlier ratings, so we cannot say whether episode 8 rose or fell.

The drama follows a chaebol heir suspected of murder and the special-forces detective who takes a job as his aide in disguise. **Im Si-wan** plays Yoon Yi-joon, **Seol In-ah** plays Kang Jae-hee, **Kim Jung-hyun** plays Kim Si-hyun and **Yeon Woo** plays Lee Hye-jung.

## The commotion was a plan

Episode 8 revealed that the earlier commotion at a corporate event was a plan by the four leads. A recording pen left by Han Eun-young (Jung Yu-min) had caught suspicious behavior by Taegang Group executive Yoon Na-jung (Park Ah-in) and chief of staff Na Man-seok (Jo Seung-yeon). To provoke the people behind them, the group leaked word to police that Yoon had the pen.

The bait was deliberate, but the danger was real. Yoon received a belt by courier that reminded him of his mother's suicide and Han's death.

## Who sent the belt

Kang and her squad traced the courier and found that Yoon Na-jung had placed the order, which deepened suspicion of her. Meanwhile Na Man-seok objected to Yoon Yi-joon's conduct in front of Chairman Yoon Tae-sik (Choi Jin-ho) after CCTV footage of Yoon rescuing Kang went viral. He did not tell the chairman that the footage had brought the company a large publicity gain.

## A confession, a refusal, a crash

Yoon admitted he had always hidden his feelings but could no longer do so around Kang. He asked whether she would have liked him if they had been a man and a woman. Kang, working as a male aide, said she felt no flutter at all, and Yoon was hurt.

On the way to a memorial service for Yoon's mother, the car's brakes failed and it crashed. Someone approached the unconscious Yoon and stole the recording pen. Kang fought the thief, who fled at the sound of a police car, and then collapsed. The episode closed with Yoon waking and hearing Kang's sex from a paramedic.

## What comes next

tvN DRAMA's official channel posted a making-of video right after the broadcast, calling Im and Seol "Bangbang Gangjwi" and Kim and Yeon "Neunggeul Nyang-i" in its title. Episode 9 airs on **16 November** at 8:50 pm, and all episodes stream on TVING. The drama is written by Moon Su-jung and directed by Park Dan-hee.
