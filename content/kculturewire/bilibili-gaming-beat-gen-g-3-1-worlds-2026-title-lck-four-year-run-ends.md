**Bilibili Gaming** (BLG) beat **Gen.G** 3-1 in the Worlds 2026 final at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York, on 14 November in the United States (the early hours of 15 November in Korea). It is Bilibili Gaming's first world championship, and it ends a run of four straight titles for teams from Korea's LCK.

The series was a best of five. Gen.G led for exactly one game.

## The four games

| Game | Winner | Length | Series |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gen.G | 38:59 | Gen.G 1-0 |
| 2 | BLG | 53:16 | 1-1 |
| 3 | BLG | 37:39 | BLG 2-1 |
| 4 | BLG | 42:58 | BLG 3-1 |

The four games add up to 172 minutes 52 seconds. Game two, at 53 minutes 16 seconds, is the longest of the four by more than ten minutes. Kukinews describes Gen.G as trailing early in that game, pulling back with a team fight around the 25-minute mark and holding off a dragon steal late, before BLG broke three inhibitors to finish it. Game three was the shortest at 37:39.

Bilibili Gaming won three games in a row after dropping the opener.

## How BLG got there

According to the tournament page, BLG finished the Swiss stage 3-2. It beat Team Liquid Alienware, Karmine Corp and Hanwha Life Esports and lost to Movistar KOI and Top Esports. In the knockout rounds it swept Dplus KIA 3-0 in the quarter-finals, beat T1 3-1 in the semi-finals, and then beat Gen.G 3-1 in the final.

All three of its knockout opponents were Korean teams, and the Swiss stage added a fourth, Hanwha Life. Bilibili Gaming's route took it past all four LCK teams at the event: Hanwha Life, Dplus KIA, T1 and Gen.G.

## What the result ends

The LCK had won the world title in each of the previous four years, from 2022 through 2025. T1 were the three-time defending champions coming in and lost to BLG in the semi-finals. Gen.G's loss in the final sends the trophy to China's LPL for the first time since 2021.

For readers new to the format: the LCK is Korea's top professional League of Legends league and the LPL is China's. Worlds brings the best teams from each region into one bracket, and Korean and Chinese teams have traded the title back and forth for most of the past decade.
