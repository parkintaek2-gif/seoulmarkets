The **Worlds 2026 final** on **15 November at 04:00 KST** is **Gen.G** of the LCK against **Bilibili Gaming** (BLG) of the LPL. This desk pulled every Worlds knockout series from Riot's official schedule data and kept only those between an LCK team and an LPL team. The data runs from the 2023 semi-finals to this year's semi-finals, all best-of-five.

*Source: Riot Games official schedule data. Series between teams of the same league are left out.*

## Seven series to three, 23 games to 14

The LCK won seven of the ten series and 23 of the 37 games. Six of the seven LCK wins belong to **T1**; the other is Gen.G's sweep of IG this year. All three LPL wins belong to **BLG**, over Hanwha Life in 2024 and over Dplus KIA and T1 in 2026.

BLG is 3-1 against LCK teams in this window. The one loss was the **2024 final**, 2-3 to T1. T1 is 6-1 against LPL teams, and the one loss is this year's semi-final to BLG.

## This year only

Three LCK-v-LPL series were played in this year's knockout rounds. BLG beat Dplus KIA and T1, and Gen.G beat IG, so the LPL leads 2-1 this year. In the three years before, LCK teams won six such series and lost one.

## The two finalists

Gen.G has played one LPL team in this window, IG in this year's semi-final, and won 3-0. BLG has beaten an LCK team three times. Gen.G and BLG have not met in a Worlds knockout series in this data.

## What was not checked

The official schedule data did not return series before the 2023 semi-finals, so earlier Worlds are not counted. Each team's league was assigned by this desk from public knowledge and not matched one by one to official team data. This piece only adds up series results and game scores. It does not rate either team or predict the final.
