Korean press this week reported that BTS's 2026 single "Arirang" had passed 5 billion streams on
Spotify and moved the group past Olivia Rodrigo for the platform's top streaming spot. It is the
kind of number that is built to travel — round, comparative, and framed as a win over a specific
rival.

We did not re-run Spotify's numbers; we have no way to. What we can measure independently is a
different kind of attention: how many people opened each artist's English Wikipedia page, every
day, over the same stretch the streaming story broke in.

## The same 44 days, read the other way

| | BTS | Olivia Rodrigo |
|---|---|---|
| Median daily reads, full window | 5,159 | 11,932 |
| Median daily reads, first 22 days | 6,004 | 9,806 |
| Median daily reads, last 22 days | 4,934 | 12,553 |
| Change, first half to second half | −18% | +28% |

Rodrigo's reading rose through the same window BTS is reported to have overtaken her streaming
total. BTS's fell.

## It is not a close call in the final week

| Day (most recent 7) | BTS | Olivia Rodrigo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4,660 | 13,761 |
| 2 | 4,928 | 12,109 |
| 3 | 4,876 | 24,974 |
| 4 | 4,856 | 18,938 |
| 5 | 4,589 | 13,966 |
| 6 | 4,712 | 12,500 |
| 7 | 4,376 | 10,579 |

Rodrigo's single biggest day in this stretch (24,974) is more than five times BTS's biggest day in
the same seven days (4,928).

## BTS was already higher before any of this

Reading further back, BTS's own median over the 63 days before our measurement window (1 July to
1 September 2026) was 7,959 — above both halves of the period we measured here. The decline is not
a one-week dip against Rodrigo specifically; by this measure, interest in BTS on English Wikipedia
has been easing for months.

## What this does and does not say

A streaming total and a Wikipedia page view answer different questions. One counts plays on a
platform where catalogue depth, playlists and years of back-catalogue streams accumulate; the other
counts people who, that day, chose to open an encyclopedia page. A group can lead on one and trail
on the other without either number being wrong.

What we can say is narrow: whatever is driving BTS's reported Spotify milestone this week, it has
not shown up as rising English-language reference-lookup interest. By that specific measure, the
two curves are moving in opposite directions.

*English Wikipedia pageviews, all-access, user traffic only (bots excluded), daily, 1 September to
14 October 2026; measured 15 October 2026. Medians, not means, so a single viral day does not carry
the whole window. The Spotify figure is attributed to the cited press report and was not
independently recomputed.*
