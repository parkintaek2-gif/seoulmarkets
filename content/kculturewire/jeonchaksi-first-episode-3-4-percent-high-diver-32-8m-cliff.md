MBC's ***Omniscient Obsessive Point of View***, a spin-off of ***Omniscient Interfering View***, aired episode 1 on **8 November**, a Sunday at 6:05 pm. The hosts are **Jeon Hyun-moo**, Minumsa editor **Kim Min-kyung**, comedian **Kim Won-hoon** and chef **Yoon Nam-no**. The show profiles "oddities" who have staked their lives on one thing. TenAsia says Jeon is the only host kept from the original show.

On ratings, TenAsia reports **3.4%** nationwide, above SBS ***Running Man*** at 3.0% in the same slot. iMBC Entertainment and bnt News report **2.0%** among 2054 viewers, first in the slot. The two figures use different bases and cannot be compared. The 3.4% comes from TenAsia alone.

## A first-time network host

According to My Daily and TenAsia, Kim Min-kyung was making her first appearance as a network host. She introduced herself this way:

> Kim Min-kyung: "My obsession is wanting to be funny."

## First oddity: game developer Kim Jae-seo

The first guest was Kim Jae-seo, known from the dating show *I'm Solo but Want to Date 2*. He graduated early from a science high school, earned a KAIST mathematical sciences degree and worked on the early *PUBG* team. His life revolves around games, not dating. He handles most of daily life at a company with drinks, snacks, a shower room and a sleeping room, and wakes up there even on Saturdays to play in an empty office. He sometimes turns off the status screen to raise the difficulty.

The records were shown: 203 games in a year and 1,206 achievements. His boss Im Ung-bin called him someone "crazy about games." iMBC Entertainment reports he plans and analyzes games, judging the success chances of new releases from his own play.

## Second oddity: high diver Choi Byung-hwa

The second guest was Choi Byung-hwa, introduced as Korea's first and only high diver. He is taking on natural cliffs across the country to promote the sport. This time the site was Gyeokryeolbiyeoldo, about 2 hours 30 minutes by boat from the mainland. Firefighter Kim Jung-ho, professional climbers and water-safety crew came along, and strong waves made even finding the diving point hard.

Seeing Choi on the cliff, Jeon said:

> Jeon Hyun-moo: "It's like a mountain movie. But movies are CG, and this is real."

Choi dived successfully from a **32.8 m** cliff and set a personal record. The scene in which he named "Sky Island" as his next target peaked at **4.9%** per minute, according to iMBC Entertainment, citing Nielsen Korea.

## What's next

The preview showed a "ramen oddity" named Woongseong, who says he has eaten 3,500 bowls, and paper-airplane national team member Lee Jung-wook. The show airs "every Sunday at 6:05 pm". By that schedule episode 2 would be 15 November, a date we calculated, so check the listing.

Reports differ on the height: TenAsia and iMBC Entertainment introduce high diving as a 27 m jump, about ten stories, and give 32.8 m for this one. We use 32.8 m only for this jump. Ratings are Nielsen Korea figures as reported by news outlets; quotes come from outlets that watched the broadcast. We did not watch it ourselves.
