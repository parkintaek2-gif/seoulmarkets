On **8 November**, KBS2's ***1 Night 2 Days*** Season 4 aired the second part of its Yeosu trip, with **Kim Jong-min**, **Moon Se-yoon**, **Dindin**, **Lee Joon**, **Lee Yong-jin** and **Lee Ki-taek**. Nielsen Korea figures cited by Topstar News, TenAsia and RNX put it at **7.7% nationwide**, first in its time slot. The scene of Lee Yong-jin and Lee Joon writing wishes on roof tiles at Hyangilam peaked at **11.1%**. Topstar News and RNX add 1.7% among 2049 viewers in the Seoul area. **None of the reports gives the episode number.**

## Gold coins by day

At Odongdo the members earned 50 gold coins in a mission with local citizens. In the "Everything About the Average" mission they got 5 of 8 questions right and won 38 more. TenAsia reports Moon accurately guessed Koreans' yearly chicken and instant-noodle consumption. The members said: "Se-yoon has lost his mind" and "Real respect."

Dinner was a buffet. RNX quotes the crew: "Take as much as you like. But you must make the shared buffet dishes from the foil we sell." TenAsia says 42 coins were spent on it.

## The room-restoration mission

The crew said: "Restore the room to the state it was in when you left." The members believed staff had moved things. The crew later said: "In fact, the staff changed nothing after entering the room." They had 7 wrong answers and won 30 of 100 coins. A coin toss gave Lee Yong-jin and Lee Ki-taek the indoor beds; the other four slept in tents.

## The go-home draw

Next morning the crew offered a "gift" car and a "wish" car. According to TenAsia, Kim, Moon, Dindin and Lee Ki-taek took the gift car; Lee Yong-jin and Lee Joon took the wish car. The crew said: "The gift we are giving you is 'going home.'"

The four went home. Lee Ki-taek escaped the go-home penalty for the first time in 18 weeks since joining. "I have been in good spirits lately," he said. TenAsia adds that Dindin objected to Lee riding with him and tried to get out of the car.
