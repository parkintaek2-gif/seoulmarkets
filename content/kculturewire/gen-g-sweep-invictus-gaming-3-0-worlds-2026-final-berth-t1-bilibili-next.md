**Gen.G** are into the Worlds 2026 final. On **8 November** the LCK side swept **Invictus Gaming** (LPL) 3-0 in the first semi-final. The final is on **15 November at 04:00 KST**. Gen.G's opponent will be the winner of **T1 v Bilibili Gaming** on **9 November at 07:00 KST**.

## Games one and two: nobody on IG lived

In game one Gen.G won 29 kills to 9. Jungler **Canyon** went 7/1/12 and bottom laner **Ruler** 9/2/11. Every IG player died at least four times, with Wei on 8 and TheShy on 6.

Game two was the same shape, 27 kills to 9. Canyon finished 5/0/15 without a death. Mid laner **Chovy** went 6/1/10, and support **Duro** added 17 assists. IG's support Meiko died eight times.

## Game three: fewer kills, more towers

IG's mid laner **Rookie** made 11 kills against 2 deaths in game three, and IG finished ahead on kills, 18 to 16. It did not lead on anything else. Gen.G took nine towers to five, the baron, and a gold lead of 78,179 to 71,690. Ruler ended the game on 7 kills and 437 creep score. That was enough to finish the series.

Gen.G also swept LYON 3-0 in the quarter-finals, so they have not dropped a game in the knockout stage.
