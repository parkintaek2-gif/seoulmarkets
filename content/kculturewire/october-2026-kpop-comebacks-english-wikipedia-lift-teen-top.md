About 150 releases by Korean acts are listed for October 2026, from full albums to Japanese singles. For each artist with an English Wikipedia article and three days of data after release, we asked one question: **did the release make more people look them up?**

We compared reads in the three days starting on release day with the 14 days before.

## The typical comeback: 1.23 times

| Change in English Wikipedia reads | Artists |
|---|---|
| Doubled or more | 7 |
| Up 50% to 99% | 9 |
| Up 0% to 49% | 18 |
| **Down** | **14** |

The **median comeback lifted reads by 1.23 times**. For more than a quarter of the artists, the release week drew **fewer** English Wikipedia readers than the fortnight before it.

## The seven that doubled

| Artist | Release | Reads a day before | First three days | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teen Top | 15 Oct, *DON'T STOP* | 116 | **816** | **7.0×** |
| Sung Han-bin | 12 Oct, *DEAD:ALIVE* | 102 | 367 | 3.6× |
| Xdinary Heroes | 12 Oct, *IMPULSE* | 163 | 449 | 2.8× |
| Eric Nam | 16 Oct, *Confessions of a Lonely Heart* | 928 | 2,165 | 2.3× |
| Davichi | 13 Oct, with Epik High | 110 | 243 | 2.2× |
| JO1 | 2 Oct, *ANIMAL* | 141 | 293 | 2.1× |
| Nam Woo-hyun | 14 Oct, *2X8* | 79 | 160 | 2.0× |

Teen Top's page peaked at **1,416 reads on 16 October**, twelve times its usual day.

## The biggest names barely moved

| Artist | Release | Before | First three days | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Katseye | 2 Oct, remix single | 17,538 | 16,115 | 0.92× |
| Meovv | 12 Oct, *GO HARD* | 733 | 1,273 | 1.74× |
| Ive | 19 Oct, pre-release *Looks Can Kill* | 694 | 996 | 1.44× |
| Nmixx | 19 Oct, *Strange Muse* | 459 | 729 | 1.59× |
| Hearts2Hearts | 5 Oct, Korean version single | 698 | 696 | 1.00× |

Katseye's article is read more than all seven of the doubling acts combined, every day, and a remix single did not change that. A high base is hard to double: the readers who look up a well-known act are already there.

## What this does and does not say

English Wikipedia is one window on international interest, not the whole of it. It leaves out Korean-language readers, streaming and social media. A release that does not move it can still chart; a jump can come from news that has nothing to do with the music, and we did not separate the two. What the count shows is narrower: **a comeback, by itself, usually brings few new English-language readers.** The ones that did this month started from small bases: none of the seven was read more than 1,000 times a day beforehand.

*Release dates from the kpopcomebacks.com October 2026 calendar. English Wikipedia pageviews, user traffic, daily, 1 September–22 October 2026; medians, not means. Releases from 21 October on are left out until three days of data exist.*
