MBC's twelve-episode thriller ***Liar*** aired its third episode on **6 November** at 9:50 pm KST. Nielsen Korea put it at **2.2%** nationwide, up from 1.9% in each of the first two episodes and the highest of the series so far. On the same night and in the same slot, SBS's ***Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*** drew **15.3%** for episode 9, its third drop in a row.

| Series (6 November, nationwide) | Episode | Rating | Previous episode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia (SBS) | 9 | 15.3% | 15.6% |
| Liar (MBC) | 3 | 2.2% | 1.9% |

*Source: Nielsen Korea, as reported by Top Star News and iZE.*

Doctor X peaked at 19.5% in episode 6 and has since gone 16.5%, 15.6% and 15.3%. It is 4.2 points under that peak, and still 13.1 points above Liar. Doctor X streams on Netflix; Liar streams on Wave and Disney+ (Top Star News).

## What happened in episode 3

***Liar*** follows two people who describe the same night in opposite ways: **Min Jun-ho** (Yoo Yeon-seok), a cardiac surgeon, and **Kang Ji-seon** (Seo Hyun-jin), a primary-school librarian teacher. The series is directed by Jo Young-min and written by Baek Jae-young.

Episode 3 opens after a lie-detector test that came out against Kang. Neither changed their account. What changed was how others saw them: at Min's hospital he became the victim, and she became the woman people whispered about.

Kang could not stop thinking about a rumour that Min's wife had died of a drug overdose. She slipped into his home, found a box of drugs under his bed and reported him to police. Min was taken away in front of his son, Min Hyun-su. The drug turned out to be insulin his wife had used, and he was cleared.

Min then stopped staying silent and decided to take the matter to the press. When the article ran, the rumour reached Kang's school. In the classroom, Hyun-su asked her, "Was that woman the teacher?" Kang backed away and ran out.

The episode ends with police detective Lee Hwa-young (Ok Ja-yeon) telling Min that the case was dropped for lack of evidence. Min, who had been hostile to her, changed tone and asked, in the iMBC recap's wording, whether she would like a drink. She said no once; he asked again. iMBC and other outlets described this as the moment that deepens viewers' doubts about Min.

## What we can and cannot say

Two measures are in circulation for the episode, and they are different things. The nationwide household rating is 2.2%. The broadcaster's release separately reports a peak minute of 4.2%, citing Nielsen Korea but not naming a region. We have not set one beside the other.

Episode 4 airs **7 November at 9:50 pm KST** on MBC.
