SBS's Friday-Saturday drama ***Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*** aired its 12th and final episode on **Saturday 14 November 2026**. Nielsen Korea's national household rating was **15.2%**, up 0.7 points from the 14.5% the night before. The drama had peaked at **19.5%** in episode 6 and opened at **11.4%**. This drama aired two nights a week, and Nielsen Korea publishes a rating for each broadcast day. This piece sets all 12 side by side with the Seoul-area rating and the viewer count.

## What stands out

**Saturday beat Friday in five of six weeks.** Each week's two episodes aired on consecutive nights, so the Saturday episode can be set against the Friday one before it. Saturday was higher by 3.5 points (episodes 1 and 2), 3.4 (3 and 4), 2.0 (5 and 6), 2.4 (9 and 10) and 0.7 (11 and 12). The one exception was week 4, when Friday's 16.5% beat Saturday's 15.6% by 0.9 points.

**Friday usually fell back from the Saturday before.** Going the other way, from a Saturday to the next Friday, the rating fell in four of five cases: 18.5% to 17.5%, 19.5% to 16.5%, 15.6% to 15.3% and 17.7% to 14.5%. The only rise was 14.9% to 15.1%. The biggest single drop in the whole run is the last one, 3.2 points from episode 10 to episode 11, larger than the 3.0 points between episodes 6 and 7.

**Six rises, five falls.** Across the 11 episode-to-episode comparisons the rating went up six times and down five. The finale's rise of 0.7 points was the smallest of the six rises except the 0.2 of episodes 2 to 3.

**The Seoul area was never higher than the nation.** In all 12 episodes the Seoul-area rating was at or below the national rating. The gap was 0.0 in episodes 9 and 10, at most 0.7 in episodes 7 and 12, and 0.2 or less in the first four episodes. Seoul-area viewers made up between **60.4% and 63.9%** of national viewers across the 12 episodes, a share this desk calculated from Nielsen Korea's two counts.

**Where the finale sits.** At 15.2%, episode 12 is the eighth highest of the 12 national figures. It is 4.3 points below the peak and 3.8 points above the opening episode. In viewers, the finale's 2,982 thousand is 75.2% of the peak of 3,964 thousand and 755 thousand more than the 2,227 thousand of episode 1.

## How this was checked

The national figures come from Nielsen Korea's day tables for each of the 12 broadcast days. The Seoul-area figures come from the same tables viewed for the Seoul area. Both views were read directly from Nielsen Korea's website, one broadcast day at a time. The first ten national figures match the press-based list this desk published on 14 November, and the last two match two press reports of the finale. Neither press report gives a Seoul-area figure, so that column rests on Nielsen Korea alone.

Top Star News reports that *Good Partner 2* takes over the Friday-Saturday slot on the 20th. These are viewing figures, not a verdict on the drama.
