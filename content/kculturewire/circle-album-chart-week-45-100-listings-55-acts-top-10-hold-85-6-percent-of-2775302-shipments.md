Circle Chart's album chart is built from shipments: copies delivered to retailers, minus returns. The full list for **week 45, 1 to 7 November 2026**, runs to 100 listings. Our 14 November piece counted the top ten by act. This one reads all 100. They add up to **2,775,302** shipments from **55 acts**, and the first ten rows hold most of that.

## How the shipments fall away

| Listings | Shipments | Share of the 100-row total |
|---|---|---|
| Ranks 1-10 | 2,376,993 | 85.6% |
| Ranks 1-20 | 2,580,602 | 93.0% |
| Ranks 1-30 | 2,645,710 | 95.3% |
| Ranks 1-50 | 2,707,323 | 97.6% |
| Ranks 11-100 | 398,309 | 14.4% |

The listing at rank 1 shipped 768,577 copies, rank 10 shipped 28,175, rank 20 shipped 9,981, rank 50 shipped 2,264 and rank 100 shipped 898. The middle of the chart, the median of the 100 rows, is 2,240.5. Only **7** listings shipped 100,000 or more. **19** reached 10,000, **88** reached 1,000, and 12 sit under 1,000.

In plain terms, the 20th listing shipped about 1.3% as many copies as the first. The fifty listings from rank 51 to rank 100 together shipped 67,979 (2,775,302 minus 2,707,323), or 2.4% of the total.

## What stands out

**Eleven listings, one number ten.** Stray Kids and BTS each have **11** listings in the 100 rows, more than any other act. Stray Kids' eleven together shipped 21,589, which is less than the 28,175 of the single listing at rank 10. BTS's eleven shipped 15,357, and NewJeans' five shipped 8,752. ILLIT has six listings and 42,460 shipments. Together, Stray Kids, BTS and NewJeans account for 27 of the 100 rows and 45,698 shipments, 1.6% of the total.

**Most of the new weight is at the top.** The table marks **37** listings as new this week. They shipped 2,278,592 copies, 82.1% of the total. Eight of the top ten are new; the two that are not are AND2BLE (rank 5) and IVE (rank 6). The 63 listings not marked new shipped 496,710, of which 214,217 sit below rank 10.

**Some listings moved a long way.** The table also gives each listing's previous rank. The largest climbs among listings that were on the chart the week before:

| Listing | Previous rank | This week | Places climbed |
|---|---|---|---|
| KiiiKiii, WhyKiiiKiii | 176 | 33 | 143 |
| Stray Kids, THIS & THAT (FANS) | 128 | 26 | 102 |
| Teen Top, DON'T STOP | 150 | 52 | 98 |
| Namwoohyun, 2X8 | 122 | 46 | 76 |
| ONEW, Nemo (Korean title) | 171 | 96 | 75 |
| BTS, WINGS | 135 | 65 | 70 |
| NewJeans, How Sweet | 141 | 71 | 70 |

None of these climbs reaches the top 20. The highest of them, Stray Kids' listing, is 26th with 6,616 shipments, against 9,981 at rank 20.

**A concentrated chart, not a flat one.** If every listing had shipped the same, the top ten would hold 10%. The ten hold 85.6%. Within them, the top three are 768,577, 508,182 and 336,916, and the fourth is 185,308.

## How this was checked

The Circle Chart table was read directly from its data for week 45. TWS's first place and IVE's cumulative total were checked against a Wikipedia list. The top-ten sum agrees with this desk's 14 November piece. For every other listing there is only the Circle table, and the grouping of listings into acts is this desk's.

These are shipment counts for one week, not a measure of any act's popularity.
