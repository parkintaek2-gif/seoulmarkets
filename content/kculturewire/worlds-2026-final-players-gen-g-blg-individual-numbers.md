The Worlds 2026 final on **15 November at 04:00 KST** is **Gen.G** against **Bilibili Gaming** (BLG). Our earlier piece compared the two teams' totals. This one adds up the ten players' own numbers from the game-by-game records of the quarter-finals and semi-finals: six games for Gen.G, seven for BLG.

*KDA is (kills + assists) divided by deaths. Kill participation is (kills + assists) divided by that player's team kills in the same games. Both are rounded to one decimal. Champions used counts each champion once.*

## What stands out

Knight has the fewest deaths of the ten, 9 in seven games, and went without a death in three of them. On Gen.G, Kiin has the fewest, 9 in six games. XUN's 72.3% is the highest kill participation of the ten, ahead of Ruler at 71.9% and Canyon at 71.1%. The lowest is Bin's 51.5%.

Ruler has the most kills, 38. Viper has 37 in one more game. The most kills in a single game is 12, by BLG's Bin (quarter-final game three) and XUN (quarter-final game one).

## Champion range

Gen.G's top, jungle and mid players used five different champions in six games; the bot laner and support used four. All five BLG players used six different champions in seven games. Each player repeated exactly one or two picks: Gen.G's Kiin (Olaf), Canyon (Qiyana), Chovy (Taliyah), Ruler (Jhin, Ashe) and Duro (Leona, Seraphine), and BLG's Bin (Gnar), XUN (Naafiri), Knight (Orianna), Viper (Ezreal) and ON (Nautilus).
