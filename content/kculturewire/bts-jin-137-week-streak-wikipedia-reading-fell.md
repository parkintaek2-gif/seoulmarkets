Korean entertainment outlets have been running a near-weekly update for most of this year: BTS's Jin, StarNews reports, has not lost the top spot in MyOnePick's K-pop individual weekly ranking since the streak began — 128 weeks in mid-August, 131 by early September, 135 at the end of September, and 137 as of this week's report. It is the kind of number that keeps renewing itself, a fandom-strength story told in consecutive weeks rather than any single event.

We cannot verify the app's vote counts. What we can measure independently, over the same recent stretch, is a different kind of attention: how many people opened Jin's English Wikipedia page, each day.

## The same six weeks, read the other way

| | Reads |
|---|---|
| Median daily reads, full 45-day window (1 Sep–15 Oct) | 1,396 |
| Median daily reads, first 22 days | 1,696 |
| Median daily reads, last 22 days | 1,322 |
| Change, first half to second half | −22% |
| Baseline median, prior 63 days (1 Jul–1 Sep) | 1,931 |

Every figure in that table but one is below the baseline that came before it. English-language reference lookups for Jin have been easing for months, not just this stretch.

## The most recent day we have is the lowest one

The last date in our window, 15 October, recorded 1,102 reads — the single lowest day across all 45 days measured, arriving in the same week StarNews reported the streak reaching 137.

## What a vote and a page view are not

A MyOnePick vote and a Wikipedia page view are not measuring the same behavior. One is cast inside a fan-voting app, repeatedly, by people already following the artist closely — a streak like this one rewards sustained, organized fan participation more than it reflects general-audience attention. The other is a one-off visit to an encyclopedia page, often by someone checking a basic fact rather than voting for anything. An artist can hold one number steady for 137 straight weeks while the other drifts down, and neither number is wrong — they are counting different things.

What we can say is narrow: whatever is sustaining the MyOnePick streak, it has not shown up as rising, or even steady, English-language reference-lookup interest. By that specific measure, reading attention eased through the same stretch the streak extended.
