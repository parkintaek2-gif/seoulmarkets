SBS's Friday-Saturday drama ***Good Partner 2*** began on **Friday 20 November 2026**. Nielsen Korea's national household rating for episode 1 was **8.4%**. In the Seoul area it was **8.2%**. The first season's opening episode, in 2024, drew **7.8%**, so the new season began **0.6 points** higher. This piece sets the premiere against the rest of the day, using Nielsen Korea's day tables.

## The premiere in the day's table

Nielsen Korea's table for all channels on 20 November puts the programmes in this order at the top:

- A KBS1 daily drama: 10.3%
- SBS *Good Partner 2*: 8.4%
- A KBS2 daily drama: 7.9%
- A KBS1 documentary series: 7.6%
- KBS1 9 o'clock news: 6.6%

*Good Partner 2* was second among all programmes on its first night, behind a KBS1 daily drama and ahead of a KBS2 one.

## Against Liar in the same slot

MBC's Friday-Saturday drama *Liar* aired at nearly the same time. *Good Partner 2* ran from 9:51 pm to 11:00 pm and *Liar* from 9:56 pm to 11:02 pm. Nielsen Korea gives these figures:

| | SBS Good Partner 2 | MBC Liar |
|---|---|---|
| Nationwide rating | 8.4% | 5.2% |
| Seoul-area rating | 8.2% | 5.2% |
| Nationwide audience | 1,510,493 | 930,737 |
| Seoul-area audience | 917,559 | 576,750 |
| Rank among all programmes | 2nd | 7th |

The gap nationwide is 3.2 points, or 579,756 in audience. In the Seoul area the gap is 3.0 points: 8.2% against 5.2%.

An individual rating of 4.2% for *Good Partner 2* is reported by Top Star News. We did not find an individual rating for *Liar*.

## What came before in the slot

The previous drama in this slot, SBS's *Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia*, opened on 9 October at 11.4% and ended on 14 November at 15.2%. *Good Partner 2* opened 3.0 points below that premiere. The slot went from 15.2% on the last night of *Doctor X* to 8.4% on the first night of the next drama. Those are two different programmes at two different points in their runs, so the drop is not a measure of anything about the slot itself.

## What this does not say

This is the first of a drama's episodes, not a trend. Nielsen Korea had not yet published the second episode, which airs on 21 November. We do not give a reason for the rating, and we do not say how the drama will do. A premiere rating is not a prediction of the ones after it.
