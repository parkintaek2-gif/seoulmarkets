Circle Chart's week 45 album chart, covering **1 to 7 November 2026**, ranks listings, not acts. The same album sold in a different edition can appear as a separate row. That changes the picture when the top ten is counted by act.

## Ten listings, seven acts

The ten listings shipped **2,376,993** copies in total. Grouped by act:

| Act | Listings in the top 10 | Shipments | Share of the ten listings |
|---|---|---|---|
| TWS | 2 | 841,294 | 35.4% |
| P1Harmony | 1 | 508,182 | 21.4% |
| Rescene | 2 | 430,816 | 18.1% |
| Xikers | 2 | 286,033 | 12.0% |
| 앤더블 (English spelling not confirmed) | 1 | 142,860 | 6.0% |
| IVE | 1 | 139,633 | 5.9% |
| Hanroro | 1 | 28,175 | 1.2% |

*Shares are rounded to one decimal. Act totals add listings of the same album in different editions.*

## What changes

On the chart itself TWS's two listings sit at 1st (768,577) and 9th (72,717). Together they come to 841,294, which is 35.4% of the ten. The top three acts, TWS, P1Harmony and Rescene, hold **1,780,292** copies, or **74.9%**.

Rescene's two top-ten listings are 3rd (336,916) and 8th (93,900). Add them and the act comes to 430,816, which is 77,366 copies behind P1Harmony's 508,182. A third Rescene edition is 14th at 22,770 copies and is not counted here.

Xikers also has two listings, 4th and 7th, and ranks fourth among the seven acts at 286,033.

## Limits

Circle Chart counts shipments minus returns. It does not count sales. The act totals here add rows that the chart lists separately, and Circle Chart does not publish them. This is one week.
