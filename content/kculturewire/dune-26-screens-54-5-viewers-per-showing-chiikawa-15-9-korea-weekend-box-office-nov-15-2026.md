A film's rank on the weekend box office is a count of admissions. It says nothing about how many screens it needed or what each ticket cost. KOFIC's KOBIS network publishes screens, showings and sales next to admissions, so the same ten films from **13 to 15 November 2026** can be ranked three more ways. On every one of them the leader is not ***Chiikawa the Movie: Secret of the Mermaid Island***, the weekend's top film with 116,176 admissions. It is ***Dune***, a film that KOBIS lists with a 20 October 2021 release date.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Dune (2021) | 17,874 | 26 | 687.5 |
| 1 | Chiikawa the Movie | 116,176 | 859 | 135.2 |
| 2 | Pol 2: Deadpoint | 107,533 | 797 | 134.9 |
| 5 | Detective Conan: 30th Murder Case | 36,600 | 301 | 121.6 |

| 3 | Final Interview | 69,506 | 747 | 93.0 |
| 9 | Madoka Magica: Walpurgisnacht Rising | 14,727 | 163 | 90.3 |
| 6 | La La Land (2016) | 36,435 | 633 | 57.6 |
| 4 | Heart of Beast | 37,591 | 684 | 55.0 |
| 10 | The Cat with a Hat | 14,714 | 412 | 35.7 |

*Admissions per screen is this desk's division of two KOBIS figures. The weekend rank and this measure do not move together: Heart of Beast was fourth on admissions and ninth on admissions per screen.*

Dune's 26 screens are the fewest in the table by a wide margin: the next smallest footprint is Maka and Roni's 243, and the biggest is Chiikawa's 859. All ten together used 4,865 screens, and Dune's 26 are 0.5% of that. It still sits eighth on admissions, ahead of Madoka Magica on 163 screens and The Cat with a Hat on 412.

The ten films together were shown 32,361 times over the weekend. Dune's 328 showings are about 1% of that total, yet each one averaged 54.5 admissions, 3.4 times Chiikawa's 15.9. Maka and Roni, which opened on Saturday and so has only two days in this count, is second on this measure at 32.5. Pol 2 is third, and it is the only film in the top three of the weekend rank that also sits in the top three here. Heart of Beast is last at 9.0. Admissions are not seats: this table cannot say how full any of these showings was.

Nine of the ten films sit between **9,179 and 11,583 won** per admission. Dune is the outlier at **19,142 won**, close to double the median of the other nine. The KOBIS table gives sales and admissions but not ticket types, screen formats or discounts, so the gap is reported and not explained.

## What the three rankings show

Dune is first on all three measures and eighth on the plain weekend count. The leader on plain admissions, Chiikawa, is second per screen and fourth per showing. Chiikawa and Pol 2 are 135.2 and 134.9 per screen, within one third of one viewer of each other, though Pol 2 had 1,283 fewer showings. La La Land, with a 2016 release, is eighth per screen and ninth per showing. The two older films in the top 10 sit at opposite ends.

The weekend count therefore hides different scales. Chiikawa's 116,176 admissions came across 859 screens and 7,297 showings. Dune's 17,874 came across 26 and 328.

## How this was checked

The weekday and weekend KOBIS tables add up to the weekly table for the films present in all three, and the prior weekend rebuilt from KOBIS agrees with the 9 November press report to within ten admissions for every film that could be compared.
