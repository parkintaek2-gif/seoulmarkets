ENA's Monday-Tuesday drama ***Doctor of Love*** aired episode 11 on **9 November**. TenAsia reports Nielsen Korea put it at **2.9%** nationwide among households, the highest since the premiere, and Sports Kyunghyang gives the same figure. One episode remains. None of the reports we checked gives earlier ratings, so we cannot say how large the rise is.

Park Min-jae (**Chu Young-woo**) lost a leg to osteosarcoma. He broke up with Im Yoo-jin (**Kim So-hyun**) and has been struggling since. TenAsia notes the two leads are both born in 1999 and acted together for the first time in this drama.

## Deleting the photos

Park deleted photos of the two of them from his phone one by one, trying to accept the breakup. Stress took its toll. At a welcome-party after-gathering with lab colleagues he complained of chest pain, coughed up blood, collapsed and was taken to the emergency room.

Meanwhile Lee Ji-hoon (Jang In-seop) confessed to Im and gave her a bracelet he had picked out. She declined, saying she likes someone else. He accepted it as the answer he expected and advised her not to force herself to forget if the feelings remained. Back home, Im could not sleep looking at their photos.

## Hospital and Canada

Park was told stress-related peptic ulcer might be the cause. Hong Eun-ju (Shim Hye-jin) asked his lab colleagues what had happened. When told Park was stressed over a problem involving Kim Se-jin (Im Jin-hyo), she told Kim not to see Park again. Kim then revealed that Park and Im had dated and broken up, and that the breakup was hurting him.

Im went to the hospital too, but family requests restricted visits and she could not see him. The next day she cooked porridge and went back, only to learn he had been discharged. At his home she heard something she did not expect: Park planned to quit school and his robotics research and return to Canada because he could not face her.

## Park's confession

When Im asked whether he was threatening her, Park explained why he had chosen the breakup. TenAsia quotes him:

> Park Min-jae: "Being with you, I was happy and terrified. Afraid of being abandoned, afraid of getting hurt, I always kept my guard up and made excuses to run."

He went on, in tears:

> Park Min-jae: "Because I didn't want to get hurt, I let you die. You loved me, and I didn't."

Im said he had loved her a lot too, and held him. Whether they end the breakup is left to the finale.

## Finale

TenAsia says the finale airs at 10 pm on 10 November, on ENA and also on KT Genie TV and Disney+. The finale's rating and ending are not in this article.

Ratings here are Nielsen Korea figures as reported by news outlets. Park's two lines come from the TenAsia article alone; News1 uses the first sentence as its headline. We did not watch the broadcast ourselves.
