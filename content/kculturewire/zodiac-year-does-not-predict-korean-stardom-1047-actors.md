Here is a question people actually type into a search box: which Korean stars share my zodiac year?
It is answerable, so we answered it — and then we checked whether the answer means anything.

## The count

1,047 Korean actors who appeared in a title that reached a Netflix weekly top 10 have a date of
birth recorded on Wikidata. Sorted by Chinese zodiac year:

| Sign | Actors |
|---|---|
| Dog | 103 |
| Rooster | 92 |
| Pig | 92 |
| Goat | 91 |
| Monkey | 91 |
| Dragon | 90 |
| Rat | 89 |
| Rabbit | 86 |
| Ox | 82 |
| Tiger | 78 |
| Horse | 77 |
| Snake | 76 |

Dog years include Bae Suzy, Hyun Bin, Rain, Park Ji-hoon and Ju Ji-hoon. Snake years include Kim
Woo-bin, So Ji-sub, Lee Jong-suk, Shin Hae-sun and Jung So-min.

## The part most of these lists leave out

Dog has 27 more people than Snake. On a page that stopped here, that gap would read as a finding —
the sign that produces stars, the sign that doesn't.

It isn't one. A chi-square test on this distribution returns **7.77 against a threshold of 19.68**.
In plain terms: if you handed 1,047 people out across twelve boxes completely at random, you would
expect a spread about this uneven, routinely. Dog leading Snake by 27 is what randomness looks
like, not what a pattern looks like.

Being born in one year rather than another does not pick out who reaches a chart. We measured it
precisely so we could say that instead of implying the opposite by staying quiet.

## What we cannot do, and will not fake

A proper saju needs four pillars, and the fourth is the hour of birth. Public profiles carry dates
and almost never hours. That means we can count how a thousand actors are distributed across twelve
years, and we cannot produce any one of their charts — not Suzy's, not Kim Woo-bin's, not anyone's.
Approximating the missing pillar would produce a reading that looks complete and is not, so there
isn't one here.

Two smaller limits, stated rather than buried. A sign assigned from the birth year alone is wrong
for anyone born in January or February, because the lunar new year falls there; those people are
dropped from the source data rather than guessed at. And 229 of the 1,047 have no readable pageview
figures across the editions we track — they are unmeasured, which is not the same as zero.

## So what is the list for

It answers the question that was asked — which stars share your year — and it declines to answer
the question that wasn't. If you were born in a Snake year, you share it with Kim Woo-bin and So
Ji-sub. That is a fact about a calendar. It is not a fact about you.

*Wikidata dates of birth, resolved 10 August 2026. Reads are each person's own Wikipedia article
across the Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai and Malay editions, August 2025 to July 2026, human traffic
only, normalised per million reads of each edition. This is a count, not a reading.*
