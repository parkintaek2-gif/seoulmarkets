MBN's Wednesday-Thursday drama ***Boss's Explicit Taste*** aired its first episode on **11 November** at 10:20 pm. TenAsia reports a Nielsen Korea nationwide rating of **1.3%**. The Seoul-area figure is not in that report and is not given here. The 1.3% comes from one source only, and the Nielsen Korea data itself was not opened.

The drama is a romance between a perfectionist boss, Kwon Do-hyuk (**Sung Hoon**), and a spirited job seeker, Lee Eun-chae (**Oh Se-young**), who clash over everything. **Lee Chung-joo** plays Eun-chae's brother-in-law, Seo Jin-woo. This account rests on TenAsia's report and on frames MBN supplied.

## A clinic mix-up

The two meet in a consulting room. Eun-chae is wearing her brother-in-law's doctor's coat and seeing patients. Do-hyuk, sitting there as a patient, catches her impersonating a doctor. He pretends not to notice and answers with an absurd confession that leaves her flustered.

## An interview, then a search

Eun-chae meets Do-hyuk again at a Saehun Group job interview, and this time he is the interviewer. She runs out of the room.

The plot then moves to the entertainment company Do-hyuk runs. While hunting for an indie-band vocalist known as "Su", whose face resembles the company's AI singer, he learns that Su is Eun-chae. He follows her around and his suspicion grows. Seo Jin-woo tells him she works for Taeyoon Group, but he finds out she is a contract worker at the Taeyoon Home Shopping call centre.

The frames MBN supplied show a doll with orange hair standing among band members, and Do-hyuk studying an album case.

## The last scene

Do-hyuk buys up every copy of Su's CD. He hints at legal action against Seo Jin-woo and smiles. That is where episode 1 ends: the two things Eun-chae was hiding, the clinic mix-up and the stage face, are now in his hands.

Episode 2 airs on Wednesday 12 November at 10:20 pm.
