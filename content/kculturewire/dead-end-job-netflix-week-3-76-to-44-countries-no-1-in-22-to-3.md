Dead-End Job spent its third week on Netflix's lists with fewer countries, a lower rank and less than half the views of the week before. In the week ending 15 November 2026 it was No. 4 on the global non-English TV chart, with 18.7 million hours viewed and 3.7 million views. A week earlier it was No. 1, with 44.4 million hours and 8.7 million views.

## Three weeks in numbers

| Week ending | Countries on the TV top 10 | No. 1 in | Global non-English TV rank | Hours viewed | Views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 November | 51 | 1 | 4 | 25.4 million | 5.0 million |
| 8 November | 76 | 22 | 1 | 44.4 million | 8.7 million |
| 15 November | 44 | 3 | 4 | 18.7 million | 3.7 million |

The series entered the lists on 1 November in 51 countries, which is more than half of the 93 that have a TV list. In its second week it reached 76 countries and led in 22 of them. In the third week it was in 44 countries and led in three.

Views in the third week were 43% of the second week's. Views are Netflix's own estimate, hours viewed divided by the running time of 5.1 hours, so the two measures move together here.

## Korea itself

In South Korea the pattern is the same but shallower. Dead-End Job was No. 1 in the week ending 8 November and No. 3 in the week ending 15 November. It has been in the Korean top 10 in each of its three weeks.

## What took No. 1 in Korea

The Devil's Plan season 3, a Netflix reality-competition series, opened at No. 1 in South Korea and in Hong Kong in the week ending 15 November. It reached only five countries: Hong Kong 1, South Korea 1, Singapore 3, Taiwan 6 and Thailand 8. On the global non-English TV chart it was No. 9, with 12.9 million hours and 2.0 million views.

The two series reached very different spreads. Dead-End Job is in 44 countries, from Argentina and Bolivia to Saudi Arabia and Vietnam, and The Devil's Plan season 3 is in five countries, all in Asia.

## What this does not say

Netflix publishes each country's top 10, not total viewing. A country where Dead-End Job is outside the top 10 may still have viewers, and it is not counted here. Three weeks is a short trend: a drop after a week at No. 1 is common and does not say how a series ends. The lists do not say where a series was made.
