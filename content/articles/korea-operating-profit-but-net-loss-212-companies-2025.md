Operating income is what a company earns from its business before interest, one-off gains and losses, and tax. Net income is what is left at the end. They usually share a sign. In 2025, for **212 of 1,670** Korean listed companies with an operating profit, they did not.

## The count

| Group | Operating profit, net loss | Share of operating-profit companies |
|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 79 of 663 | 11.9% |
| KOSDAQ | 130 of 1,000 | 13.0% |
| All measured | 212 of 1,670 | 12.7% |

The median such company lost **1.57 times** its operating profit at the bottom line.

## The biggest by market cap

| Company | Market cap (12 Nov 2026) | Operating income | Net income |
|---|---|---|---|
| SK Innovation | 18.2 trillion won | 448.7 billion | -5,436.4 billion |
| LG Chem | 17.0 trillion | 1,180.9 billion | -977.1 billion |
| EcoPro | 9.8 trillion | 213.8 billion | -105.5 billion |
| HYBE | 6.7 trillion | 49.3 billion | -254.4 billion |
| Simmtech | 4.8 trillion | 11.9 billion | -164.6 billion |

## The mirror case

The gap also runs the other way. **126 of 855** companies with an operating loss reported a net profit: 28 of 143 on KOSPI (19.6%) and 98 of 707 on KOSDAQ (13.9%).

The sign of operating income alone did not predict the sign of net income for roughly one company in eight to one in seven, on either side.
