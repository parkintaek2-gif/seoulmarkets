Across 2,680 Korean listed companies with a market value on 24 November 2026, age and size line up in an uneven way. The **304 companies incorporated before 1970** are 11.3% of the list but **56.0%** of its market value: 2,424.7 trillion won of 4,332.96 trillion. The **1,313 incorporated since 2000** are 49.0% of the list and **24.4%** of the value.

The share of 2025 net income follows the share of value closely: companies founded before 1970 reported 61.5% of the 238.42 trillion won earned by those with a profit figure, against 20.8% for those founded since 2000.

## Two names carry the older end

SK hynix (dated 1949 in the DART record) and Samsung Electronics (1969) are together 44.4% of the whole market's value. Remove them and the picture narrows but does not reverse: companies founded before 1970 hold 20.7% of the remaining value, and those founded since 2000 hold 44.0%. Most of the older group's weight sits in a handful of very large companies, not in the 302 others.

Going the other way, the 2020-or-later group looks large only because of two reorganised companies. SK Square and LG Energy Solution account for 167.9 trillion won of that group's 195.7 trillion.

## Two boards, two age profiles

The median KOSPI company was incorporated in 1978. The median KOSDAQ company was incorporated in 2000, and 995 of its 1,752 companies (56.8%) date from 2000 or later. On the KOSPI, 260 of 824 companies (31.6%) pre-date 1970 and account for 61.1% of the board's value; 236 (28.6%) date from 2000 or later and account for 21.3%.

On the KOSDAQ, 472 companies (26.9%) were founded since 2010 and hold 29.4% of the board's value. Its 326 companies founded before 1990 hold 14.9%.

## Loss rates by decade

Among companies with a 2025 net income figure, the share reporting a loss was lowest for those founded in the 1960s (35 of 154, 22.7%) and highest for those founded in the 2010s (258 of 498, 51.8%). The 1990s and 2000s groups sit at 36.7% and 42.5%, and the 2020-or-later group at 25.5% (24 of 94). These are counts of 2025 results only and say nothing about what any group will earn.

## What this does not show

A founding date is a legal fact about incorporation. It does not tell us how long a business has operated, and a reorganisation can reset it. Market value is one day's price set by expectations, so a different day would give different shares. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell any security.
