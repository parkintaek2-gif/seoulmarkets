The KOSPI closed on Monday 23 November 2026 at 4,708.54, down 3.31%. The headline number says little about how many parts of the market fell with it. The exchange publishes 51 KOSPI-series indices that are measured prices rather than computed return products: size groups, industries and the KOSPI 200 sub-groups. **All 51 fell.** None rose and none was unchanged.

## The KOSPI series: a median fall of 3.36%

- 41 of the 51 fell 3% or more.
- 7 fell 5% or more.
- The median change was -3.36%, almost exactly the headline.

The seven largest falls:

| Index | Change | Companies |
|---|---|---|
| IT Services | -8.48% | 25 |
| Construction | -6.91% | 28 |
| KOSPI 200 Construction | -6.52% | 10 |
| KOSPI 200 Communication Services | -5.83% | 10 |
| General Services | -5.47% | 32 |
| KOSPI 200 Healthcare | -5.22% | 14 |
| Insurance | -5.12% | 11 |

The smallest falls were Medical and Precision Instruments (-0.70%, eight companies), KOSPI 200 Consumer Staples (-1.29%), KOSPI 200 Information Technology (-2.17%), Food, Beverage and Tobacco (-2.34%) and Securities (-2.55%).

By size, the fall was spread evenly. KOSPI Large Cap lost 3.32%, Mid Cap 3.39% and Small Cap 2.92%. The KOSPI 200 lost 3.24%; the part of the KOSPI outside it lost 2.83%. The KOSPI 200 Top 10 index, which tracks the ten largest companies, fell 3.04%.

## KOSDAQ: a shallower fall, with one exception

The KOSDAQ index closed at 635.45, down 1.58%. Of its 39 series indices, 38 fell and one rose: **KOSDAQ 150 Consumer Staples, up 1.35%**, which holds six companies. The median change was -1.65% and only two fell 3% or more: Publishing and Media Reproduction (-4.03%, two companies) and Financials (-3.18%, 103 companies).

By size the KOSDAQ pattern was the reverse of the one in the main market. Large Cap fell 1.32%, Mid Cap 1.55% and Small Cap 2.30%. KOSDAQ 150, the benchmark of the largest names, fell 1.33%.

## Reading the two together

The KOSPI series fell about twice as hard as the KOSDAQ series at the median (3.36% against 1.65%). Within the KOSPI series, a fall of 3% or more was the rule, not the exception: 41 of 51, or 80%. Within the KOSDAQ series it applied to 2 of 39, or 5%.

Indices with few constituents move on one or two companies, so we do not read Medical and Precision Instruments, Publishing or KOSDAQ 150 Consumer Staples as evidence about an industry. The larger groups, such as Construction (28 companies), IT Services (25) and General Services (32), carry more weight in the comparison.

## What this does not show

One session cannot say whether the fall continues or reverses. The exchange records give prices and counts but not reasons, and we draw no cause from them. This is a count of what fell on 23 November and by how much, and not advice to buy or sell.
