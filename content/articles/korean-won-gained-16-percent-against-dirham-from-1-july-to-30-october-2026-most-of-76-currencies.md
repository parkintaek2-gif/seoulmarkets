The UAE central bank publishes a daily table of exchange rates against the dirham. The dirham is quoted at **3.6725 to the dollar** on every day in the file. So a currency's rate against the dirham is also a rate against the dollar, scaled.

On that table the Korean won gained more than any other of the 76 currencies that have a quote on both 1 July and 30 October 2026. It rose from 0.002363 dirhams per won to 0.002754, a gain of **16.5%**.

## In won per dollar

Dividing 3.6725 by the won's rate gives the won per dollar the table implies:

- 1 July 2026: **1,554**, the weakest value of the won in the file, which starts in January 2018
- 30 October 2026: **1,334**, or 14.2% fewer won per dollar

The gain is 16.5% in dirham terms and 14.2% in won per dollar because they are the same move measured from two sides. The level agrees with what we have used elsewhere: the dollar-won rate in our 21 November piece was 1,333.78 on 13 November and 1,334.79 on 20 November.

## How big were the daily moves

The 2026 file has 217 daily quotes for the won, which gives 216 day-to-day changes. The won moved by 1% or more on **23** of them, and on 14 of those it was the stronger direction. The largest was on 30 July, when it strengthened 2.17%. Other days above 1.5% in the stronger direction were 8 April (1.73%), 19 August (1.63%), 26 January (1.61%) and 8 June (1.58%).

## What this does not show

The figures are derived from a table in the UAE, not from a Korean source. The table does not say what time of day each rate is taken, and we did not compare it with Seoul closes day by day. The window ends on 30 October. We do not know why the won strengthened, and four months of one table do not show what will happen next. Nothing here is advice on currencies.
