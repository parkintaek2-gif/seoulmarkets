The 1kg gold bar on the Korea Exchange closed at **153,090 won a gram** on Friday 20 November 2026. That is the lowest close in the 97 sessions we hold, which start on 1 July, and it is **26.5% below** the **208,300** the bar closed at on 25 August. Measured from the first session, 1 July (197,190), the fall is 22.4%.

The last session was the sharpest of the window. The bar fell **4.17%** on the day, on 631,507 grams of volume.

## The path from 208,300 to 153,090

The price rose before it fell. From 197,190 on 1 July it climbed 5.6% to the 25 August peak, then lost ground in every month after. The month-end closes were 187,460 (July), 196,480 (August), 182,940 (September), 171,020 (October) and 153,090 (20 November).

| Month | Sessions | Highest close | Lowest close | Last close |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| July | 22 | 204,340 | 185,990 | 187,460 |
| August | 20 | 208,300 | 186,430 | 196,480 |
| September | 20 | 196,280 | 181,460 | 182,940 |
| October | 20 | 183,480 | 170,390 | 171,020 |
| November (to 20th) | 15 | 178,770 | 153,090 | 153,090 |

November's highest close, 178,770 on 9 November, is below September's lowest, 181,460. The fall was not a straight line: on 4 and 5 November the bar rose 2.90% and 2.64% in two sessions, to 178,460. From that close to 20 November it lost 14.2%.

## Most days were falls, but the big moves came in clusters

Of the 97 sessions, **58** closed lower, 38 higher and one unchanged. The bar moved by 2% or more, in either direction, on 17 sessions, and fell by 2% or more on 11 of them. Three of those 11 came in November, on the 12th (2.71%), the 18th (2.86%) and the 20th (4.17%).

The five largest one-day falls were:

- 20 November: down 4.17%, to 153,090
- 31 August: down 3.22%, to 196,480
- 28 September: down 2.93%, to 183,940
- 18 November: down 2.86%, to 162,090
- 2 September: down 2.79%, to 190,810

The largest rises were 3.09% on 20 August, 2.90% on 4 November and 2.72% on 2 July. Only three sessions in the window moved by 3% or more, and 20 November was one of them.

The last four sessions were all falls except one. From the 17 November close of 166,860 to the 20 November close, the bar lost **8.25%** in three sessions. Earlier, from 19 to 27 October, the bar fell on seven sessions in a row, though each fall was small (between 0.01% and 1.74%).

## Volume: 20 November was the heaviest session

Volume is quoted in grams. On 20 November **631,507 grams**, about 632 kg, changed hands in the 1kg bar. The median session in the window was 181,402 grams, so the last day traded **3.5 times** the usual amount. It is the highest volume of the 97 sessions; the next two, 551,947 grams on 25 August and 528,724 on 24 August, were the two days around the peak.

That makes the window's two heaviest-volume episodes the peak and the latest low. Traded value tells a similar story: 97.1 billion won on 20 November was the third largest, behind 115.0 billion on 25 August and 109.0 billion on 24 August.

Average traded value per session fell as the price fell: 42.7 billion won in July, 49.5 billion in August, 37.4 billion in September, 26.8 billion in October and 29.7 billion in the 15 sessions of November. So trading was thinnest in October, when the price ended the month 17.9% below its peak, and picked up again in November as the price fell faster.

## What this does not show

The window starts on 1 July, so we cannot say how 153,090 compares with earlier years. The record does not tell us why the price moved, and we have not compared it with the international price or the won. These are the exchange's own closes for one product, the 99.99% 1kg bar; retail prices at banks and jewellers are a different market. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell.
