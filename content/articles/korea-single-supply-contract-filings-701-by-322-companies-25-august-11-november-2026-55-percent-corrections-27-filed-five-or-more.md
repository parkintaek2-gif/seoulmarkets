Between 25 August and 11 November 2026, **322 listed companies filed 701 single sales or supply contract disclosures** with the Financial Supervisory Service. That is 26.5% of the 2,642 material disclosures in our feed for the period, the largest of the 19 labels in the feed (the next, major shareholder reports, has 465). Of the 701 filings, **382 (54.5%)** are marked as corrections.

## KOSPI filers file more often

On the KOSPI, 112 of 830 companies (13.5%) filed at least once, 317 filings in all, or 38.2 per 100 companies. On the KOSDAQ, 209 of 1,772 (11.8%) filed, 383 filings, or 21.6 per 100. A KOSPI filer averaged 2.8 filings in the window and a KOSDAQ filer 1.8. One KONEX company filed once.

Half of the companies (155 of 322, 48.1%) filed once. The 27 that filed five times or more account for 24.8% of all filings. The most frequent were Sphere Corp. with 11, Samho Development and Dong-A Geological Engineering with 10 each, Kumho Engineering & Construction with 9 and Daewoo Engineering & Construction with 8.

## Construction is where the repeats are

Under the tape's industry classification, General Construction has 41 companies and 31 of them filed, with 122 filings. Specialty Trade Construction has 19 companies and 11 filed, with 49 filings. Together, 42 construction companies made 171 filings, 24.4% of the total. The next largest groups by filings were Other Machinery and Equipment (124 filings from 62 of 218 companies) and Electronic Components, Computers and Communication Equipment (53 filings from 31 of 305).

## Corrections and timing

The 382 amendments are not evenly spread: 110 companies (34.2%) filed only amendments in the window, which means the original disclosure was dated earlier. Filings by month were 135 from 25 to 31 August, 278 in September, 277 in October and 11 from 1 to 11 November. By weekday, Wednesday had 193 filings (27.5%), against 119 on Monday, 138 on Tuesday, 140 on Thursday and 111 on Friday. We did not look for the reason.

## What this does not show

These are counts of filings, and the feed does not carry contract amounts, so a large number of filings is not a large amount of business. Because corrections, new contracts and repeat orders are all separate rows, a company with 10 filings may have one contract or ten. Nothing here is a view on any company or an investment recommendation.
