Our piece of 23 November counted the premium of the 100g gold bar over the 1kg bar on the Korea Exchange for 66 sessions, to 7 October. The median was **0.06%**. We now hold 97 sessions, to 20 November, and the 31 added sessions look different from the first 66.

## Two periods, two medians

Both bars trade on the exchange's gold market in 99.99% purity. The premium here is the 100g close divided by the 1kg close on the same day, minus one.

| Period | Sessions | Median premium | Within 0.1% of the 1kg price | 100g bar cheaper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 July to 7 October | 66 | 0.06% | 35 | 24 |
| 8 October to 20 November | 31 | 0.36% | 2 | 2 |
| All | 97 | 0.21% | 37 | 26 |

After 7 October the 100g bar closed more than 0.1% above the 1kg bar on 28 of the 31 sessions. It closed below it on only two, 4 November (0.21% below) and 13 November (0.01% below).

Month by month, the median premium was -0.02% in July, 0.05% in August, 0.32% in September, 0.36% in October and 0.28% in the 15 sessions of November. The step up comes in early September, not on 8 October, which is only where our first count stopped.

## The widest day is now 11 November

On **11 November** the 100g bar closed at 176,900 won a gram and the 1kg bar at 174,890, a premium of **1.15%**. That is wider than the 1.05% of 9 September, the previous widest. The five widest days across the 97 sessions are:

- 11 November: 1.15%
- 9 September: 1.05%
- 3 September: 0.88%
- 7 September: 0.64%
- 11 September: 0.62%

Ten sessions had a premium above 0.5% and two were above 1%. The narrowest day is still 21 July, when the 100g bar closed 0.29% below.

The 11 November gap came on a quiet day for the big bar. The 1kg bar fell 1.35% on 93,373 grams, about half its median of 181,402, while the 100g bar fell 0.56% on 6,580 grams. A day later the 1kg bar fell 2.71% and the 100g bar 3.34%, and the premium dropped to 0.50%.

## The two bars sometimes move in opposite directions

On eight sessions of the 97 the two bars' daily changes had opposite signs, and on two more one bar was unchanged while the other moved. Five of the eight fell between 1 and 10 September, one on 13 August, and two in the added period, on 29 October (1kg down 0.11%, 100g up 0.18%) and 3 November (1kg down 0.04%, 100g up 0.06%).

## A small market next to a big one

Trading in the 100g bar is small. Its median volume was 9,379 grams a session against 181,402 for the 1kg bar, so the 100g bar's volume on the median day is about **5.1%** of the big bar's, taking the median of the daily ratio. The 100g bar's busiest day was 12 August, at 40,661 grams. On 11 November, the day of the 1.15% premium, only 6,580 grams of the 100g bar changed hands.

## What this does not show

The data end on 20 November. We do not know why the premium has been higher since September, or what it will do next. Our closes come from one archive of exchange quotes and were not checked against a second source. This piece says nothing about fees or taxes on either bar, and nothing in it is advice to buy or sell.
