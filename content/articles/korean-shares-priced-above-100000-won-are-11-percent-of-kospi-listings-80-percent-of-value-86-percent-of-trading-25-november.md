A Korean share's price in won says little about the size of the company, but in the KOSPI it lines up with weight. On 25 November, **106** of the 935 KOSPI listings closed above 100,000 won. They are 11.3% of listings, **79.7%** of market value and **86.0%** of trading value.

## The KOSPI by price band

| Closing price (won) | Listings | Share of listings | Share of value | Share of trading | Median change on the day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 or less | 42 | 4.5% | 0.1% | 0.0% | -1.45% |
| 1,000 to 5,000 | 284 | 30.4% | 1.1% | 0.7% | -0.90% |
| 5,000 to 10,000 | 166 | 17.8% | 1.3% | 1.3% | -1.07% |
| 10,000 to 50,000 | 264 | 28.2% | 10.3% | 5.9% | -0.69% |
| 50,000 to 100,000 | 73 | 7.8% | 7.5% | 6.1% | -1.04% |
| Above 100,000 | 106 | 11.3% | 79.7% | 86.0% | -0.38% |

The median KOSPI price is 8,960 won. More than half the listings, 492 of 935, close at 10,000 won or less, and between them they hold 2.5% of the market's value. The 106 above 100,000 won include Samsung Electronics at 170,700 won and SK hynix at 1,030,000 won, which together are 44.9% of the KOSPI by value. They are in this band, so part of the 79.7% is those two.

The KOSDAQ median price is 3,450 won. Over half its listings, 55.7%, close between 1,000 and 5,000 won, yet hold 14.9% of value. Nineteen shares above 100,000 won hold 17.6% and trade 20.1%. The 10,000 to 50,000 won band has the most value and trading, at 37.5% and 45.1%.

## The difference between the two markets

On the KOSPI the top price band dominates both value and trading. On the KOSDAQ it does not: the middle bands carry most of both. That is a count of where the won sit in each market on one day, not a reason to prefer either.

## The highest prices

The three highest closes on 25 November were Hyosung Heavy Industries at 1,608,000 won, Samsung Biologics at 1,363,000 won and Samyang Foods at 1,309,000 won, all on the KOSPI. A high price does not by itself mean a large company; it can also mean the company has issued relatively few shares.

## The day's move by band

The median change on 25 November was smaller in the top KOSPI band, minus 0.38%, than in the others, which ranged from minus 0.69% to minus 1.45%. The index fell 6.16%, and the median share in that band fell far less than the index. That fits the picture in our other articles today, that a few large, high-priced listings carried the index.

## What this does not show

A share price alone says nothing about whether a company is large, cheap or expensive; it reflects how many shares the company issued. Splits, par values and issuance choices differ across companies and we have not adjusted for them. The table is one day's close and is not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
