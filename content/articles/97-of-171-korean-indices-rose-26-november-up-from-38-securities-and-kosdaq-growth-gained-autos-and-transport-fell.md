On Thursday 26 November, **97** of the 171 Korean indices in our file rose, 73 fell and one was unchanged. On 25 November, when the KOSPI fell 6.16%, only 38 rose.

## Where the gains and losses sat

| Index family, 26 November | Indices | Rose | Fell |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI Series | 53 | 27 | 26 |
| KOSDAQ Series | 39 | 32 | 7 |
| KRX Series | 40 | 23 | 16 |
| Thematic | 39 | 15 | 24 |
| All | 171 | 97 | 73 |

One KRX Series index closed unchanged. KOSDAQ indices were the most broadly higher, 32 of 39, while thematic indices were the weakest, 15 of 39.

## Highest and lowest

Leaving aside three strategy indices with no constituent count, the biggest rises were in KOSDAQ-linked groups: the KRX K-AI Biotech KOSDAQ index rose 3.87%, KOSDAQ General Services 3.17% and KOSDAQ Technology Growth Companies 3.12%. Securities also gained, with the KOSPI Securities index up 2.60% and KRX Securities up 2.12%.

The biggest falls were in instruments, autos and transport. The KOSPI Medical and Precision Instruments index fell 3.57%, but it has only 8 constituents. KRX Autos fell 2.94%, KOSPI Transport Equipment and Parts 2.72%, KOSPI 200 Consumer Discretionary 2.66% and KRX Transport 2.59%.

An index with the same sector name in the KOSPI and the KOSDAQ holds different companies. On 26 November the two sides of all four names moved in opposite directions. The 60 constituents of the KOSPI transport index and the 77 of the KOSDAQ one are not the same companies, so the table compares market segments, not one sector.
