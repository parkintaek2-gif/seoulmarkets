On Thursday 26 November the KOSPI rose 0.29%, and three carmaker-group shares fell sharply. **Hyundai Mobis** closed at 329,500 won, down **5.72%**, **Hyundai Motor** at 259,000 won, down **3.90%**, and **Kia** at 93,700 won, down **2.40%**.

All seven fell on the day. Hyundai Mobis fell more than any other of the 20 largest KOSPI listings, and Hyundai Motor and Kia ranked second and third.

## The size of the loss

Hyundai Motor, Kia and Hyundai Mobis hold 118.9 trillion won of market value, **3.1%** of the KOSPI. Together they lost **4.84 trillion won** on 26 November: Hyundai Motor 2.14 trillion, Hyundai Mobis 1.80 trillion and Kia 0.90 trillion. In the same session the KOSPI's total value rose 12.63 trillion, driven by one chipmaker. So a group that is 3.1% of the market took out an amount equal to 38% of the net gain, which shows the weighting, not the cause.

## The index view

The KRX Autos index, with 18 constituents, fell 2.94%. The KOSPI Transport Equipment and Parts index (60 constituents) fell 2.72% and KOSPI 200 Consumer Discretionary (25) fell 2.66%. The KOSDAQ index of the same name, with 77 constituents, rose 0.80%, so the fall was a KOSPI feature on this day.
