The KOSPI ended the week of 16 to 20 November 2026 at 4,869.96, down 7.95% from the 5,290.53 it closed at on Friday 13 November. That is a loss of 420.57 points in five sessions. The KOSDAQ closed the week at 645.68, down 7.72% from 699.66.

This piece puts the week among the 26 weeks since late May, using each week's last close.

## The five days

The week was one big fall at the start, a partial recovery in the middle and one big fall at the end:

- Monday 16 November: down 3.59%, to 5,100.50
- Tuesday 17 November: down 2.59%, to 4,968.48
- Wednesday 18 November: up 1.99%, to 5,067.44
- Thursday 19 November: up 1.39%, to 5,137.89
- Friday 20 November: down 5.21%, to 4,869.96

Two sessions rose and added up to about 3.4%. The Friday fall alone took back more than those two rises. The 18 and 19 November closes come from the Yahoo series and were not checked against an exchange report.

## Where the week ranks

Over the 26 weeks ending 29 May to 20 November, the KOSPI's weekly change was lower than this week's in three weeks:

- Week to 16 October: down 14.46%, to 5,668.05
- Week to 6 November: down 11.71%, to 5,059.69
- Week to 16 July: down 8.77%, to 6,820.60

This week's 7.95% fall is the fourth-worst. The next two are 7.57% in the week to 10 July and 7.08% in the week to 26 June. The week to 16 July had four sessions, not five, so it is not a like-for-like week.

The KOSDAQ ranks the same way. Its 7.72% fall is the fourth-worst of the 26 weeks, behind 11.92% (week to 26 June), 11.06% (week to 6 November) and 10.93% (week to 16 October).

## Four of the last seven weeks

Since the week to 8 October, which had three sessions, the KOSPI has had seven weekly closes. Five of them were down:

- Week to 8 October: down 5.39%
- Week to 16 October: down 14.46%
- Week to 23 October: down 2.08%
- Week to 30 October: up 3.25%
- Week to 6 November: down 11.71%
- Week to 13 November: up 4.56%
- Week to 20 November: down 7.95%

Four of those seven fell by 5% or more. In the 19 weeks before them, going back to the week to 29 May, four did. The two rises, 3.25% and 4.56%, were each smaller than the fall that came right after or before them.

## Since 2 October

The KOSPI closed the week to 2 October at 7,003.74. The 20 November close of 4,869.96 is 30.5% lower, a loss of 2,133.78 points in seven weeks. A holder of the index from 2 October would need a rise of about 43.8% to get back to 7,003.74.

## What this does not say

This is a count of weeks in a 26-week window, not a forecast. The reports we read tie parts of the week to selling in US technology shares, but they do not agree on the details and we have not checked them, so we do not state a cause. Most of the Friday closes rest on a single data source, noted above. Nothing here is a reason to buy or sell anything. It is a statistic about an index, not about any one investor.
