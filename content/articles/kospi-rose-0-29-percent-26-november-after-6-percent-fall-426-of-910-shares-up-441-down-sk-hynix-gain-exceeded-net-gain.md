On Thursday 26 November the KOSPI closed at **4,711.21**, up **0.29%**, or 13.70 points. The day before it had fallen 6.16%. The index barely moved, and so did the typical share: the median change among 910 traded KOSPI listings was **0.00%**.

## A split market

| 26 November | KOSPI | KOSDAQ |
|---|---|---|
| Index change | +0.29% | +0.78% |
| Traded listings | 910 | 1,748 |
| Rose | 426 | 900 |
| Fell | 441 | 750 |
| Unchanged | 43 | 98 |
| Median change | 0.00% | +0.14% |

More KOSPI shares fell than rose, 441 against 426, yet the index rose. On 25 November the count was 295 up and 594 down, so 131 more shares finished higher on 26 November than the day before. Of the 910, 434 moved less than 1% in either direction, 238 rose 1% or more and 238 fell 1% or more.

## One company larger than the net gain

KOSPI market value rose from 3,847.7 trillion won to 3,860.3 trillion, a net gain of **12.63 trillion won**. SK hynix, up 2.23% to 1,053,000 won, added 16.26 trillion won on its own, which is 129% of the net gain. Taken together, the other 934 listings lost 3.62 trillion won. Samsung Electronics, up 0.35% to 171,300 won, is included in those 934 and added 3.51 trillion.

That is how an index can rise while 441 shares fall: the index is weighted by market value, and one share is 19.3% of that value. On this day it was SK hynix.

## The biggest companies pointed both ways

Among the 20 largest KOSPI listings, 9 rose and 10 fell, and Samsung SDI closed unchanged. The ten largest hold 59.2% of the KOSPI's value, and they do not move together: SK Square rose 2.68% and Samsung Electro-Mechanics 3.84%, while Hyundai Motor fell 3.90% and LG Energy Solution 2.11%.

## The KOSDAQ

The KOSDAQ index rose 0.78%, and 900 of its 1,748 traded listings rose against 750 that fell. Its median change was +0.14%, against +0.78% for the KOSDAQ index and 0.00% for the KOSPI median share.

## What this does not show

The data give prices and counts; they do not say why the market moved or whether it will continue. One day of 0.29% after a 6.16% fall is a count of how shares closed. It is not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
