On Monday 23 November only **71** of the 908 KOSPI listings that traded closed higher. On Tuesday 24 November **797** of 910 did. In the 60 sessions in our stock-by-stock archive, which starts on 27 August, those are the two most extreme days for the share of KOSPI listings rising: 7.8% on the 23rd, the lowest, and 87.6% on the 24th, the highest.

## Two days, side by side

| KOSPI, traded listings | 23 November | 24 November |
|---|---|---|
| Rose | 71 | 797 |
| Fell | 824 | 94 |
| Unchanged | 13 | 19 |
| Not traded | 27 | 25 |
| Share rising | 7.8% | 87.6% |
| Median change | -3.00% | +2.48% |
| Rose 10% or more | 0 | 37 |
| Fell 10% or more | 13 | 2 |

The pattern holds if the count is limited to the 803 traded KOSPI listings whose code ends in 0: 708 of them rose on 24 November and 80 fell. It is not an effect of a few unusual listings.

The second-widest rally in the window was 16 October, with 82.9% rising. The second-narrowest was 20 November, with 9.6%. So the sessions of 20, 23 and 24 November hold the second-narrowest, the narrowest and the widest rally of the 60.

## The index moved more than the median share

The KOSPI index rose 6.32% on 24 November, but the median listing rose 2.48%. The index weights companies by size: Samsung Electronics rose 6.70% and SK hynix 6.73%, and the two were 48.5% of KOSPI company value on 23 November in our earlier piece. On 28 October, when the index rose 6.63%, 63.6% of traded listings rose, so a similar index gain came with a narrower market.

## KOSDAQ

On the KOSDAQ, 1,389 of 1,750 traded listings rose on 24 November (79.4%), the third-highest share in the window. A day earlier 304 of 1,749 had (17.4%). The median KOSDAQ listing moved from -2.29% to +2.37%.

## Value traded did not rise with the rally

The KOSPI traded 15.77 trillion won on 24 November, against 16.06 trillion on the 23rd. Across the 67 index snapshots the median is 20.58 trillion won, and only eight sessions traded less than on 24 November. The widest rally of the window came on a quiet day by this measure. The KOSDAQ traded 5.93 trillion won, close to its median of 6.04 trillion.

## What this does not show

These are counts of listings with a positive or negative close-to-close change. They say nothing about size of position, about who traded, or about intraday paths, and one session is not a pattern. This is a description of two days, not a view on the next one and not advice to buy or sell any share.
