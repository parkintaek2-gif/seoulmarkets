Net profit margin is net income divided by revenue. A margin of 5% means five won of profit after all costs, interest and tax for each hundred won of sales. We read both from each company's fiscal-2025 annual report and spread the companies across bands, instead of quoting an average that no company actually has.

## The middle company

Among **2,487** companies with revenue above zero and a net income figure, the median net margin was **1.9%**. Half the companies earned less than that, and many earned less than nothing.

## The bands

| Net margin | Companies | Share of companies | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| -50% or below | 252 | 10.1% | 1.1% |
| Above -50% to -10% | 321 | 12.9% | 1.2% |
| Above -10% to 0 | 405 | 16.3% | 3.7% |
| Above 0 to 5% | 688 | 27.7% | 12.1% |
| Above 5% to 10% | 357 | 14.4% | 14.6% |
| Above 10% to 20% | 290 | 11.7% | 31.7% |
| Above 20% | 174 | 7.0% | 27.4% |

The right-hand column is each band's share of the whole market's value. The three loss bands together were **978** companies, **39.3%** of those measured, and **6.1%** of market value. The two bands above 10% were **464** companies (18.7%) and **59.1%** of value.

## The extremes

Some ratios sit outside any normal range. **Eight** companies reported net income larger than their revenue, a margin above 100%, which cannot come from sales alone. At the other end, **166** companies lost more than their whole year of sales.

## KOSPI and KOSDAQ

| Market | Companies | Median net margin |
|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 778 | 2.7% |
| KOSDAQ | 1,697 | 1.1% |

KOSPI's median company kept more than twice as much of each won of sales as KOSDAQ's. KONEX has 12 companies with both figures and is not summarised.

## By industry

There are 23 industries with at least 30 companies measured. Among them, the lowest median was Research and Development at **-185.9%**, where 55 of 62 companies had a net loss. Motion Picture, Video, and Audio Production had a median of **-10.6%** (26 of 37 at or below zero), and Medical, Precision, and Optical Instruments **0.4%** (49 of 100).

The highest medians were Information Services at **6.6%** (7 of 33 at or below zero), Financial Services at **6.4%** (19 of 90) and Professional Services at **3.9%** (9 of 30). No industry with this many companies had a median above 7%.

## What this does not say

Net margin is one year's ratio and includes one-off items. A low margin can be a business that moves large volumes cheaply, and a high one can be a single gain. We do not rank industries as better or worse, and we do not say any margin is normal.
