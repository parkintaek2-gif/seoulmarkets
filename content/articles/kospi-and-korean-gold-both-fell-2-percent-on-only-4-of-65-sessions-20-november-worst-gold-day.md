Between 18 August and 20 November 2026 the KOSPI fell from 6,869.83 to 4,869.96, a loss of **29.1%**. Over the same 65 sessions the 1kg gold bar on the Korea Exchange fell from 199,950 to 153,090 won a gram, a loss of **23.4%**. Both lost a lot. Did they lose it on the same days? Mostly they did not.

## Same direction on 35 of 65 sessions

We lined up the exchange's daily percentage change for the KOSPI and for the gold bar.

- Both fell on **23** sessions.
- Both rose on **12** sessions.
- They moved in opposite directions on **29** sessions.
- On 15 September gold was unchanged while the KOSPI fell 0.85%.

So the two moved the same way on 35 sessions and opposite ways on 29, nearly an even split. The correlation of the 65 pairs of daily changes is 0.18, weakly positive. One weak number from 3.5 months says little by itself, so we rely on the day-by-day counts below.

## Four days when both fell by 2% or more

Gold fell by 2% or more on 10 sessions and the KOSPI on 18 sessions. Both did so on the same day only four times:

| Date | KOSPI | Gold 1kg bar |
|---|---|---|
| 19 August | -5.80% | -2.19% |
| 2 September | -3.99% | -2.79% |
| 28 September | -2.70% | -2.93% |
| 20 November | -5.21% | -4.17% |

On the 10 sessions when gold fell by 2% or more, the KOSPI fell on only six. On the other four the KOSPI rose or barely moved: 31 August (+0.46%), 7 September (+4.61%), 22 September (+0.15%) and 18 November (+1.99%). On 7 September gold lost 2.23% on a day the KOSPI gained 4.61%.

## The KOSPI's worst days were not gold's

The KOSPI fell by 5% or more on six sessions. Gold's moves on them were:

- 19 August: -2.19%
- 13 October: +0.63%
- **15 October: -0.07%** (the KOSPI's worst day, down 7.57%)
- 5 November: **+2.64%**
- 6 November: -0.99%
- 20 November: -4.17%

On two of the six, gold rose. On the KOSPI's worst day it moved 0.07%. On 5 November, when the KOSPI fell 5.28%, gold rose 2.64%, one of four rises of 2% or more that gold had in the window. The other three came on 20 August (with the KOSPI up 5.89%), 24 August (KOSPI down 3.12%) and 4 November (KOSPI up 0.62%).

Across the KOSPI's 15 falls of 3% or more, gold fell on 11 and rose on four, and gold's median move on those 15 days was -0.61%.

## 20 November: the two markets fell together

20 November was the exception. The gold bar fell 4.17%, the largest one-day fall of its 65 sessions, and the KOSPI fell 5.21%, the sixth-largest in the window behind 7.57%, 6.02%, 5.80%, 5.46% and 5.28%. It is the only session in the window on which gold fell by more than 3% and the KOSPI by more than 5% on the same day.

The gold bar's volume that day, 631,507 grams, was the highest of the 97 sessions we hold, 3.5 times its median. Our companion piece of the same date on the gold bar gives the monthly figures. In size, the KOSPI moves more on a typical day: the average absolute daily change was 2.30% for the KOSPI and 1.19% for gold.

## What this does not show

The window is 65 sessions, and the figures say nothing about what moved either market. One day of both falling, 20 November, does not show that the two will move together again, and the sessions when they diverged were just as numerous. We did not test the international gold price or the won. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell.
