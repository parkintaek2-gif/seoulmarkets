Operating income is what a company earns from its business before interest and tax. A negative figure means the core business cost more than it brought in. We counted how often that happened in each industry in fiscal 2025.

## The overall count

**855 of 2,525** companies with an operating income figure reported a loss: **33.9%**.

| Market | Companies | Operating loss | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 806 | 143 | 17.7% |
| KOSDAQ | 1,707 | 707 | 41.4% |
| All | 2,525 | 855 | 33.9% |

Together the 855 were worth **4.4%** of market value on 12 November 2026.

## By industry

Of 24 industries with at least 30 measured companies, these were at the two ends.

| Industry | Companies | Operating loss | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research and Development | 55 | 49 | 89.1% |
| Financial and Insurance Related Services | 62 | 38 | 61.3% |
| Motion Picture, Video, and Audio Production | 36 | 22 | 61.1% |
| Medical, Precision, and Optical Instruments | 99 | 46 | 46.5% |
| Other Machinery and Equipment | 210 | 89 | 42.4% |
| Information Services | 33 | 6 | 18.2% |
| Food Products Manufacturing | 73 | 12 | 16.4% |
| Financial Services | 114 | 14 | 12.3% |
| Motor Vehicles and Trailers | 107 | 13 | 12.1% |

The two ends are 7.4 times apart: 89.1% against 12.1%. In the large Electronic Components, Computers, and Communication Equipment group, **110 of 284** (38.7%) reported a loss.

## What this does not say

The classification is the one in the tape, and a company is placed in one industry only. We do not know why any company lost money.
