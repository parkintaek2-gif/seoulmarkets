The KOSPI rose 6.32% on 24 November, but the market had fallen sharply the two sessions before it. Measured from the 19 November close, **748 of 935 KOSPI shares** (80.0%) closed lower on the 24th. Only 158 were higher and 29 were unchanged. On the KOSDAQ the count was 1,369 lower out of 1,835 (74.6%).

## The spread of changes

| Change, 19 to 24 November | KOSPI shares | KOSDAQ shares |
|---|---|---|
| -10% or worse | 39 | 148 |
| -10% to -5% | 202 | 446 |
| -5% to 0% | 507 | 775 |
| 0% to +5% | 159 | 357 |
| +5% to +10% | 22 | 58 |
| +10% or more | 6 | 51 |

On the KOSPI the largest group, 507 shares, lost less than 5%. A total of 241 lost 5% or more. Six gained at least 10%. The median KOSPI share is 2.87% lower and the median KOSDAQ share 2.92% lower. The "0% to +5%" row includes 29 KOSPI and 111 KOSDAQ shares that closed exactly unchanged.

## Weighted by size

The KOSPI listings together were worth 4,215.18 trillion won on 19 November and 4,104.82 trillion on 24 November, a fall of 2.62%. The shares that closed at or above their 19 November level hold 14.1% of the KOSPI listings' value on 24 November and 30.4% of the KOSDAQ listings'.

The KOSDAQ listings were worth 391.37 trillion won on 19 November and 381.23 trillion on 24 November, down 2.59%, almost the same percentage as the KOSPI.

Size mattered little in the count. Of the 50 largest KOSPI listings by value on 24 November, 15 closed at or above their 19 November level (30.0%); of the 200 smallest, 51 did (25.5%).

Among the ten largest KOSPI listings by value, eight are lower. Samsung Electronics is 3.81% lower and SK hynix 2.11% lower. The two that are higher are LG Energy Solution at +4.14% and Hyundai Motor at +2.80%.

## A shorter look

Over only the last two sessions the picture is closer to even. Against the 20 November close, 501 of the 935 KOSPI shares (53.6%) were lower on the 24th, and 434 were at or above it. Measured from the 20th, the board is split about half and half either side of the line, while the longer measure from the 19th leaves four in five below it.

## What this does not show

The files hold closing prices and daily changes. They cannot separate shares that fell for company-specific reasons from those that fell with the market, and they carry no adjustment for dividends or splits. Three sessions are a short window. This is a description of where shares closed on 24 November, not a forecast and not advice to buy or sell any share.
