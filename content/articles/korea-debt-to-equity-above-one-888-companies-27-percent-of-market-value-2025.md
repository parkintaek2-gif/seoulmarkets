Debt-to-equity here is a company's total liabilities divided by its total equity at the end of 2025. At 1.0, the company owes as much as its owners have put in and kept. We read the figure from each annual report.

## The count

**888 of 2,545** companies with positive equity were above 1.0: **34.9%**.

| Market | Companies | Above 1.0 | Share | Median ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 804 | 384 | 47.8% | 0.97 |
| KOSDAQ | 1,732 | 496 | 28.6% | 0.54 |
| All | 2,545 | 888 | 34.9% | not computed |

## Not only banks

Banks and insurers carry liabilities that are mostly deposits and policy reserves, so a high ratio is built in. We set the 114 financial companies aside. Of the remaining **2,431**, **845** (34.8%) were still above 1.0. Finance does not explain the count.

## Count and value

The 888 were worth **1,231.2 trillion won** on 12 November 2026, **27.0%** of the 4,553.1 trillion won for all 2,545. The share of value (27.0%) is lower than the share of companies (34.9%).

| Threshold | Companies | Share of companies | Share of value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Above 1.0 | 888 | 34.9% | 27.0% |
| Above 2.0 | 315 | 12.4% | 12.2% |
| Above 4.0 | 114 | 4.5% | 8.3% |

At the top of the range the picture turns: the 114 companies above 4.0 hold 8.3% of value, nearly twice their 4.5% of the count. The largest by value among the 888 are LG Energy Solution (1.29), Hyundai Motor (1.89), KB Financial Group (12.12), Samsung Life Insurance (4.41) and Shinhan Financial Group (12.02).

## What this does not say

The ratio does not separate borrowings from payables, and it is one year-end. We do not call any level high.
