Market capitalisation is the share price times the number of shares. Summed over a country's listed companies, it gives the total market value. We ranked every Korean listed company by it on one day.

## The top

On 12 November 2026 the 2,683 companies with a market value were worth **4,643.1 trillion won** together.

| Rank | Company | Market cap | Share | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Samsung Electronics | 1,216.0 trillion won | 26.2% | 26.2% |
| 2 | SK hynix | 916.0 trillion | 19.7% | 45.9% |
| 3 | SK Square | 104.4 trillion | 2.2% | 48.2% |
| 4 | LG Energy Solution | 77.1 trillion | 1.7% | 49.8% |
| 5 | Samsung Biologics | 67.1 trillion | 1.4% | 51.3% |

**Five companies** held more than half of the value. The top 10 held 57.1%, the top 50 held 76.0%, and the top 100 held 84.1%.

## The bottom

The 1,000th company by value was worth **145.9 billion won**, and the top 1,000 together held 98.0%. The other 1,683 companies shared the last 2.0%.

**1,449 companies** were worth less than 100 billion won each, and together they made up **1.3%** of the total. The median company, in the middle of the ranking, was worth **85.6 billion won**.

## What this does not say

A count of companies and a count of won answer different questions. Both are true, and the tape gives no reason to prefer one. We did not adjust for the shares that actually trade, and we did not compare with any other market.
