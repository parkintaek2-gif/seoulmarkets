On Monday 23 November 2026, the day the KOSPI fell 3.31% and the KOSDAQ 1.58%, the two boards traded very different shares of their own value. The KOSPI, worth 3,860.35 trillion won, traded 16.06 trillion won, a turnover of **0.416%**. The KOSDAQ, worth 363.71 trillion won, traded 4.67 trillion, a turnover of **1.285%**. The KOSDAQ's turnover was 3.1 times the KOSPI's.

## One tenth of the value, nearly a third of the trading

The KOSDAQ is 9.4% of the KOSPI's market value and was 29.1% of its trading value that day. It also changed hands in more shares: 405.9 million against 218.6 million for the KOSPI. The price per share is far lower on the KOSDAQ, so the count of shares says less than the won value does, but the direction is the same.

The pattern holds across the series indices. The median of 51 KOSPI-series indices was 0.401% of value traded; the median of 39 KOSDAQ-series indices was 1.123%.

## Turnover inside the KOSPI

The industry indices of the KOSPI show where the heavy trading was. Electrical and Electronics, the group that includes Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, is 57.9% of the KOSPI's market value and 64.1% of its trading value, a turnover of 0.46%. Others stood as follows:

| KOSPI industry index | Share of market value | Share of trading value | Turnover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction | 0.6% | 1.4% | 0.96% |
| IT Services | 2.2% | 4.2% | 0.79% |
| General Services | 1.3% | 2.2% | 0.69% |
| Financials | 15.1% | 10.8% | 0.30% |
| Transport Equipment and Parts | 5.9% | 3.5% | 0.25% |
| Insurance | 2.4% | 1.1% | 0.20% |
| Pharmaceuticals | 3.5% | 1.6% | 0.18% |

Construction and IT Services were also the two largest fallers of the KOSPI series that day, down 6.91% and 8.48%. Among industry indices with more than ten companies, they had the highest turnover. That is a coincidence in one session, not a rule. The quietest KOSPI industry indices, Paper and Wood and Non-Metallic Minerals, each traded 0.15% of their value.

## Turnover inside the KOSDAQ

On the KOSDAQ the highest turnover was in technology and machinery groups:

- KOSDAQ 150 Information Technology: 1.97% (57 companies, 1.57 trillion won traded).
- Machinery and Equipment: 1.85% (188 companies).
- KOSDAQ Tech Growth Tier: 1.80% (260 companies).
- IT Services: 1.73% (233 companies).
- Chemicals: 1.69% (125 companies).

The lowest were Telecommunications (0.15%, nine companies), Paper and Wood (0.11%, nine) and Other Manufacturing (0.23%, 16). Even the busiest KOSPI industry index with more than ten companies, Construction at 0.96%, turned over less than the KOSDAQ median.

## What this does not show

One session cannot say whether KOSDAQ's higher turnover is typical, and a day when both boards fell sharply may not look like an ordinary one. The records give amounts traded, not who traded or why. Nothing here is a forecast or advice to buy or sell.
