The KOSPI closed at 7,080.92 on 23 September, the highest close in our 68-session archive, and at 4,697.51 on 25 November, a fall of **33.7%**. The typical share fell by less than the index and many fell by more. Among the 900 KOSPI shares that traded on both days, the median change was **minus 13.5%**, and **130** were higher.

## The KOSPI, share by share

| Change in close, 23 September to 25 November | KOSPI shares | KOSDAQ shares |
|---|---|---|
| Down 50% or more | 22 | 69 |
| Down 30% to 50% | 132 | 313 |
| Down 10% to 30% | 384 | 739 |
| Down 0 to 10% | 230 | 316 |
| Up 0 to 10% | 76 | 146 |
| Up 10% to 30% | 39 | 68 |

On the KOSPI, 154 shares, 17.1%, are down 30% or more. A further 384 are down between 10% and 30%. Only 54 (6.0%) are up by 10% or more.

## Three ways to say how far it fell

| KOSPI measure | Change since 23 September |
|---|---|
| Index close | -33.7% |
| Sum of market value of the 900 shares | -34.3% |
| Median share | -13.5% |
| Market value excluding Samsung Electronics and SK hynix | -24.8% |

The index and the market value agree, as they should. The median share's loss is less than half the index's. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, which fell 40.2% and 44.7%, hold a large part of the value, and that is why the weighted measure is worse than the typical share. Take them out and the rest of the market is down about a quarter.

## The KOSDAQ

The KOSDAQ looks different. Its median share fell more, 17.0% against 13.5%, and 1,437 of 1,702 shares are lower (84.4%). Yet its market value fell 22.2%, less than the KOSPI's 34.3%. Sixty-nine shares, 4.1%, have lost half or more, against 2.4% on the KOSPI, and 45 are up more than 30%.

## A caution on price changes

These are changes in closing price. A share that split, paid a bonus issue or had a similar event between the two dates shows a large fall in price that is not a loss, and we have not adjusted for that. The most extreme rows in the 22 and 69 shares that halved may include such cases, so the counts at the extremes are less firm than the median.

## Who is not counted

We counted shares that traded on both days and were in the same market on both. That leaves out 35 KOSPI listings and 135 KOSDAQ listings, which includes those not traded on either day and any that listed or moved market since September.

## What this does not show

The table compares two dates, not the path between them. A share could have fallen further and recovered, or the reverse. It says nothing about why prices moved, and our copy ends on 25 November. The median share's fall, the index's fall and the market-value fall are three different things, and none is a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
