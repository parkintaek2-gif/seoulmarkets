The National Pension Service's latest 5%-rule report is at or above 5% in **230 of the 824 KOSPI companies** in our copy of the filings. Those 230 companies were worth 2,519.1 trillion won on 24 November 2026, **63.7%** of the board's 3,954.3 trillion. The window is narrow and the count is a floor, as explained below.

## How large the stakes are

Among the 230, the lowest reported stake is 5.00% and the highest is 13.41%. The median is **7.43%**. Thirty-five stakes are at or above 10%, 98 are at or above 8%, and 51 are under 6%. Another 48 companies carry a pension-fund row whose latest report is below 5%, which means the fund has told the regulator it dropped under the threshold.

Most of the reports are recent: 161 are dated 2026, 61 are dated 2025 and 8 are dated 2024.

## Who else appears

Counting holders at or above 5% across the 777 companies in the snapshot, the pension fund (230) is far ahead of any other single name. Next are Mirae Asset Management with 22 companies, BlackRock Fund Advisors with 21, VIP Asset Management with 17, Samsung Asset Management with 12 and Baring Asset Management with 11.

## How many holders sit above 5%

Of the 777 companies, 57 (7.3%) have no holder at or above 5% in the window. 371 have one, 238 have two, 73 have three, 24 have four, 6 have five and 8 have six or more. The largest reported holder is at 50% or more in 257 companies (33.1%) and between 30% and 50% in 259 (33.3%).

## What this does not show

A 5%-rule report is filed when a holder crosses 5% and again on each change of 1 point or more, so it records events, not a daily position. Our copy covers filings from 10 September 2024 to 8 September 2026. A holder whose last report is older does not appear, which is why some of the largest companies show no pension-fund row at all. The 230 is therefore a count of what the filings in our window show, and not a census of the fund's holdings. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell any security.
