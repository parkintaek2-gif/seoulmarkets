Total assets are everything a company reports owning at the year-end: cash, receivables, stock, buildings, machinery, investments. We took the figure from each fiscal-2025 annual report and sorted the listed companies into size bands.

## The middle company

Of **2,559** companies with total assets above zero, the median held **203.1 billion won**. A company at the 25th percentile of the list held about 83 billion, and one at the 75th about 632 billion. Added up, the group held **11,152.9 trillion won**.

## The bands

| Total assets at year-end 2025 | Companies | Share of companies | Share of summed assets | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 50 billion won | 307 | 12.0% | 0.1% | 0.5% |
| 50 to under 100 billion | 491 | 19.2% | 0.3% | 0.9% |
| 100 to under 500 billion | 1,018 | 39.8% | 2.2% | 4.1% |
| 500 billion to under 1 trillion | 275 | 10.7% | 1.7% | 3.6% |
| 1 to under 5 trillion | 303 | 11.8% | 5.8% | 7.0% |
| 5 to under 10 trillion | 55 | 2.1% | 3.5% | 3.6% |
| 10 trillion and above | 110 | 4.3% | 86.5% | 78.4% |

The last column is each band's share of the whole market's value, so it does not add to 100%. **798** companies, 31.2% of the list, held less than 100 billion won each. The **110** companies at 10 trillion or more were 4.3% of the list and **86.5%** of the assets.

## The top

| Company | Total assets, end 2025 |
|---|---|
| KB Financial Group | 797.9 trillion won |
| Shinhan Financial Group | 786.0 trillion |
| Hana Financial Group | 674.6 trillion |
| Woori Financial Group | 601.5 trillion |
| Samsung Electronics | 566.9 trillion |

The five largest held **30.7%** of the total, the ten largest **46.5%**, and the hundred largest **85.6%**.

## Financial companies and the rest

The 114 companies that the tape classes as Financial Services held **43.5%** of all summed assets, but were **10.5%** of market value. Of the 110 companies with 10 trillion won or more, 25 were in that group, and they held 4,678.8 of the band's 9,648.0 trillion won. The balance sheet of a bank is made mostly of what it lends and what it owes, so we do not compare it with a factory's.

## KOSPI and KOSDAQ

| Market | Companies | Median total assets |
|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 807 | 889.0 billion won |
| KOSDAQ | 1,740 | 123.9 billion |
| KONEX | 12 | 42.9 billion (few companies) |

The median KOSPI company held about seven times the assets of the median KOSDAQ company.

## Assets and market value

Of the **2,536** companies with both a total assets figure and a market value, **1,990** (78.5%) had assets above their market value. The two are on different dates, year-end 2025 against 12 November 2026, and measure different things, so the count is a description and not a signal.

## What this does not say

Total assets add up where one listed company holds shares in another, and we did not net those out. Size by assets is not size by sales, profit or value. We do not say any band is better.
