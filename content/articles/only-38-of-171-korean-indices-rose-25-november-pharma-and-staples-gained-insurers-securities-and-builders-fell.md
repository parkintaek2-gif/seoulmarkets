Of the 171 indices in our 25 November snapshot of Korean index data, **38 rose** and 133 fell. The KOSPI itself fell 6.16%. The gains were concentrated in healthcare, consumer staples and a few entertainment and software series.

## Where the rises were

| Family | Indices | Rose | Fell |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI Series | 53 | 14 | 39 |
| KOSDAQ Series | 39 | 8 | 31 |
| KRX Series | 40 | 9 | 31 |
| Thematic | 39 | 7 | 32 |
| All | 171 | 38 | 133 |

The largest rise among the 171 was the KOSDAQ 150 Consumer Staples index at plus 6.92%. In the KRX family, the KRX 300 Consumer Staples index rose 5.09%, and among thematic indices the KRX Games Top 10 rose 5.58%. In the KOSPI family, KOSPI 200 Healthcare rose 5.30% and the Pharmaceuticals sector index 4.66%.

## The biggest falls

The sharpest declines in the snapshot were two strategy indices, the K-Sharpe Index (1-Year) at minus 13.79% and the K-Sharpe Index (5-Year) at minus 13.04%. Among the sector and size series, the KOSPI Electrical and Electronics index fell 9.81% and the KOSPI 200 Information Technology index 9.38%. The KOSPI 200 excluding mega caps fell 2.43%. A separate article today looks at how two shares account for much of the KOSPI's loss.

## Same name, different index

The point of the table is that "insurers" or "builders" on this day were not one number. Anyone quoting a sector move needs to name the index. The KOSPI Series Financials index, with 111 constituents, fell 3.57%, while the KRX 300 Financials index, with 29, fell 1.97%.
