Does a larger Korean listed company lose money less often? We sorted 2,438 companies by fiscal 2025 revenue and counted how many reported an operating loss in each band. The answer is yes, at every step.

## Six revenue bands

| Revenue, 2025 | Companies | Median operating margin | With an operating loss | Loss share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 50 billion won | 704 | -10.2% | 443 | 63% |
| 50 to 100 billion | 389 | 2.1% | 157 | 40% |
| 100 to 500 billion | 786 | 3.9% | 167 | 21% |
| 500 billion to 1 trillion | 195 | 4.3% | 23 | 12% |
| 1 to 5 trillion | 268 | 4.2% | 18 | 7% |
| 5 trillion and above | 96 | 5.4% | 4 | 4% |

Across all six bands, 812 of 2,438 companies, a third, reported an operating loss. They are not spread evenly. 443 of the 812 are in the smallest band, more than half of all the loss-makers, even though that band holds 29% of the companies.

## The loss rate falls every step

The share with an operating loss goes 63%, 40%, 21%, 12%, 7%, 4%. No band breaks the order. The median operating margin does not move as smoothly. It is negative only in the smallest band (-10.2%), then 2.1%, 3.9%, 4.3%, 4.2% and 5.4%. From 100 billion won of revenue upward the median margin sits in a narrow range of 3.9% to 5.4%, while the loss rate keeps falling. Larger companies in this list are not much more profitable at the median. They are much less likely to be in the red.

## What a small company's number may hide

A company with under 50 billion won of sales includes firms that have only just listed, firms in research that sell little, and shells. A single year's operating loss for such a company is not the same fact as a loss at a company with 5 trillion won of sales. The table counts them the same way and says nothing about why.

## What this does not say

271 of 2,709 companies are not here because the figures were missing. Revenue and operating income are one fiscal year. Financial companies report them differently from manufacturers and are in the same bands. A loss rate describes a group, not any one company in it. This is a statistic about a list and is not investment advice.
