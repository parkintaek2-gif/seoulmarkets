The KOSPI closed at 4,869.96 on Friday 20 November 2026, down 5.21% on the day. The KOSDAQ closed at 645.68, down 5.71%. Samsung Electronics fell 7.01% and SK hynix 5.92%. A sell-side sidecar, the exchange's brief halt on programme selling, was triggered at 11:54. Those figures are from the Korean exchange reports.

This piece puts the day in the 63 sessions since 20 August, using the index's daily closes.

## Where 4,869.96 sits

Over the 63 sessions the KOSPI's highest close was 7,080.92 on 23 September. The close on 20 November is 31.2% below that, and it is the lowest close in the window. On 20 August the index closed at 6,852.58, so it has lost 28.9% since the first day of the window.

## Five days of 5% or more

The index has fallen by 5% or more on five of the 63 sessions:

- 13 October: down 5.46%, to 6,035.35
- 15 October: down 7.57%, to 5,554.22
- 5 November: down 5.28%, to 5,383.80
- 6 November: down 6.02%, to 5,059.69
- 20 November: down 5.21%, to 4,869.96

Between 13 October and 20 November, which is 29 sessions, there were five such days. Before 13 October the window has none: the worst fall in the first 34 sessions was 3.99% on 2 September. Four of the five were within 19 trading days, from 13 October to 6 November.

A 5% fall is not the same as a 5% loss for a holder. The index at 5,059.69 on 6 November and at 4,869.96 on 20 November are 3.8% apart.

## The rises in between

The window also has three rises of more than 4%: 4.61% on 7 September, 6.63% on 28 October and 4.39% on 10 November. The 28 October rise took the index to 6,012.44. From that close to 20 November the index lost 19.0%.

Recent days alternated. The index fell 2.59% on 17 November, rose 1.99% on 18 November, rose 1.39% on 19 November to 5,137.89, and fell 5.21% on 20 November. The 19 November figure is from the Yahoo close and was not checked against an exchange report.

## The KOSDAQ

The KOSDAQ closed at 645.68 on 20 November, down from 684.81 the day before and from 662.81 on 18 November. Its 5.71% fall is larger than the KOSPI's 5.21%. We did not build the KOSDAQ's own five-day count, so we cannot say how this day ranks in that index.

## What this does not say

This is a count of days in a 63-session window, not a forecast. The reports we read tie the fall to selling in US technology shares, but they do not agree on the details and we have not checked them, so we do not state a cause. Four of the five big falls rest on a single data source, noted above. Nothing here is a reason to buy or sell anything. It is a statistic about an index, not about any one investor.
