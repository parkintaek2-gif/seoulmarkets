On the Korea Exchange's gold market, the same 99.99% pure gold trades in two bar sizes, 1kg and 100g. The 100g bar's close was a median **0.06%** above the 1kg bar's across the 66 sessions from **1 July to 7 October 2026**. It was as much as **1.05%** above on one day and **0.29%** below on another.

## The gap in one number and its range

Both bars are quoted in won per gram. On **1 July** the 1kg bar closed at 197,190 and the 100g bar at 197,170, a gap of 20 won. On **7 October** they closed at 178,750 and 178,830. Between those dates the 1kg bar's close ranged from 178,750 to 208,300.

Across the 66 sessions:

- the premium of the 100g bar was within 0.1% of the 1kg price, in either direction, on **35** days;
- the 100g bar was **cheaper** than the 1kg bar on **24** days;
- it was more than 0.3% dearer on 14 days, and more than 0.5% dearer on **5** days.

## The widest and narrowest days

The widest gap was on **9 September**, when the 100g bar closed at 193,000 against 191,000 for the 1kg bar, a premium of 1.05%. The narrowest was on **21 July**, when the 100g bar closed at 191,900 against 192,460, so it was 0.29% cheaper.

The five widest days all fall between 2 and 11 September: the 2nd (0.56%), the 3rd (0.88%), the 7th (0.64%), the 9th (1.05%) and the 11th (0.62%). Nothing like that clustering appears in July or August.

## Volume: the small bar is a small market

Trading in the 1kg bar is much larger. The median day's volume was 210,736 for the 1kg bar and 9,787 for the 100g bar, so the 100g bar's median is about 4.6% of the 1kg bar's. The five widest days did not stand out by volume: the 100g bar traded between 5,119 and 15,432 on them, close to its median.

## Days when the two bars moved in opposite directions

On six days the two bars' daily percentage changes had opposite signs, and on one more day one bar was unchanged while the other moved. For example, on 9 September the 1kg bar fell 0.09% while the 100g bar rose 0.68%. On 10 September the 1kg bar rose 0.26% and the 100g bar fell 0.52%.

## What this does not show

The window ends on 7 October. Nothing here says what the gap has done since, or why it widened in early September. The closes come from one archive of exchange quotes and were not checked day by day against a second source.
