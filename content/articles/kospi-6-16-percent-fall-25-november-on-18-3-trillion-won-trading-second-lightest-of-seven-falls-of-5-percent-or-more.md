The KOSPI fell **6.16%** on 25 November on trading value of **18.29 trillion won**. In the 68 sessions of our index archive, 23 had lower trading value and 44 higher. The median session traded 20.58 trillion won, so the day's volume was about 11% below the median even though the index fell the most since 15 October.

## Seven falls, seven volumes

The KOSPI has fallen 5% or more on seven sessions in the window.

| Date | KOSPI change | KOSPI trading value (trillion won) | Versus median day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 August | -5.80% | 23.12 | +12% |
| 13 October | -5.46% | 28.45 | +38% |
| 15 October | -7.57% | 28.64 | +39% |
| 5 November | -5.28% | 23.61 | +15% |
| 6 November | -6.02% | 28.98 | +41% |
| 20 November | -5.21% | 14.95 | -27% |
| 25 November | -6.16% | 18.29 | -11% |

Only 20 November traded less. Two of the three lightest falls fell in the last four sessions, 20 and 25 November. The three heaviest, at 28.45 to 28.98 trillion won, were 13 October, 15 October and 6 November. A larger fall did not mean a heavier day: the fall of 7.57% on 15 October and the fall of 5.46% on 13 October traded almost the same amount.

## Who traded on the way down

Adding up trading value by direction, listings that closed lower on 25 November carried **84.8%** of KOSPI trading, about 15.5 trillion won. Rising listings carried 15.2%, about 2.8 trillion won. Across the 61 sessions of our stock archive the median share carried by falling listings is 57.2%, and 15 sessions were higher.

| Highest share of trading in falling listings | Share |
|---|---|
| 20 October | 97.2% |
| 15 October | 94.4% |
| 20 November | 94.2% |
| 17 November | 93.1% |
| 25 November | 84.8% (16th highest) |

This share does not distinguish selling from buying. Every trade has both sides; the figure says only where the won were traded.

## The KOSDAQ

KOSDAQ trading value on 25 November was 5.45 trillion won, below the median of 6.04 trillion won, with 22 sessions lower and 45 higher. The KOSDAQ index fell 2.29%, compared with 5.71% on 20 November, a session with lower volume of 4.57 trillion won. The index fell more on the lighter day.

## A smaller market

All of these totals are in won, and the KOSPI's market value has fallen from 5,852 trillion won on 23 September to 3,848 trillion won on 25 November. A given volume is therefore a larger share of the market now than it was in September. For that reason we do not rank days by turnover as a share of value in this article, and we do not claim that 25 November was a quiet day by that measure.

## What this does not show

Trading value is a total and says nothing about who traded or why. Seven sessions are a small sample and the archive starts on 18 August, so the pattern may not hold over longer periods. Our copy ends with the 25 November close, and nothing here describes later sessions. This is a count of one window, not a forecast and not advice to buy or sell any share.
