On Thursday 26 November the ten largest listings on the KOSPI had a combined market value of **2,287 trillion won**, or **59.2%** of the KOSPI's 3,860 trillion. On 27 August, the first day of our stock-by-stock archive, the ten largest were 62.8%. On the KOSDAQ the ten largest were 17.1% of 376 trillion won.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Samsung Electronics | 1,001.47 | 25.94% | +0.35% |
| SK hynix | 744.26 | 19.28% | +2.23% |
| Samsung Electronics (preferred) | 109.92 | 2.85% | +1.63% |

| KB Financial Group | 51.54 | 1.34% | -1.89% |
| Celltrion | 50.02 | 1.30% | -0.69% |

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix together are 45.2% of KOSPI value. Counting the Samsung Electronics preferred share with the common share, Samsung Electronics is 28.8%.

Only 25 and 26 November were below 59.25%. The share fell as the KOSPI's total value fell from 5,702 trillion won on 27 August to 3,860 trillion. The ten largest of each day lost value too, from 3,583 to 2,287 trillion, a fall of 36.2% against 32.3% for the whole KOSPI. Two listings in the 27 August ten, Samsung Life Insurance and Samsung C&T, are no longer in it; KB Financial Group and Celltrion are.

## The KOSDAQ ten

The ten largest KOSDAQ listings had 64.26 trillion won of the market's 375.75 trillion, **17.10%**. Alteogen is the largest at 11.86 trillion won (3.16%), followed by Rainbow Robotics at 9.06 trillion and EcoPro BM at 8.74 trillion. The KOSDAQ ten were 18.87% on 27 August; over 62 sessions the share ranged from 16.42% on 9 November to 19.10% on 6 October.

## The two markets side by side

Samsung Electronics alone, at 1,001 trillion won, is **2.7 times** the value of all 1,837 KOSDAQ listings together. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are 4.6 times the KOSDAQ. The KOSPI's ten largest are 6.1 times it.

## What this does not show

These are shares of total market value, not of what investors can buy: free-float and foreign-ownership limits are not in the files. Each listing is counted separately, so one company can occupy two places. The files do not say why concentration rose or fell. This is not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
