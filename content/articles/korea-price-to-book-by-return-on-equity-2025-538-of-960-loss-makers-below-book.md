Does the Korean market pay more for companies that earn more on their equity? We sorted 2,535 listed companies by their fiscal 2025 return on equity, which is net income divided by year-end equity, and looked at the median price-to-book in each band. The price is from 13 November 2026.

## Five bands

| Return on equity, 2025 | Companies | Median price-to-book | Market value, trillion won | Below book value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Below 0 | 960 | 0.84 | 279 | 538 |
| 0 to 5% | 607 | 0.53 | 543 | 452 |
| 5 to 10% | 525 | 0.63 | 831 | 378 |
| 10 to 15% | 246 | 0.85 | 1,501 | 144 |
| 15% and above | 197 | 1.52 | 1,405 | 70 |

The median price-to-book falls from 0.84 to 0.53 as return on equity goes from negative to the 0 to 5% band, then rises: 0.63 at 5 to 10%, 0.85 at 10 to 15%, 1.52 above 15%. The market does not pay more at every step. The group that lost money on its equity is valued at a higher median multiple of book than the group that earned a little.

## Below book value is the norm in the lower bands

Of the 960 companies with a negative return on equity, 538 trade below book value. Of the 607 earning 0 to 5%, 452 do, three in four. Of the 525 earning 5 to 10%, 378 do. Even in the top band, 70 of 197 trade below book. A low price-to-book is not rare and it is not specific to weak earners in this list.

## The top band is led by a few large names

The five largest companies at 15% or more are SK hynix (return on equity 35.6%, price-to-book 7.36), SK Square (31.5%, 3.59), Samsung Biologics (24.0%, 8.77), HD Hyundai Heavy Industries (15.2%, 3.04) and Meritz Financial Group (20.9%, 1.76). The band's median of 1.52 is far below the first three, which shows how wide the spread is inside it.

## What this does not say

The pattern is in medians of groups of different sizes, and we did not test whether it holds after removing the largest companies. Price is one day and earnings are a year-end figure from months earlier. A company with negative equity has no price-to-book at all and is not here. A return on equity is one year, and a negative year does not say what comes next. This is a statistic about a list, not about any one company, and it is not investment advice.
