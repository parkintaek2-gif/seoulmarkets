One company accounts for **41.3%** of the market value of the companies listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange that filed statements for the first half of 2026. It is listed in the exchange feed as TSMC (code 2330), at **66.1 trillion** New Taiwan dollars out of a total of **160.1 trillion**. The next three, MediaTek, Delta and Hon Hai, add 4.7%, 3.2% and 2.2%, which brings the top four to **51.4%**. Prices are the 8 October close, the latest in the feed.

| Measure (1,069 firms with a market value) | Value |

| Top 10 companies | 60.1% |
| Semiconductors industry group, share of market value | 55.9% |
| Electronic components | 11.5% |
| Finance and insurance | 7.4% |

Profits are as concentrated. Of the 1,061 companies with a net-profit line, 841 reported a profit for the first half. TSMC's NT$1,279.6 billion is **41.1%** of the sum of those profits. The five largest profit-makers together earned half of the total. The same company's revenue, NT$2,404.5 billion, is 8.7% of all first-half revenue in the feed, so it is more concentrated in value and profit than in sales.

The first company is more than eight times the size of the second. The tenth holds 1.2%, so below the top four the shares fall away quickly. Names are as the feed gives them, in short English forms, and we have not expanded them or checked them against another source.

## Losses and companies below book value

Of 1,061 companies with a net-profit line, **220** (20.7%) reported a net loss for the first half. Those 220 hold only 1.25% of market value, 219 of them having a market value in the feed. The loss-makers are numerous but small in value terms.

Price-to-book can be computed for 1,072 companies. For **254** of them (23.7%) it is below 1.0. The 253 below-book firms with a market value total 3.0% of the market. The median price-to-book across the 1,072 is 1.72, and the median dividend yield, for companies where one is given, is 3.34%.

## Reading it

Count by firms and the exchange is a market with one in five companies losing money and one in four trading below book. Weight by value and it is a market that, on these numbers, is determined by a handful of electronics and semiconductor names. The two views describe the same 1,074 filings.

## What this does not say

These are counts and shares from one exchange feed on one price date, not a forecast, and not a statement about why Taiwan's market is shaped this way. They do not cover the Taipei Exchange, free-float weights or foreign holdings. A cumulative half-year profit is not a full-year profit, and a company below book value is not thereby cheap or expensive. Nothing here is a reason to buy or sell any share. It is a statistic about a set of companies, not about any one investor.
