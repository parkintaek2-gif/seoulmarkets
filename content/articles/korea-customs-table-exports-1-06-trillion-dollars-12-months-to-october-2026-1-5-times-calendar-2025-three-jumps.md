Add up the twelve months from November 2025 to October 2026 in Korea's partner-country trade table and exports come to **$1,060.5 billion**. January to October 2026 alone is **$930.2 billion**. The commodity-group table gives calendar 2025 at $709.3 billion, the highest of the six years from 2020, so the latest twelve months are 1.5 times that year.

## A round number, by arithmetic

Reaching $1 trillion in calendar 2026 would need $69.8 billion from November and December together. In the same table those two months of 2025 were $60.8 billion and $69.5 billion, $130.3 billion in all, and the remainder is 53.6% of that. The table cannot say what the last two months of 2026 will be, and we do not guess.

## Three jumps carry the rise

| Month | Exports, $ billion | Change on the month |
|---|---|---|
| January 2026 | 65.84 | -5.3% |
| February | 67.64 | +2.7% |
| March | 87.37 | +29.2% |
| April | 85.74 | -1.9% |
| May | 87.52 | +2.1% |
| June | 101.96 | +16.5% |
| July | 99.22 | -2.7% |
| August | 98.25 | -1.0% |
| September | 121.25 | +23.4% |
| October | 115.41 | -4.8% |

The series does not climb evenly. It steps three times, in March, June and September, and between steps it drifts. January and February average $66.7 billion; March to October average $99.6 billion. September's $121.25 billion is the highest month in the table, October's $115.41 billion the second.

At the March jump China's share moved 0.2 points and the United States' 0.0. At the September jump China's share fell 3.1 points and the United States' rose 3.3.

## What this does not show

In August we wrote that the March step looked like a change in how the table is compiled rather than a change in trade, and that the dollar levels after February should not be read as fact. Nothing since then has settled it. Real growth and a compilation change both fit these numbers, and the table cannot tell them apart. It also gets revised each month, so the figures here may differ next month. This is a sum of what the table holds, not a forecast of the year and not advice to buy or sell any share.
