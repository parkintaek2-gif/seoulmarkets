The equity ratio is total equity divided by total assets. If it is 0.60, then 60 won of every 100 won of assets is financed by owners and the other 40 by creditors. We read both figures from each company's fiscal-2025 annual report and ranked the ratio across the listed universe.

## The middle and the spread

Across **2,559** companies with both numbers, the median equity ratio was **60.3%**. The spread was wide: the company ranked at the 10th percentile sat near **30%**, the one at the 90th near **88%**, and the middle half ran from about **43%** to about **78%**. No company in the set had equity above its assets.

## The bands

| Equity as a share of assets | Companies | Share of companies | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero or below | 14 | 0.5% | 0.0% |
| Above 0 to 20% | 114 | 4.5% | 8.1% |
| Above 20 to 40% | 403 | 15.7% | 9.1% |
| Above 40 to 60% | 734 | 28.7% | 14.3% |
| Above 60 to 80% | 729 | 28.5% | 60.1% |
| Above 80% | 565 | 22.1% | 6.5% |

**128** companies had 20% or less: the 14 at or below zero and the 114 above zero up to 20%. The last column is each row's share of the whole market's value, not a share within the row.

The 60-80% row is where the largest names sit. Its 729 companies, 28.5% of the count, held **60.1%** of market value. The 565 companies above 80% held 6.5%.

## KOSPI and KOSDAQ

| Market | Companies | Median equity ratio |
|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 807 | 50.8% |
| KOSDAQ | 1,740 | 64.9% |

The median KOSDAQ company was financed more by its owners than the median KOSPI company, by about 14 percentage points. KONEX has 12 companies with both figures, too few for a median.

## By industry

Among industries with at least 30 companies, the lowest medians were General Construction at **35.6%** (38 companies), Motor Vehicles and Trailers at **43.3%** (106) and Electrical Equipment at **49.5%** (84). The highest were Financial and Insurance Related Services at **83.8%** (64), Publishing at **70.4%** (185) and Information Services at **68.2%** (33).

The Financial Services group of 114 companies had a median of 60.9%, close to the 60.3% for everyone. We show it for completeness and do not compare it with factories, whose liabilities are of a different kind.

## What this does not say

Book equity is an accounting figure. A company with a low ratio may be financed by long, cheap loans, and one with a high ratio may simply be small. We do not say which is stronger.
