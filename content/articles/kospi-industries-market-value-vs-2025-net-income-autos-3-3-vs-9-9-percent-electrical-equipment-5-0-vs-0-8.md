An index weight tells you how much of the market's value an industry holds. The 2025 annual reports tell you how much of its profit it earned. For 23 KOSPI industries with at least 10 companies each, we put the two side by side. Market value is the 23 November 2026 price; net income is the full year 2025, with losses netted against profits inside each industry.

## Ten largest industries by value

| Industry | Companies | Share of market value | Share of 2025 net income | Value / net income |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electronic components, computers, communication equipment | 45 | 51.98% | 39.49% | 21.1x |
| Financial services | 78 | 11.79% | 23.19% | 8.1x |
| Electrical equipment | 24 | 5.00% | 0.78% | 103.2x |
| Medicinal substances and pharmaceuticals | 48 | 3.63% | 1.47% | 39.5x |
| Motor vehicles and trailers | 50 | 3.28% | 9.89% | 5.3x |
| Other transport equipment | 10 | 2.60% | 2.84% | 14.7x |
| Insurance and pension funding | 11 | 2.45% | 4.02% | 9.7x |
| Chemicals and chemical products | 73 | 2.24% | 0.79% | 45.4x |
| Wholesale and commission trade | 40 | 1.73% | 2.62% | 10.5x |
| Other machinery and equipment | 32 | 1.67% | 0.44% | 61.0x |

The last column is the industry's total market value divided by its total 2025 net income, not an average of company ratios.

## Who is ahead of its profit, who is behind

Dividing the profit share by the value share gives one number per industry. Four industries earn at least 2.4 times their share of value:

- Electricity, gas and steam supply: 0.58% of value, 4.02% of net income, **6.9 times**.
- Motor vehicles and trailers: **3.0 times**.
- Rubber and plastic products: 0.33% of value, 0.81% of net income, 2.5 times.
- Apparel manufacturing: 0.12% of value, 0.28% of net income, 2.4 times.

At the other end, electrical equipment earned **0.16 times** its share of value. Other machinery and equipment earned 0.26 times, real estate 0.19 times, and food products manufacturing 0.04 times (0.67% of value, 0.03% of net income). Pulp and paper had a net loss across its 15 companies in 2025, so its ratio is negative.

## One company moves an industry

Industry totals hide how few companies carry them. Electrical equipment's 186 trillion won of value includes LG Energy Solution, which was worth 68.2 trillion won on 23 November and reported 2025 net income of 0.08 trillion won. That one company is 36.7% of the industry's value. With 24 companies in the group, a change in one company's earnings moves the industry's ratio a long way. The same caution applies to the 10-company groups, such as other transport equipment.

Electronic components, computers and communication equipment is the heavyweight: 51.98% of value and 39.49% of profit, with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix inside it. Its value-to-profit ratio of 21.1 times is above the market's 16.0 times, though below electrical equipment's and chemicals'.

## What this does not show

The comparison uses one price date against one fiscal year, so an industry whose earnings changed in 2026 would look different against current figures. A low value-to-profit ratio can mean a market that sees little growth or a one-time profit; the tape cannot tell the two apart. Nothing here is a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
