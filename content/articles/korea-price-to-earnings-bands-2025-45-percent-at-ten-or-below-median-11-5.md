A price-to-earnings ratio, or P/E, is a company's market value divided by its net income. A P/E of 10 means the market value is ten times a year's profit. Here we use the market value on 12 November 2026 and the net income in each company's fiscal-2025 annual report. A company with a loss has no P/E, so we count them separately.

## Who has a P/E

Of **2,577** companies with a net income figure, **1,592** (61.8%) were profitable and **985** (38.2%) had a loss or zero. A P/E exists for **1,580** of the profitable group; 12 are not in the price file. The 985 without one were a large share of the companies but only **6.1%** of market value.

## The bands

| P/E | Companies | Share of companies | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 10 | 712 | 45.1% | 13.8% |
| Above 10 to 20 | 372 | 23.5% | 13.2% |
| Above 20 to 40 | 217 | 13.7% | 54.4% |
| Above 40 to 100 | 178 | 11.3% | 7.5% |
| Above 100 | 101 | 6.4% | 4.3% |

The column on the right is a share of the whole market, so it adds up to 93.2%, the value of all profitable companies, and not to 100%.

The count and the value point in different directions. Almost half of the companies were at 10 or below, yet they held about a seventh of market value. The 20 to 40 band had under 14% of the companies and **54.4%** of the value. Three of the five largest companies are in it: Samsung Electronics at 26.9, SK hynix at 21.3 and Samsung Biologics at 37.6.

## The middle, and the edges

The median P/E was **11.5**. The company at the 10th percentile had a P/E of about 3.7, and the one at the 90th about 65.8. The highest in the set was 78,800, at a company whose profit was a tiny fraction of its value; a figure like that is a sign of near-zero earnings, not of a premium.

SK Square, the third largest, was at 11.8. LG Energy Solution, the fourth, was at **954.2**, which falls in the top band because its fiscal-2025 profit was small against its value of 77.1 trillion won.

## KOSPI and KOSDAQ

| Market | Companies with a P/E | Median P/E | Up to 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 612 | 10.6 | 295 (48.2%) |
| KOSDAQ | 964 | 12.2 | 415 (43.1%) |

KOSPI's median was lower than KOSDAQ's by about 1.6 turns. KONEX has only four companies with a P/E, too few to show.

## What this does not say

The P/E here is trailing and annual. It mixes a price from November 2026 with profit from 2025, and a company whose earnings changed in 2026 will look different once its next report is filed. One-off gains also move it. We do not say a low P/E is good or a high one is bad: the table shows only how the companies were spread across the bands.

*This is statistics, not you or your company.*
