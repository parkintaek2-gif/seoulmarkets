On the 26 November 2026 close, 348 of the 1,088 Taiwan Stock Exchange companies that carry a
price-to-book figure traded below 1.0. That is 32.0%, close to one company in three. The other
740 sat at or above book value, and 77 of them at five times book or more.

The headline number hides most of the story. Below-book is not spread evenly across the market.
It is concentrated in a few old-economy industries, while the industry Taiwan is known for sits
almost entirely above book.

## The shape of the whole market

| Price-to-book band | Companies | Share of 1,088 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Below 0.5 | 50 | 4.6% |
| 0.5 to under 1.0 | 298 | 27.4% |
| 1.0 to under 2.0 | 416 | 38.2% |
| 2.0 to under 3.0 | 162 | 14.9% |
| 3.0 to under 5.0 | 85 | 7.8% |
| 5.0 and above | 77 | 7.1% |

The median is 1.36. The largest single group, 416 companies, trades between one and two times
book. The two tails are of similar size: 348 companies under 1.0, and 162 at 3.0 or above.

## Where below-book clusters

| Building materials and construction | 55 | 35 | 63.6% | 0.79 |
| Electrical appliances and cable | 16 | 9 | 56.3% | 0.93 |

Plastics is the extreme. 18 of 21 companies trade under book, and the industry median is 0.62:
the typical plastics company is priced at about three-fifths of its stated net assets. Textiles
and construction follow, with 29 of 42 and 35 of 55.

Semiconductors are the mirror image. Only 5 of 96 sit below 1.0, and the industry median, 2.25,
is the highest of the 24 industries with at least 15 companies. The five below book are SiS,
SYNCOMM, FocalTech, JMC and ITH-KY.

Between these ends the larger electronics groups sit in a narrow band: electronic components 21
of 104 below book (20.2%), computers and peripherals 13 of 65 (20.0%), communications and
networking 9 of 47 (19.1%), optoelectronics 21 of 68 (30.9%). Biotechnology and healthcare is 10
of 62 (16.1%) and chemicals 4 of 28 (14.3%).

## The two ends of the list

At the bottom, the lowest ratios on the 26 November close are Jinli (0.13, trading and
department stores), Cayman Engley (0.16, automobiles), Hiroca (0.18, automobiles), JYPKG (0.19,
other), Lida (0.20, electrical machinery) and GORG (0.20, trading and department stores). Each is
priced at a fifth of its stated net assets or less.

At the top, the highest are GUC at 45.9 (semiconductors), TAHO Pharma at 34.0 and PELL BMT at
19.0 (both biotechnology and healthcare), COSMO at 29.8 (optoelectronics), JENTECH at 25.0 and
EMC at 17.9 (both electronic components). The highest ratio is about 350 times the lowest.

The count above is of companies "with a figure", not of all 1,098: ten companies carry no ratio
in the feed, and we did not make one for them.

## By count against by value

A count treats the smallest company like the largest. Weighted by market value, the picture
changes sharply. The 348 below-book companies make up only 4.3% of the combined market value of
the 1,097 companies for which we could compute one. TSMC alone, at 7.95 times book, is a larger
share of that total than the whole below-book group.

So "a third of Taiwan's listed companies trade below book" is true by count, and it says little
about where the market's money sits. Most of the below-book names are small or mid-sized, in
industries that do not carry much of the index's value.

## What we did not do

We did not say why. A ratio under 1.0 can come from low profits, from assets carried above what
they would fetch, from a controlling shareholder who rarely sells, or from nothing more than a
quiet stretch in a thinly traded share. Every one of those is a different story and the tape
cannot tell them apart.

The count covers the main-board Taiwan Stock Exchange only. Companies listed on the Taipei
Exchange are not in it. Ten of the 1,098 companies have no ratio in the feed, and we left them
out rather than fill them. Fifteen have no industry name in the feed, so they are in the
market-wide counts above but not in the industry table.

Book value is an accounting figure from the most recent filed quarter, the third quarter of
2026, while the price is a single day's close. A company that has changed a lot since its
quarter-end will read high or low here for that reason alone.

Taiwan company has its own page under [/taiwan/companies](/taiwan/companies).
