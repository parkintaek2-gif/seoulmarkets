Revenue is what a company reports as sales for the year. We took it from each fiscal-2025 annual report and sorted the listed companies into size bands, so the shape of the whole list is visible and not only its largest names.

## The middle company

Of **2,487** companies with revenue above zero, the median sold **120.8 billion won**. A company at the 25th percentile of the list sold about 40 billion and one at the 75th about 433 billion. Together the group reported **3,630.9 trillion won** of revenue.

## The bands

| Revenue in 2025 | Companies | Share of companies | Share of summed revenue | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 10 billion won | 165 | 6.6% | 0.0% | 0.7% |
| 10 to under 50 billion | 568 | 22.8% | 0.4% | 1.2% |
| 50 to under 100 billion | 395 | 15.9% | 0.8% | 1.3% |
| 100 to under 500 billion | 790 | 31.8% | 5.1% | 5.0% |
| 500 billion to under 1 trillion | 201 | 8.1% | 3.9% | 2.2% |
| 1 to under 5 trillion | 271 | 10.9% | 16.3% | 13.2% |
| 5 trillion and above | 97 | 3.9% | 73.5% | 68.2% |

The last column is each band's share of the whole market's value, so it does not add to 100%. The three bands below 100 billion won held **1,128** companies, 45% of the list, and **1.2%** of summed revenue. The 97 companies in the top band were 3.9% of the list and **73.5%** of the sales.

## The top

| Company | Revenue 2025 |
|---|---|
| Samsung Electronics | 333.6 trillion won |
| Hyundai Motor | 186.3 trillion |
| SK Inc. | 122.7 trillion |
| Kia | 114.1 trillion |
| Korea Electric Power | 97.4 trillion |

The five largest booked **23.5%** of the total, the ten largest **34.9%**, and the hundred largest **73.9%**. Of the 97 companies above 5 trillion won, 13 are classed as Financial Services in the tape, whose revenue is measured on its own basis.

## KOSPI and KOSDAQ

| Market | Companies | Median revenue | 1 trillion or more | Under 10 billion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 778 | 641.3 billion won | 313 | 15 |
| KOSDAQ | 1,697 | 72.8 billion | 55 | 147 |
| KONEX | 12 | not computed | 0 | 3 |

Four in ten KOSPI companies sold at least a trillion won, against 3% of KOSDAQ companies. Leaving out the Financial Services class, the median of the other 2,397 companies sold **118.1 billion won**, close to the all-company figure of 120.8 billion.

## What this does not say

Revenue is sales, not profit and not value. Sales between group companies may be counted more than once in the total, and we did not remove them. Neither the bands nor the shares say what size is better, and we give no benchmark or other country's figure.
