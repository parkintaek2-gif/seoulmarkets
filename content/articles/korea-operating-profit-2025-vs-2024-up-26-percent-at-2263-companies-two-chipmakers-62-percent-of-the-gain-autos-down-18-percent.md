Between the 2024 and 2025 annual reports, the combined operating profit of 2,263 Korean listed companies rose from 211.92 trillion won to **267.51 trillion won**, a gain of **26.2%**, while their revenue rose 7.2% from 3,194.17 to 3,424.48 trillion won. Their combined operating margin went from 6.6% to 7.8%. Each company is compared with itself on the same accounting basis in both years.

## Where the gain sits

SK hynix and Samsung Electronics together added 34.61 trillion won of operating profit. That is **62.3%** of the panel's 55.58 trillion won increase. The other 2,261 companies went from 155.73 to 176.70 trillion won, an increase of 13.5%.

The count tells a more even story. **1,213 companies** reported higher operating profit and **1,050** reported lower. Operating losses were reported by 710 companies in 2024 (31.4%) and 732 in 2025 (32.3%). In between, 195 of the 2024 loss-makers returned to an operating profit, and 217 companies slipped from profit to loss.

## By industry

Operating profit rose in 33 of 59 industries and fell in 26. The five largest increases and the three largest decreases, in trillion won:

| Electricity, gas and steam supply | 11 | 12.08 | 16.52 | +4.44 |
| Medicinal substances and pharmaceuticals | 163 | 3.35 | 5.27 | +1.92 |
| Water transport | 6 | 4.45 | 2.27 | -2.18 |
| Air transport | 6 | 2.76 | 0.37 | -2.40 |
| Motor vehicles and trailers | 100 | 33.38 | 27.33 | -6.05 |

Motor vehicles and trailers lost 18.1% of their operating profit, and their combined margin fell from 7.7% to 6.0%. Air transport lost 86.7% from a small base, and water transport 49.0%. Electronic components, computers and communication equipment rose 53.7%, taking its combined margin from 11.3% to 15.3%.

## By market

The 731 KOSPI companies in the panel went from 200.18 to 253.21 trillion won, up 26.5%. The 1,522 KOSDAQ companies went from 11.73 to 14.28 trillion won, up 21.7%. The KOSDAQ group's total is 5.6% of the KOSPI group's in 2025.

## What this does not show

These are reported results for two past fiscal years, not a forecast. The panel leaves out 248 companies whose basis changed or whose figures were missing, and we did not try to estimate them. We also did not use the earlier years in the tape, where we found unit errors; a longer series would need those checked first. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell any security.
