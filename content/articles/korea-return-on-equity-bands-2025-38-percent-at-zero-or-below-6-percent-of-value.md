Return on equity is net income divided by total equity. It asks how much profit a company made on the owners' money tied up in it. We computed it from each company's fiscal-2025 annual report.

## The spread

| Return on equity | Companies | Share of companies | Share of market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero or below | 971 | 37.9% | 6.1% |
| Above 0 to 5% | 617 | 24.1% | 11.8% |
| Above 5% to 10% | 528 | 20.6% | 18.0% |
| Above 10% to 20% | 327 | 12.8% | 36.3% |
| Above 20% | 119 | 4.6% | 27.8% |
| All | 2,562 | 100% | 100% |

The median company earned **2.5%**. Companies earning above 10% were **17.4%** of the count and **64.1%** of the market value, priced on 12 November 2026.

## Two markets

| Market | Companies | Median | At zero or below |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 815 | 4.27% | 199 (24.4%) |
| KOSDAQ | 1,738 | 1.47% | 767 (44.1%) |

On KOSDAQ, 44.1% of companies sat in the bottom band, against 24.4% on KOSPI.

## At the top

Three companies above 20% had the largest market value: SK hynix at 35.6%, SK Square at 31.5% and Samsung Biologics at 24.0%. The 119 companies above 20% were worth 1,281.6 trillion won together.

## What this does not say

A year-end equity base makes the return jump when equity is small, and one year of earnings is a short record. The 147 companies with no usable equity figure are not in any band, and we do not guess where they would sit.
