On 23 November 2026 the 824 KOSPI companies in our valuation tape were worth 3,718.97 trillion won together. Two of them, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, were worth 1,804.59 trillion won, or **48.5%**. The same two companies earned **37.9%** of the group's 2025 net income and booked **13.2%** of its revenue. A company's weight in the index and its weight in the economy it reports are different measures, and for these two they sit far apart.

## One weight, five measures

We summed each measure across the 824 companies and took the share held by the top two by market value.

| Measure | Samsung Electronics | SK hynix | Both |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market value | 27.90% | 20.62% | 48.52% |
| 2025 net income | 19.45% | 18.48% | 37.93% |
| 2025 operating income | 14.02% | 15.17% | 29.19% |
| Total equity (book) | 14.83% | 4.10% | 18.93% |
| 2025 revenue | 10.20% | 2.97% | 13.17% |

The ordering is the same for both companies: the further a measure is from the share price, the smaller their share. SK hynix is 18.5% of KOSPI net income and 15.2% of operating income, close to its 20.6% of market value. Its book equity is only 4.1% of the total, so its price-to-book ratio of 6.36 is the second highest of the five largest KOSPI companies, behind Samsung Biologics at 8.00. Samsung Electronics has the opposite profile on that last point: 14.8% of equity and a price-to-book ratio of 2.38.

## Beyond the top two

The other 822 companies are 51.5% of the market value, 62.1% of net income and 86.8% of revenue. Taking market value divided by 2025 net income for each group gives 20.5 times for the top two and 13.3 times for the other 822. The whole market sums to 16.0 times. These are group totals, not an average of company ratios, and with the date gap described above they are a rough guide only.

Widening the cut moves the numbers slowly. The top 10 by market value are 61.1% of market value, 51.3% of net income and 20.2% of revenue. The top 20 are 69.6%, 64.7% and 32.9%. Only at 20 companies does the profit share come within five points of the value share.

## The next three

The third to fifth largest add 5.9 points of market value. SK Square (89.7 trillion won, 8.8 trillion won of net income), LG Energy Solution (68.2 trillion won, 0.08 trillion won) and Samsung Biologics (59.6 trillion won, 1.8 trillion won) show how wide the range is: their market value divided by 2025 net income runs from 10 times to 844 times. That range is one reason an index weight says little by itself about current profit.

## What this does not show

The tape holds one price date and one fiscal year, so it cannot say whether the value share or the profit share is the one that will move. It says nothing about why investors price the two companies as they do, and it contains no forecast of 2026 earnings. This is a description of the weights on 23 November, not a judgment on them and not advice to buy or sell any share.
