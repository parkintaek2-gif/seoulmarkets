Korea's disclosure system lets a reader count what companies say about themselves. In the feed we keep, 2,642 material filings from 25 August to 11 November 2026 split **1,678** for KOSDAQ companies and **905** for KOSPI companies, with 35 on KONEX and 24 from other boards. That is 63.5% KOSDAQ and 34.3% KOSPI. If the types of filing were spread evenly, each type would show about that ratio. It does not.

## The split by type

| Type of filing | KOSDAQ | KOSPI | Total in feed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control change | 58 | 0 | 58 |
| CEO change | 28 | 0 | 29 |
| Conversion price reset | 60 | 0 | 63 |
| Rumour or press-report answer | 1 | 40 | 41 |
| Convertible bond issue | 103 | 20 | 128 |
| Delisting-risk matter | 170 | 26 | 201 |
| Paid-in capital increase | 242 | 67 | 326 |
| Supply contract | 383 | 317 | 701 |
| Acquisition of a stake | 58 | 61 | 122 |
| Material management matter | 61 | 66 | 127 |
| Debt guarantee | 47 | 51 | 98 |
| Share cancellation | 30 | 32 | 62 |
| Treasury buyback | 29 | 21 | 51 |

The totals include the few KONEX and other-board filings, so the first two columns do not always add up to the last. Four types are close to even: acquisitions, material management matters, debt guarantees and share cancellations are each within five filings of a 50-50 split. At the other end, one type of filing is almost entirely on the KOSPI. Of 41 answers to rumours or press reports, 40 were filed by KOSPI companies, 34 of them in the unconfirmed form of the title.

## What kind of count this is

The skew is largest where the type depends on the wording of a title. We assigned every filing to a type by a pattern in its report title, so the counts show titles, not events. A KOSPI company that changes its CEO may file under a title our pattern does not catch. The same goes for control changes. The zeros in the first two rows are therefore a limit on what this feed can see, and we do not claim that no KOSPI company changed its CEO in the period. The same warning applies the other way for rumour answers, where we did not check whether KOSDAQ companies answer under a different title.

The control-change row is also mostly corrections. Of its 58 filings, 46 are marked amended, and the filers behind them are 40 companies. A reader who counts "control changes" from the feed as events would overcount.

## Where the skew is not about titles

Other rows are less likely to hinge on a title. Capital increases and convertible bonds are filed under titles that describe the decision itself. There KOSDAQ companies made 242 of 326 paid-in capital increases (74.2%) and 103 of 128 convertible bond issues (80.5%), against 63.5% of all filings. KOSPI companies made 67 and 20 of those. The same feed has 700 KOSDAQ filers and 332 KOSPI filers, a ratio of 2.1 to 1, so the KOSDAQ lead on these two types is larger than its lead in filers. The counts do not say that each KOSDAQ company issues more, only that more of the filings are theirs.

## What this does not say

This is a tally of one feed over 36 filing days. It is not a ranking of the two boards, not a forecast, and not a view on any company. A type's count depends on our selection of titles and on the rules of each board, which we did not read. Nothing in it is a reason to buy or sell any share.
