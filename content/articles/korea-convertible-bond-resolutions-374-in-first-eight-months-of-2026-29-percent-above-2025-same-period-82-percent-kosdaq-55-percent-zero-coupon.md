Korean boards resolved to issue **374 convertible, warrant or exchangeable bonds between 1 January and 8 September 2026**, our tape shows. In the same stretch of 2025 the figure was 291, so 2026 is **28.5% higher**. Only 2021, at 403, had more in the same window since 2020. The tape holds 5,084 filings from 1,296 companies, 98.3% of them private placements.

## The same window, year by year

| 1 Jan to 8 Sep | Filings | Exchangeable bonds | KOSDAQ issuers' share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 287 | 15 | 78.0% |
| 2021 | 403 | 26 | 77.7% |
| 2022 | 208 | 24 | 77.9% |
| 2023 | 297 | 26 | 79.5% |
| 2024 | 346 | 34 | 81.5% |
| 2025 | 291 | 46 | 80.8% |
| 2026 | 374 | 33 | 82.4% |

Using the same window avoids comparing a full year with the part of 2026 that has passed. The KOSDAQ share of filings has stayed near 80% throughout: 308 of the 374 filings this year came from KOSDAQ companies, from 226 companies.

## The exchangeable bond year was 2025

Across all of 2025, exchangeable bonds jumped to 133 of 525 filings (25.3%), up from 49 of 489 in 2024. KOSPI companies filed 47 of the 133, against 13 in 2024, and KOSDAQ companies 86, against 35. In 2026 to date the count is back to 33.

## Zero coupon is the norm

Of all 5,084 filings, **2,787 (54.8%)** carry a zero coupon. By type, 51.8% of convertible bonds (2,240 of 4,324), 56.6% of bonds with warrants (188 of 332) and 83.9% of exchangeable bonds (359 of 428). A zero coupon is not always the whole return: 1,361 of the 2,240 zero-coupon convertibles state a positive maturity rate, which is the yield paid if the bond is redeemed rather than converted. The other 874 state a zero maturity rate. 246 filings give no coupon figure and are not counted as zero.

## Size

For convertible bonds with a stated amount, the median face value was 10 billion won in 2024 (400 filings), 9 billion in 2025 (374) and 10 billion in the 329 filings of 2026. We do not give amount totals, because a few very large exchangeable bonds dominate them and we could not verify those against a second source.

## What this does not show

A board resolution is a plan to issue, and the tape records terms as filed, not what later happened to the bond. Nothing here estimates dilution or judges any company. The tape ends with filings to 8 September 2026, and the issuer's board is taken from today's valuation tape. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell any security.
