The KOSPI closed at 5,006.03 on Tuesday 24 November, after a 6.32% rise. Two sessions later, on 26 November, it closed at **4,711.21**, **5.89%** lower. The median KOSPI share fell by far less: **0.73%**.

## The five sessions

| Date | KOSPI close | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 20 November | 4,869.96 | -5.21% |
| 23 November | 4,708.54 | -3.31% |
| 24 November | 5,006.03 | +6.32% |
| 25 November | 4,697.51 | -6.16% |
| 26 November | 4,711.21 | +0.29% |

The mean absolute daily move over the five sessions is 4.26%. The 26 November close is 3.26% below the 20 November close and 0.29% above the lowest of the five closes, 4,697.51.

## Counting shares, not points

|---|---|---|
| Index change | -5.89% | -1.53% |
| Listings traded on both days | 910 | 1,748 |
| Closed higher | 340 | 632 |
| Closed lower | 556 | 1,072 |
| Median change | -0.73% | -1.06% |
| Fell 5% or more | 123 | 345 |
| Fell 10% or more | 21 | 65 |

On the KOSPI, 340 of 910 shares, 37.4%, closed above their 24 November price. On the KOSDAQ it was 632 of 1,748, 36.2%. The KOSDAQ index fell much less than the KOSPI, but its median share fell more, and a larger part of its shares fell 5% or more: 19.7% against 13.5%.

## Where the value went

KOSPI market value fell from 4,104.8 trillion won to 3,860.3 trillion, a loss of **244.5 trillion**. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, counting ordinary shares only, lost **180.0 trillion** of that, or 73.6%. The other 933 listings together lost 64.5 trillion. That is why the index fell 5.89% while the median share fell 0.73%.

## What this does not show

The 24 November close is a high point after a one-day rebound and we chose it because the 25 November fall began from it; another starting day would give other counts. The files do not say why any share moved. This is not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
