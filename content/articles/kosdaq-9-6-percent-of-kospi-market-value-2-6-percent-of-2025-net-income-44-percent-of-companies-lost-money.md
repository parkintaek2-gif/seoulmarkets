The KOSDAQ's 1,752 companies with a market value were worth 357.85 trillion won on 23 November 2026, **9.6%** of what the KOSPI's 824 were worth (3,718.97 trillion won). Their 2025 revenue was 10.9% of the KOSPI's and their book equity 9.3%. On net income the figure drops to **2.6%**: 6.02 trillion won against 232.42 trillion won.

## Same size on most lines, not on profit

| Measure | KOSDAQ | KOSPI | KOSDAQ as % of KOSPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market value (trillion won) | 357.85 | 3,718.97 | 9.6% |
| 2025 revenue (trillion won) | 356.70 | 3,271.20 | 10.9% |
| Total equity (trillion won) | 274.34 | 2,942.88 | 9.3% |
| 2025 net income (trillion won) | 6.02 | 232.42 | 2.6% |

Net income as a share of revenue was 1.7% for the KOSDAQ group and 7.1% for the KOSPI group. Dividing group market value by group net income gives 59.4 times for the KOSDAQ and 16.0 times for the KOSPI. These are group totals, not an average of company ratios.

## Who lost money

Among the 1,727 KOSDAQ companies with a 2025 net income figure, **764 (44.2%)** reported a loss. On the KOSPI it was 201 of 812, or 24.8%. The loss-makers' share of market value tells a similar story: 32.3% of KOSDAQ's value sits in companies that lost money in 2025, against 3.7% on the KOSPI.

The sums are lopsided. The 963 profitable KOSDAQ companies earned 18.33 trillion won together, and the 764 loss-makers lost 12.31 trillion won, leaving a net 6.02 trillion won. On the KOSPI, profits of 253.84 trillion won were set against losses of 21.42 trillion won.

## The ten biggest KOSDAQ names

The ten largest KOSDAQ companies by market value were worth 61.40 trillion won, 17.2% of the board. Their combined 2025 net income was **minus 0.02 trillion won**, and three of the ten reported a net loss. They include Alteogen (11.74 trillion won, the largest), Ecopro (8.58), Ecopro BM (8.55), Rainbow Robotics (7.80) and Jusung Engineering (6.59). Alteogen's value would place it 48th on the KOSPI list.

Take away those ten and the other 1,742 KOSDAQ companies earned 6.04 trillion won. In other words, the board's net profit did not come from its largest-valued names.

## What this does not show

Market value is a price on one day, set by expectations of years to come. Net income is one year, and it includes one-off items we cannot separate. A KOSDAQ company valued for its future may legitimately show a small profit now, and nothing here says which of these valuations will prove right. This is a comparison of two sets of totals on two dates, and not advice to buy or sell.
