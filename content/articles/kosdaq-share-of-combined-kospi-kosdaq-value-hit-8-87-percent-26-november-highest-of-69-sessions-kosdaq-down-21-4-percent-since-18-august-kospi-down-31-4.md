The KOSDAQ's market value was **8.87%** of the combined value of the KOSPI and KOSDAQ on Thursday 26 November, the highest of the 69 sessions in our index archive, which starts on 18 August. On 18 August it was 7.53%. The lowest was 7.20% on 21 August.

The KOSDAQ's share rose because the KOSPI's value fell by more, not because the KOSDAQ grew: both markets are smaller than on 18 August.

## Trading share

The KOSDAQ traded 5.13 trillion won on 26 November and the KOSPI 12.97 trillion, so the KOSDAQ was **28.35%** of the combined 18.11 trillion. The median session in the archive is 23.41%. Seven sessions were higher; the highest was 35.63% on 2 October and the lowest 14.64% on 24 August. Part of the 26 November reading is that the KOSPI traded its lightest day of the 69.

## How the two moved

Across 68 daily changes the indices moved in the same direction on 50 days and in opposite directions on 18. The KOSDAQ's change was higher than the KOSPI's on 33 of the 68 days, close to an even split. Moves of 3% or more in either direction happened on 24 days for the KOSPI and 19 for the KOSDAQ. Both fell most on 15 October, the KOSPI by 7.57% and the KOSDAQ by 7.65%.

On 24 and 25 November the KOSDAQ moved less than the KOSPI in size: +4.79% against +6.32%, then -2.29% against -6.16%. On 26 November it rose 0.78% against 0.29%.

## What this does not show

The files hold index levels and market and trading values, not who bought or sold. A share can rise from the other market falling, as here. Index composition changes and new listings also move market value. The archive covers 69 sessions, so "highest" means within that period. This is a description of past figures, not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
