Net income is what a company has left after every cost, interest and tax, as reported for the year. We took it from each fiscal-2025 annual report and asked a plain question: how is the profit of the whole list spread out?

## Two columns, not one

Of **2,577** companies, **1,592** reported a profit and **985** a loss. The profits added up to **272.5 trillion won**, the losses to **34.1 trillion**, so the list as a whole earned **238.4 trillion**. Roughly four in ten companies lost money.

## How concentrated the profit is

| The largest profit earners | Share of all profit |
|---|---|
| Top 1 | 16.6% |
| Top 5 | 42.6% |
| Top 10 | 52.3% |
| Top 50 | 76.8% |
| Top 100 | 84.8% |

The running total passes half at the **ninth** company. Samsung Electronics earned 45.2 trillion won and SK hynix 42.9 trillion. Hyundai Motor followed at 10.4 trillion, SK Square at 8.8 trillion and Korea Electric Power at 8.7 trillion. Only 42 companies earned 1 trillion won or more.

## Two markets

| Market | Companies | At a loss | Total net income |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 818 | 202 (24.7%) | 232.7 trillion won |
| KOSDAQ | 1,746 | 774 (44.3%) | 5.8 trillion won |

KOSDAQ has more than twice as many companies as KOSPI and earned about one fortieth as much, in total.

## The other end

The largest losses were SK Innovation at 5.44 trillion won, Lotte Chemical at 2.48 trillion and LG Chem at 0.98 trillion.

## What this does not say

This is one year of one number. Some profits and losses come from a one-time sale or write-off and do not repeat. Where a listed holding company includes a listed subsidiary, the same profit may appear twice on this list, so the top of the table can be overstated. **This is a statistic about a list, not about any one company.** 132 companies have no figure and are left out, not counted as zero.
