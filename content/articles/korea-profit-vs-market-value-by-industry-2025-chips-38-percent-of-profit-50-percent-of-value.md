Korean listed companies earned 238.4 trillion won in net income for fiscal 2025, counting the 2,577 companies for which a figure is available. One industry group earned more than a third of it. Electronic Components, Computers and Communication Equipment, the group that contains the memory chipmakers, has 292 companies and 91.8 trillion won of net income, or 38.5% of the total.

Its share of market value is larger still. On 13 November the same 292 companies were worth 2,293 trillion won, which is 50.2% of the 4,568 trillion won for all 2,577. Half of the listed market's value sits in one group of about one company in nine.

## Profit share and value share, side by side

| Industry group (as labelled in the data) | Companies | Net income, trillion won | Share of profit | Market value, trillion won | Share of value | At a loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electronic Components, Computers and Communication Equipment | 292 | 91.8 | 38.5% | 2,293 | 50.2% | 122 |
| Financial Services | 115 | 54.7 | 22.9% | 485 | 10.6% | 19 |
| Motor Vehicles and Trailers | 109 | 23.6 | 9.9% | 141 | 3.1% | 30 |
| Medicinal Substances and Pharmaceuticals | 180 | 3.4 | 1.4% | 190 | 4.2% | 84 |

The gap between the two shares differs by group. The electronics group's value share is 12 points above its profit share. Financial services is the other way round: 22.9% of profit and 10.6% of value, a 12-point gap in the opposite direction. Motor vehicles earned 9.9% of profit and carry 3.1% of value.

Pharmaceuticals is the reverse of motor vehicles. Its 180 companies earned 3.4 trillion won in total, 1.4% of profit, yet are worth 190 trillion, 4.2% of value, and 84 of the 180 lost money in 2025. A market value is a price for years ahead, and a profit figure is one year behind us. The table shows that the two do not line up by industry. It does not say which one is right.

## Where the losses are

Across the whole list, 985 companies lost money in 2025. Of the 292 in the electronics group, 122 did, so even the largest earner is also home to many loss-makers. The group's total is carried by a few very large names, not by the median company in it.

At the bottom, Coke and Refined Petroleum Products has five companies and a combined net loss of 5.14 trillion won. Research and Development has 65 companies and a combined loss of 0.96 trillion, with 58 of the 65 at a loss.

## What this does not say

This is one year of annual reports against one day of prices. A company's group label comes from the data, not from us. Some listed holding companies and their listed subsidiaries may both be counted. And a share of profit is a description of 2025, not a forecast of any company or group. It is a statistic about a list, not about any one company, and it is not investment advice.
