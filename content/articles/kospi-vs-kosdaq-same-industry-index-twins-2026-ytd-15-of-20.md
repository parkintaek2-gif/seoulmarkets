KOSPI is up **26.78%** in 2026 so far. KOSDAQ is down **24.74%**. A natural question is whether that is the same industries doing different things, or different industries.

Twenty industries have an index in both markets under the same name, so we lined them up.

## The twins

| Industry | KOSPI YTD | KOSDAQ YTD | Gap (points) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electrical and Electronics | +67.45% | -3.14% | 70.59 |
| Medical and Precision Instruments | +21.10% | -36.76% | 57.86 |
| General Services | +5.37% | -47.71% | 53.08 |
| Manufacturing | +34.92% | -17.90% | 52.82 |
| Construction | +23.56% | -20.16% | 43.72 |
| Pharmaceuticals | -6.07% | -45.24% | 39.17 |
| Machinery and Equipment | -23.00% | +5.83% | -28.83 |
| Non-Metallic Minerals | -19.42% | +9.35% | -28.77 |

The first six rows are the largest KOSPI leads; the last two are the only cases where KOSDAQ led by more than 5 points.

## The pattern

KOSPI did better in **15 of 20** pairs, with a median lead of **23.9 points**. Eight pairs were down in both markets, nine were up on KOSPI and down on KOSDAQ, and only one was up in both. Two ran the other way.

Across all index families, 28 of 53 KOSPI-series indices and 4 of 39 KOSDAQ-series indices are up. The one KOSDAQ index with a large gain is KOSDAQ 150 Information Technology, +22.74%.

The split is wide inside the same industry, so industry alone does not explain it.
