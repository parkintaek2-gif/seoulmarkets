Korean brokerages publish research every business day. We counted when.

The list holds **1,087 reports** with a publication date between 10 August and 2 October 2026, spread over **37 publishing days**.

## By weekday

| Weekday | Days | Reports | Average per day | Median per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 7 | 252 | 36.0 | 36 |
| Tuesday | 8 | 243 | 30.4 | 29 |
| Wednesday | 8 | 193 | 24.1 | 24 |
| Thursday | 7 | 224 | 32.0 | 28 |
| Friday | 7 | 175 | 25.0 | 25 |

A Monday carried about **1.5 times** as many reports as a Wednesday: 36.0 against 24.1.

## The extremes

The busiest single day was Monday 21 September, with 52 reports. The quietest was Friday 21 August, with 5.

Wednesday and Friday were the two lowest weekdays on both average and median. Monday was the highest on both.

## What this does not say

The list gives a date and nothing about when the work was done, so we offer no reason for the gap. The seven-or-eight-day samples per weekday are small, and one busy day can lift a weekday's average.

*This is statistics about a calendar, not a signal about any company.*
