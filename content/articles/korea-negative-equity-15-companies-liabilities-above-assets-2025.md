Total equity is what is left on the balance sheet when all liabilities are subtracted from all assets. When it is zero or below, the company owes more than it owns on paper. We read total equity from each company's fiscal-2025 annual report and counted the cases at or below zero.

## The count

**15 of 2,577** companies with an equity figure ended 2025 at zero or below: **0.6%**. None was exactly at zero.

| Market | Companies with equity figure | Equity at or below zero | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSDAQ | 1,746 | 8 | 0.5% |
| KOSPI | 818 | 3 | 0.4% |
| KONEX | 13 | 4 | not computed (13 measured) |
| All | 2,577 | 15 | 0.6% |

KONEX has only 13 companies with a figure, so we show the count and leave the share out.

| Company | Market cap (12 Nov 2026) | Total equity | Net income 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|

| Sambu Construction | 227.8 billion | -142.1 billion | -76.7 billion |
| Union Korea Pharm | 192.4 billion | -22.9 billion | -30.8 billion |
| Eone Diagnomics Genome Center | 167.1 billion | -22.5 billion | +28.2 billion |
| Dawonsys | 96.6 billion | -515.6 billion | -196.9 billion |
| EOFlow | 75.2 billion | -144.0 billion | -42.4 billion |

Twelve of the 15 have a market value in the price file. Together they were worth **1.40 trillion won**, which is **0.03%** of the 4,643.1 trillion won in the whole market. The largest of them, STX Corporation, was worth less than one-two-thousandth of Samsung Electronics.

## Loss and deficit

**Fourteen** of the 15 reported a net loss for 2025. One, Eone Diagnomics Genome Center, reported a net profit of 28.2 billion won and still had negative equity. A profit in one year and a deficit on the balance sheet can sit together when earlier losses were larger.

## Where they are

The 15 are spread across 11 industry names. General Construction has two of its 38 measured companies in the group, Research and Development two of 65, Medicinal Substances and Pharmaceuticals two of 180, and Medical, Precision, and Optical Instruments two of 100. The rest have one each. Because every count is two or less, we do not rank industries here.

Thirteen of the 15 are measured on a consolidated basis and two on a separate basis.

## What this does not say

A balance sheet at one date does not tell what happens next. We do not know whether any of the 15 raised capital, converted debt or sold assets after year-end, because the tape holds only the annual report. We also did not apply the legal impairment test, which compares equity with registered capital and would give a different, larger list.
