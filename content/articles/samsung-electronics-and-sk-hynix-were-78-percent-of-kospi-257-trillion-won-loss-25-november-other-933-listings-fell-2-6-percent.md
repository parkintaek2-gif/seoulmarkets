On 25 November the KOSPI's listings lost **257.2 trillion won** of market value, 6.26% of the total. Two of them, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, lost **199.8 trillion won** between them, or **77.7%** of the net fall. The other 933 listings together lost 2.63%.

## The size of the two

| 25 November | Change | Market value lost | Market value after (trillion won) | Share of KOSPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Samsung Electronics | -9.87% | 109.3 trillion | 998.0 | 25.9% |
| SK hynix | -11.05% | 90.5 trillion | 728.0 | 18.9% |
| Both | | 199.8 trillion | 1,726.0 | 44.9% |
| All other KOSPI listings | -2.63% | 57.4 trillion | 2,121.7 | 55.1% |

The shares of value in the table are those at the close. The loss of 77.7% is far above the 44.9% weight, because both fell by about 10% against 2.63% for the rest. See [KOSPI weights](/data/kospi-weights) for the current ranking.

The largest market-value gains were Samsung Biologics (plus 5.66%, plus 3.4 trillion won) and Celltrion (plus 3.83%, plus 1.9 trillion won).

## Trading did not follow the weight

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix made up 43.0% of KOSPI trading value on 25 November. That is below the median of 46.3% across the 61 sessions of our stock archive, and 48 sessions were higher. The loss was concentrated far more than the trading.

## The same two since 23 September

The KOSPI's market value fell from 5,852 trillion won on 23 September, the index high, to 3,848 trillion won on 25 November, a loss of 2,005 trillion won. Samsung Electronics fell from 285,500 won to 170,700 won (minus 40.2%), and SK hynix from 1,862,000 won to 1,030,000 won (minus 44.7%). Together they lost 1,303 trillion won, which is **65.0%** of the whole. The other 933 listings, taken together, fell 24.8%, so the rest of the market has also fallen a long way.

## What the indices say

On the same day the KOSPI 200 Information Technology index fell 9.38% and the Electrical and Electronics sector index 9.81%. The KRX single-company indices for the two companies fell 9.87% and 11.05%, matching the shares. The KOSPI 200 excluding mega caps fell 2.43%, close to our figure of 2.63% for the other listings.

## What this does not show

Market value is price times listed shares and we have no view of who sold. These are counts of one day and one window, not an explanation of why the prices moved, and nothing here is a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share. The window starts on 27 August, so we cannot say how common a two-company concentration like this has been before.
