Market value is what the market priced a company at on one day. We sorted the Korean listed companies into size bands, so the shape of the whole list shows and not only its largest names.

## The middle company

Of **2,683** companies with a market value, the median was worth **86 billion won**. Together they were worth **4,643.1 trillion won**. The middle company is small: one in five (542) was worth under 30 billion won, and more than half (1,449) under 100 billion.

## The bands

| Market value, 12 Nov 2026 | Companies | Share of companies | Share of summed value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 30 billion won | 542 | 20.2% | 0.21% |
| 30 to under 100 billion | 907 | 33.8% | 1.14% |
| 100 to under 300 billion | 618 | 23.0% | 2.33% |
| 300 billion to under 1 trillion | 327 | 12.2% | 3.68% |
| 1 to under 10 trillion | 233 | 8.7% | 15.18% |
| 10 trillion and above | 56 | 2.1% | 77.46% |

The 1,449 companies under 100 billion won make up 54% of the list and **1.35%** of its value. The 56 companies at 10 trillion won or more make up 2.1% of the list and **77.5%** of its value.

## Two markets, two middles

| Market | Companies | Median market value | Total market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| KOSPI | 825 | 235 billion won | 4,249.0 trillion won |
| KOSDAQ | 1,754 | 66 billion won | 390.7 trillion won |

KOSDAQ lists more than twice as many companies as KOSPI and is worth under a tenth as much. KONEX has 104 companies with a market value and is not shown here.

## The top of the list

Samsung Electronics was worth 1,216.0 trillion won and SK hynix 916.0 trillion. The third, SK Square, was worth 104.4 trillion, then LG Energy Solution at 77.1 trillion and Samsung Biologics at 67.1 trillion. The gap between second and third is nearly nine to one.

## What this does not say

These are one day's prices. A band is a place on a list, not a verdict on a company: a firm worth 20 billion won can be profitable and a firm worth 20 trillion can lose money. **This is a statistic about a list, not about any one company.** 26 companies have no market value in the data and are left out, not counted as zero.
