Operating margin is the share of sales a company keeps after paying the costs of running its business, before interest and tax. We took revenue and operating income from each fiscal-2025 annual report and sorted the listed non-financial companies by margin.

## The middle company

Of **2,349** companies, the median margin was **2.7%**. A company at the 25th percentile lost 4.2 won on every 100 won of sales; one at the 75th kept 7.7. Added together, the group reported **3,175.6 trillion won** of revenue and **237.6 trillion** of operating profit, a margin of **7.5%**. The total is higher than the middle because the biggest earners are very big.

## The bands

| Operating margin, 2025 | Companies | Share of companies | Share of summed market value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loss | 800 | 34.1% | 4.5% |
| 0 to under 5% | 712 | 30.3% | 9.6% |
| 5 to under 10% | 413 | 17.6% | 17.9% |
| 10 to under 20% | 298 | 12.7% | 38.1% |
| 20% and above | 126 | 5.4% | 29.8% |

One company in three had an operating loss. They hold 4.5% of the market value of the group. The 424 companies at 10% or more are 18% of the list and hold 68% of its value.

## The top, and the other end

Among companies with revenue above 100 billion won, the highest margins were SHIFT UP at 61.6%, HPSP at 52.0%, CLASSYS at 50.7%, Alteogen at 49.5% and SK hynix at 48.6%. SK hynix is the only one of the five with revenue near 100 trillion won; the other four sold between 173 and 337 billion.

Size does not protect: of 326 companies with revenue of 1 trillion won or more, 20 had an operating loss. At the other end, 101 companies lost an amount at or above their whole revenue.

## What this does not say

This is one year of one line of the income statement. A margin is a place on a list, not a verdict on a company: a loss can be the cost of building something and a high margin can be a good year. **This is a statistic about a list, not about any one company.** 240 non-financial companies have no usable figure and are left out, not counted as zero.
