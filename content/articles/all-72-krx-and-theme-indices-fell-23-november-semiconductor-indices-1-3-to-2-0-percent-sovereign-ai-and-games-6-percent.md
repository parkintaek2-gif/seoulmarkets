Our index tape holds 171 Korea Exchange index records, including industry baskets, themes, dividend lists and two that follow a single company. We looked at the 72 of them on the KRX Series and Theme lists that are measured prices of a basket of shares. On Monday 23 November 2026, when the KOSPI fell 3.31%, **all 72 fell**. The median was -3.22%, and 49 of the 72 fell 3% or more.

KRX Shipbuilding Top 10 (-4.58%) and KRX Internet Top 10 (-4.56%) followed. The 10-company baskets move on a few shares, so their moves are best read as a count, not as a verdict on a theme.

## The smallest falls

Ten of the 72 fell less than 2%. Semiconductors were the most visible group: the four semiconductor indices fell between 1.26% and 2.02%, and three of them are among the ten.

- KRX K-AI Semiconductor Top 2+ Index: -1.26%
- KRX AI Semiconductor Index: -1.30%
- KRX Semiconductors: -1.57%
- KRX Semiconductors Top 15: -2.02%

The two consumer staples baskets were the smallest falls of all: KRX 300 Consumer Staples fell 0.69% and KRX Consumer Staples 0.74%. KOSDAQ Top 10 (-1.92%), KOSDAQ Top 15 (-1.70%), KRX Securities (-1.77%), KOSDAQ 150 Governance (-1.57%) and KRX K-AI Biotech KOSDAQ (-1.94%) completed the ten.

## Two single-company indices

Two KRX indices follow one company each. The KRX Samsung Electronics Index fell 3.06% and the KRX SK hynix Index 2.52%, against the KOSPI's 3.31%. Both fell less than the KOSPI, but more than any of the four semiconductor baskets.

## Dividend, governance and ESG baskets

The five dividend lists fell between 3.05% and 4.42%, a median of 3.60%. The KOSPI 200 Financials High Dividend Top 10 Index was the worst of them at -4.42%. Nine ESG, climate and governance baskets ranged from -1.57% (KOSDAQ 150 Governance) to -4.45% (KRX Eco Leaders 100), with a median of -3.21%. Those lists did not behave as a defensive group on the day: their median was almost the same as the whole set's.

## The broad indices for comparison

KRX 300 fell 3.16%, KRX 100 3.32%, the broad KRX TMI of 2,376 companies 3.11% and KTOP 30 3.60%. The widest basket, the TMI, traded 19.42 trillion won, the highest of the 72.

## What this does not show

One session cannot say whether the pattern repeats. The indices overlap, since the same companies sit in several lists, so we do not add them up or count them as independent observations. The exchange records give prices and counts, not reasons. This is a description of one day, and not advice to buy or sell any index or share.
