On Thursday 26 November the KOSPI traded **12.97 trillion won** of shares. In the 69 sessions of our index archive, which starts on 18 August, that is the **lightest day**. The next lightest were 26 October at 12.99 trillion and 19 November at 13.42 trillion.

## Against a typical session

| KOSPI trading value (trillion won) | |
|---|---|
| 26 November | 12.97 |
| 25 November | 18.29 |
| Median of 69 sessions | 20.48 |
| Highest of 69 sessions | 29.91 |

26 November is 29.1% below the day before and 63.3% of the median session. It came after a 6.16% fall on 25 November, which was itself the heaviest of the week at 18.29 trillion.

## KOSPI against KOSDAQ

The KOSDAQ traded 5.13 trillion won, 5.9% less than on 25 November. On the KOSDAQ it was the 18th lightest of 69 sessions, so the thin day was a KOSPI feature more than a KOSDAQ one.

## Two companies, nearly half

SK hynix traded 3.77 trillion won and Samsung Electronics 2.42 trillion. Together they were **47.7%** of KOSPI trading, against 43.0% the day before. Their trading fell 21.3%, from 7.87 to 6.19 trillion. The other 933 listings traded 6.78 trillion won, down from 10.43 trillion, a fall of **35.0%**. Most of the drop in KOSPI trading therefore came from outside the two largest shares.

## Turnover

Trading value was 0.34% of KOSPI market value on 26 November and 0.48% on 25 November. That is a ratio of two sums; it is not the same as the share turnover of any one company.

## What this does not show

The files record how much traded, not who traded or why. A light day can follow a heavy one for reasons we cannot see here, and one session says nothing about the next. This is not a forecast or advice to buy or sell any share.
