Ten companies are worth 56% of the Korean listed market. How much of everything else do they hold? We took the ten largest by market value on 13 November 2026 and measured their share of five other totals, using fiscal 2025 annual reports.

## One group of ten, six shares

| Measure, the ten's share of the list | Share |
|---|---|
| Market value (2,470 of 4,433 trillion won) | 55.7% |
| Net income (119.3 of 238.4 trillion) | 50.0% |
| Operating income (127.7 of 325.4 trillion) | 39.2% |
| Total equity (902 of 3,219 trillion) | 28.0% |
| Total assets (2,406 of 11,115 trillion) | 21.6% |
| Revenue (662 of 3,628 trillion) | 18.2% |

The shares fall the further the measure is from the price. The price, which is a bet on the future, is concentrated most. Profit comes next at 50.0% for net income and 39.2% for operating income. The accounting stock of what a company owns and owes is lower, at 28.0% for equity and 21.6% for assets. Sales, the measure of what a company does, are lowest at 18.2%.

## Who the ten are

Samsung Electronics (1,151 trillion won) and SK hynix (818) are 1,969 trillion together, 44% of the whole list's value. The other eight are SK Square, LG Energy Solution, Samsung Biologics, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, KB Financial Group, Hyundai Motor, Samsung Life and Celltrion, worth 50 to 91 trillion each.

Their profit is not alike. Samsung Electronics earned 45.2 trillion won and SK hynix 42.9 trillion, while LG Energy Solution earned 0.08 trillion. Nine of the ten are above 0.7 trillion.

## What the gap does and does not tell us

The gap between 56% of value and 18% of sales is a description of how the market prices this list at one date. It does not say the ten are over- or under-priced. Some of the ten are holding or financial companies whose sales and assets are not comparable with a manufacturer's. And a share of profit in one year can change by the next.

## What this does not say

Net and operating income are totals net of losses. Holding companies in the ten may count profit that another company in the ten also reports, so the ten's profit share may read high. Market value is one trading day. This is a statistic about a list, not about any one company, and it is not investment advice.
