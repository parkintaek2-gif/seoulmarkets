A company that borrows has to earn its interest before it earns anything else. We took every Korean listed company that filed both an operating income figure and a finance cost figure for fiscal 2025 — 1,293 of 2,709 — and divided one by the other.

The median company cleared its interest bill 1.10 times.

## Three groups

| Coverage | Companies | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Below 1 times | 627 | 48% |
| 1 to 5 times | 356 | 28% |
| 5 times and above | 310 | 24% |

Nearly half did not cover interest out of operating income. That group is not all loss-makers: 446 of the 627 had an operating loss, and the other **181 were profitable at the operating line and still fell short**. For those 181 the shortfall is a financing one rather than a trading one, and no single year says which way it moves next.

## Smaller companies carry the weight

| Market value | Companies | Below 1 times | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 30 billion won | 253 | 190 | 75% |
| 30 to 100 billion | 425 | 219 | 52% |
| 100 to 300 billion | 303 | 122 | 40% |
| 300 billion to 1 trillion | 157 | 50 | 32% |
| 1 to 10 trillion | 108 | 29 | 27% |
| 10 trillion and above | 36 | 7 | 19% |

Three of every four of the smallest borrowers are below 1 times; fewer than one in five of the largest are. The gradient runs the same direction as the net loss rate we counted by the same bands, which is what you would expect when the same companies appear in both counts — this is one list sorted two ways, not two independent findings.

## By industry

Among groups with 40 or more companies in the measured set:

| Industry | Companies | Below 1 times | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Other machinery and equipment | 99 | 56 | 57% |
| Medicinal substances and pharmaceuticals | 101 | 55 | 54% |
| Wholesale and commission trade | 65 | 35 | 54% |
| Electronic components, computers, communication equipment | 156 | 83 | 53% |
| Medical, precision and optical instruments | 49 | 25 | 51% |
| Publishing | 102 | 48 | 47% |
| Motor vehicles and trailers | 44 | 17 | 39% |
| Food products manufacturing | 44 | 15 | 34% |
| Financial services | 47 | 11 | 23% |

The spread between the top and bottom of this table is 34 percentage points. Pharmaceuticals and precision instruments sit high partly because research spending lands in operating income while the borrowing that funds it lands in finance costs; the ratio reads that as weakness in both halves at once. Financial services sit lowest, and borrowing is their raw material rather than a strain on it.

## What this does not measure

More than half the market is absent. 1,358 companies file no finance cost line at all, and we do not read that silence as "no interest" — a company with no borrowings and a company that reports the charge elsewhere look identical here, and we cannot tell them apart from the filing. A further 184 report no operating income figure. Those 1,416 companies are not measured, not zero.

Finance costs as filed can include foreign exchange losses and other charges, so a company near the line may clear it on a narrower definition of interest. Read 1.10 times as the stricter number.
